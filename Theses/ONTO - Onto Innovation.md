# Onto Innovation

## Summary

The vault flagged ONTO as the cheapest, highest-leverage uncovered name in the CoWoS→CoPoS panel-packaging transition — the only process-control vendor that also patterns the panel. The market got there first: ONTO is +174% over twelve months to ~$336 (~$16.6B cap), re-rated from the ~24x "value or value-trap" laggard of the metrology trio (per the vault's own late-May sector note) to ~46x forward / ~65x trailing, with the sell-side at "Strong Buy" but an average target only ~5% above spot. The investable question is therefore no longer *"is ONTO cheap"* — that entry is gone — but *"does a genuine CoPoS call-option plus 50%+ advanced-packaging growth justify a compounder multiple on the sub-scale, most-contestable member of a field that KLA is actively invading?"* The non-consensus answer: the panel-lithography franchise (JetStep, qualified at two AI-packaging suppliers, ramping 2027) is a real asymmetric option round-wafer steppers physically cannot replicate, and ONTO's path-agnostic breadth (inspection + metrology + litho + software, earning on CoWoS, CoPoS *and* CoWoP) is undervalued by the "spread-thin sub-scale" framing — but that same breadth caps its margin and scale in every niche, KLA takes the high-resolution HBM4/hybrid-bond tier where the profit concentrates, and US manufacturing structurally cedes the fastest-localizing China AP market to Camtek. Medium conviction: own the structural exposure the vault wants, but at 46x the margin of safety is in the optionality, not the price.

## Key Non-consensus Insights

**1. The vault's own "cheap value play" thesis is already dead — and that repricing is the most important fact about ONTO today.** The [[Sectors/Advanced Semi Metrology]] sector note (2026-05-27) frames ONTO as "the cheapest of the four (~24x P/E): either a value opportunity or a correct read on structural scale disadvantage." Four weeks later the market has resolved that question by re-rating ONTO *up*: ~46x forward (on ~$7.22 2026E non-GAAP EPS), ~65x trailing, +174% in a year, with the Street's average price target now only ~5% above spot. The "value" leg of the case has been arbitraged away in real time on the CoPoS/panel-packaging narrative. This matters because every downstream judgment inverts: a sub-scale challenger trading at a *discount* is a margin-of-safety setup where the option is free; the same challenger trading at a *premium-compounder* multiple is a setup where you are paying KLA-adjacent multiples for a business with none of KLA's monopoly economics, defect-library moat, or ~$4B service annuity. The thesis is no longer "buy the cheap one" — it is "is the panel-litho optionality worth a full multiple re-rate," and the honest answer is *partially*.

**2. JetStep panel lithography is an asymmetric call-option the metrology-trio framing structurally cannot see.** The standard frame (Onto vs Nova vs Camtek vs KLA) is an inspection-and-metrology comparison — and on that axis ONTO is sub-scale. But ONTO is the *only* one of the four with a panel-lithography franchise, and panel litho is the one CoPoS layer where incumbent round-wafer steppers (ASML/Canon/Nikon wafer tools) *physically do not apply* — die-shift, CTE mismatch and panel warpage on a 600×600mm+ rectangular panel require a purpose-built tool. JetStep (S3500 / X500, exposing CCL, FR4, composite *and glass* up to 720×600mm) is exactly that tool, and management disclosed it is **already qualified at two packaging suppliers to AI device manufacturers with a 2027 ramp**. The asymmetry: ONTO sizes the whole panel-level franchise (JetStep + Firefly) at only ~$200M against a >$1.3B 2026 base — small enough that the market discounts it, but with "meaningful upside if industry adoption accelerates." If TSMC's CoPoS pilot (VisEra ~June 2026, AP7 HVM 2028-29) ramps as the companion macro note now argues, panel litho is a multi-hundred-million franchise where ONTO has a first-mover qualification lead and incumbents are still adapting wafer tools. You are not paying much for this option inside 46x — that is the part of the re-rate that *is* justified.

**3. ONTO's advanced-packaging growth is real but structurally margin-capped — "high-volume, margin-capped growth," not monopoly economics — because the AP collision resolves on resolution, not price.** AP is genuinely inflecting: >50% growth guided for 2026, 3Di winning 10+ orders in Q1 on sub-6μm bump adoption (vs 15-25μm historically), Dragonfly G5 (150nm defect sensitivity) with a 15+ application pipeline across 10+ customers, a >$240M HBM volume-purchase agreement through 2027. But the binding structural fact (per [[Sectors/Advanced Semi Metrology]] and [[Theses/KLA - KLA Corporation]]) is that a missed defect at HBM4 16-Hi / hybrid-bonding scraps a multi-thousand-dollar stacked package, so the *highest-resolution* inspector wins the *highest-value* work regardless of price — and at the sub-0.5nm copper-recess tier that is KLA, whose AP revenue grew 70% to $950M precisely by invading this zone. ONTO's durable territory is the cost-sensitive mid-range plus the litho/films-metrology adjacencies KLA doesn't contest (Iris films metrology, 3Di, JetStep). That territory grows in *volume* with every HBM die-stack, but not in *pricing power*. The market is extrapolating AP unit growth into a compounder multiple; the correct read is high-growth, structurally-lower-incremental-margin work beneath a competitor who takes the cream.

**4. The "Onto + Camtek = ~65% of AP inspection" headline hides a geographic asymmetry that caps ONTO's real AP TAM.** The duopoly framing treats ONTO and Camtek as interchangeable co-leaders. They are not: ONTO manufactures in the US and faces tightening export-control friction selling advanced-packaging tools into mainland China, while Camtek (Israel) ships into China — the single fastest-localizing, highest-volume AP market — with far less constraint. China is where mature-node and OSAT AP capacity is being built out most aggressively and where domestic substitution is policy-driven. The consequence: a structurally meaningful slice of the AP-inspection TAM is effectively *Camtek's and the domestic entrants'* to win, not ONTO's, and ONTO's addressable AP market is smaller than the global share figure implies. The sell-side models "AP duopoly, both win"; the geography says ONTO's AP runway is narrower and more US/Korea/Taiwan-concentrated than Camtek's — a quiet structural disadvantage that widens if export controls tighten further.

**5. The diversification the trio-framing penalizes as "spread-thin R&D" is simultaneously ONTO's best hedge and the reason it stays sub-scale — bull and bear are the same fact.** ONTO spans inspection (Dragonfly) + metrology (Atlas, Iris) + lithography (JetStep) + analytics (Discover), front-end GAA *and* back-end packaging. The pure-play critique is that this spreads a ~$1.3B revenue base and a sub-KLA R&D budget across four fronts, leaving it second-best in each. The non-consensus inversion: that same breadth makes ONTO genuinely *path-agnostic* — it earns whether the winning format is CoWoS, CoPoS, or CoWoP, whether the substrate is organic or glass, and whether intensity grows fastest in GAA logic metrology (Atlas G6, 2nd logic GAA customer won, ~25% advanced-nodes growth) or HBM/AP. In a transition whose format and material winner is still unresolved (per the companion CoPoS macro note), optionality across all paths is worth more than depth in one — but it is also precisely why ONTO will never have KLA's defect-library moat or Camtek's China-AP volume. The investor who resolves this correctly sizes ONTO as a *transition-optionality* position, not a quality-compounder.

## Outstanding Questions

**1. Did ONTO actually fail TSMC's CoWoS-L qualification at the tighter bump pitch, as the most credible bear alleges?** The sharpest short take (irrationalanalysis "Onto Innovation Implosion") claims ONTO "failed TSMC qualification for CoWoS-L" and that KLA's repurposed process-control tools "crush Onto" in AP inspection. If true at the *leading-edge* 2.5D node, ONTO's AP growth is concentrated in the trailing tier and far more contestable than the >50% headline suggests. If false — and Q1 disclosed Dragonfly G5 qualification "at a leading 2.5D logic customer" — the bear is overstated. *Resolution: TSMC CoWoS/CoPoS tool-of-record disclosures, ONTO's named-customer AP qual scorecard through 2026, SEMICON West (July 2026) competitive intelligence.*

**2. What is the panel-lithography TAM at CoPoS HVM, and what share does ONTO actually hold once ASML/Canon/Nikon/SCREEN field panel tools?** ONTO's ~$200M panel-level expectation is a 2026-2027 qual/pilot number. If CoPoS reaches AP7 HVM in 2028-29, the panel-litho TAM could be multiples larger — but ASML, Canon, Nikon and SCREEN are all developing PLP-capable systems, and ONTO's first-mover lead is not a monopoly. *Resolution: TSMC CoPoS panel-litho tool selections at AP7/VisEra, ONTO panel-level backlog trajectory, competitor panel-tool announcements.*

**3. Is the Q1 GAAP gross-margin compression (50.1% vs 53.7% a year ago) a mix/amortization artifact or an early pricing-power signal?** Non-GAAP GM held/expanded (55.7%) and Q2 is guided to 56-56.5%, but the GAAP delta plus management's flag of "increasing headwinds around certain material costs, fuel charges" warrants watching as KLA and Camtek compete in overlapping AP categories. *Resolution: GAAP-to-non-GAAP bridge in the 10-Q, GM trajectory through 2026, any ASP commentary.*

**4. How much of 2026's >30% growth is a structural intensity step versus a cyclical AP-capex peak?** ONTO printed a near-flat 2025 ($1.005B, +1.8%) before a +30% 2026 guide — a sharp inflection that maps to the AI-packaging capex wave. At 46x forward, the multiple assumes the step is structural and durable. If 2026 is an AP-capex peak that digests in 2027 (as HBM/CoWoS allocation normalizes), both estimates and multiple compress together. *Resolution: 2027 order trajectory, TSMC/SK Hynix AP capex guidance, backlog composition.*

**5. Can Discover software ever become a KLA-Klarity-style annuity, or is software/services permanently a sub-scale ~20% line?** KLA's re-rate to compounder status rests on a ~$4B recurring service+software annuity with data lock-in. ONTO's software & services is ~18.5-23% of revenue and lacks the cross-customer defect corpus. Whether Discover can build genuine switching-cost stickiness is the swing factor between "ONTO is a hardware-cyclical at 46x" and "ONTO is an emerging compounder." *Resolution: software attach-rate and recurring-mix disclosures, Discover installed-base growth.*

**6. Is the >$240M HBM volume-purchase agreement through 2027 incremental demand or a pull-forward?** A multi-year VPA is a backlog-visibility positive, but if it front-loads HBM metrology demand it flatters 2026-27 at the expense of a 2028 air-pocket. *Resolution: backlog disclosures, whether further VPAs are signed, HBM4→HBM5 transition timing.*

**7. How fast does China export-control tightening erode ONTO's accessible AP market versus Camtek's?** If US controls extend to more AP/mature-node tooling, ONTO loses TAM that Camtek and domestic entrants capture. *Resolution: BIS rule changes, ONTO China revenue mix trajectory, Camtek China share.*

## Business Model & Product Description

Onto Innovation sells the inspection, metrology, lithography and yield-analytics tools that semiconductor manufacturers use to find defects, measure features, pattern panels and manage yield — concentrated in the two fastest-growing pockets of process control: **advanced packaging** (2.5D/3D, HBM, CoWoS/CoPoS) and **advanced nodes** (gate-all-around logic, advanced DRAM). The mental model: ONTO is a *yield auditor like KLA, but the only one that also owns a printing press for the panel*. Where KLA is the full-spectrum front-end inspection monopoly and Nova/Camtek are pure metrology / AP-inspection specialists, ONTO is the diversified mid-scale generalist — broadest portfolio, sub-scale in each lane, and uniquely holding a lithography franchise that gives it a foothold in AP *patterning*, not just measurement. Formed from the 2019 merger of Rudolph Technologies (inspection + AP litho) and Nanometrics (OCD/film metrology), the breadth is structural DNA.

| Segment | FY2025 | % of rev | 2026E growth | What it is |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Specialty Devices & Advanced Packaging | ~$500M+ | ~50% | AP sub-line **>50%**; specialty soft (~$25M/qtr) | Dragonfly inspection, Iris films metrology, 3Di 3D metrology, JetStep panel litho — the AI/HBM/CoWoS engine; specialty (power/RF/CIS/mature) is the cyclical drag, now a minor tail |
| Advanced Nodes | ~$300M (2× the $148.5M of FY2024) | ~30% | **~25%** | Atlas G6 OCD/film metrology for GAA logic + HBM4 / advanced DRAM; 2nd logic GAA customer won; new TSV-metrology application |

**Key products:**
- **Dragonfly G5** — macro/AP inspection, 150nm defect sensitivity, extensible to next-gen nodes; shipments began Q1 2026 ahead of schedule; qualified at a leading 2.5D logic customer and a leading HBM maker for HBM4; 15+ application pipeline across 10+ customers. The AP inspection workhorse.
- **Atlas G6** — OCD/thin-film metrology for gate-all-around logic and HBM4 DRAM; selected by a second logic GAA customer; new TSV-metrology win (2H 2026 shipments). The advanced-nodes growth driver.
- **Iris** — films metrology; a stated driver of the AP growth alongside Dragonfly.
- **3Di** — 3D metrology for advanced packaging; 10+ incremental orders in Q1 on sub-6μm microbump adoption (the smaller the bump, the higher the metrology intensity).
- **JetStep S3500 / X500** — panel-level packaging lithography; 59.4×59.4mm exposure field, 2/2 L/S resolution (option to 1/1), substrates to 720×600mm including CCL/FR4/composite/**glass**; corrects die-shift, CTE mismatch, panel warpage. The CoPoS optionality; qualified at two AI-packaging suppliers, 2027 ramp. JetStep + Firefly panel franchise sized ~$200M with upside on CoPoS adoption.
- **Discover** — analytics/yield software; ONTO's sub-scale answer to KLA's Klarity/RAPID.

**Customers** (mid-2024 concentration): TSMC ~23%, Samsung ~20%, SK Hynix ~13% (~56% top-three) — a leading-edge logic + HBM/DRAM customer base, plus mature/mid-node logic, memory, power and OSATs (a slice of which is China-constrained for AP tools).

**Balance sheet:** net cash ~$850M ($852M cash + short-term investments end-FY2024, $0 long-term debt), FY2024 FCF $213.8M, ~150% cash conversion of non-GAAP net income in Q4 2025 — financially unlevered and self-funding, which de-risks the R&D-across-four-fronts model.

## Industry Context

ONTO sits in **process control** — the inspection + metrology sub-stratum of wafer-fab equipment, structurally the fastest-growing slice of WFE (PC% rising from ~9% at 28nm toward 16-19% at 2nm/1.4nm) because every architectural transition (FinFET→GAA→CFET, monolithic→HBM stacking→hybrid bonding) *adds* inspection/measurement passes without removing any. The defining structural fact of the sub-sector (per [[Sectors/Advanced Semi Metrology]]): **process control is not one market** — it is a leading-edge inspection monopoly (KLA, 56-63% PC share, defect-library + Klarity/RAPID software moat) bolted to a contestable metrology field (Nova, ONTO) and an emerging advanced-packaging battleground (ONTO, Camtek) that KLA is now invading. ONTO holds ~6-8% of total process control but co-leads AP inspection with Camtek (~65% combined).

**Where ONTO sits in the value chain and who has leverage:**
- *Customers* (TSMC, Samsung, SK Hynix, Intel, Micron, OSATs) have **low** pricing power at the leading edge (12-24 month tool-of-record qualification, switching costs in the millions per tool per node) but **moderate** power in AP and at mature nodes where ONTO, Camtek and Chinese alternatives provide partial substitutes — i.e., exactly ONTO's mix is the part of process control where customers can push back hardest.
- *The AP collision* is the defining competitive dynamic: KLA's defect-resolution edge at HBM4 16-Hi / hybrid-bonding (sub-0.5nm copper-recess) means a *KLA-takes-the-high-end, specialists-hold-the-cost-sensitive-mid-range* split rather than winner-take-all. ONTO grows in absolute terms (every HBM stack multiplies touchpoints) but the highest-margin work concentrates with KLA.
- *The panel-litho new layer* is where ONTO has genuine *structural* advantage: round-wafer steppers cannot pattern rectangular panels, so JetStep competes against adapted Canon/Nikon/SCREEN/ASML panel efforts from a first-mover qualification position rather than against an entrenched monopoly. This is the one layer where ONTO is offense, not defense.
- *The integration / bundling threat* (AMAT PVI in-line metrology, LRCX Equipment Intelligence, ASML eScan) pressures *standalone metrology* — ONTO's Atlas/Iris lines — more than inspection or litho, a slow-moving TAM compressor at mature/mid nodes.

**Structural forces reshaping the sub-sector:** AP TAM-*creation* (bond-line, copper-recess, panel categories that did not exist before ~2022); GAA→CFET metrology-intensity step; HBM4→HBM5 hybrid-bonding (sub-10μm bump pitch → ~3x bond-line inspection intensity per [[Research/2026-05-11 - HBM Packaging Equipment Stack - MR-MUF to Hybrid Bonding Transition - deep-dive]]); the CoWoS→CoPoS format transition (now accelerated to 2028-29 HVM per the companion macro note); and the China substitution asymmetry that favors Camtek over ONTO in AP.

**Panel-level packaging — ONTO's actual CoPoS position.** Direct answers to the three questions the panel-litho leg of this thesis turns on:

*Share: there is none to measure yet.* CoPoS is pre-HVM — TSMC's pilot line completes ~June 2026 (VisEra), pilot production 2027, mass production 2H 2028-29 (AP7 Chiayi). You cannot hold share of a market that does not yet ship in volume. ONTO's real position is a **first-mover qualification lead in panel lithography — the one panel layer where round-wafer steppers physically do not apply** — JetStep qualified at two packaging suppliers to AI device makers, 2027 ramp. On the panel market that exists *today* (FOPLP for PMIC/RF at up to 620×750mm), ONTO's entire panel franchise (JetStep + Firefly) is ~$200M, <15% of 2026E revenue. ONTO is the presumptive front-runner in the uncontested-by-physics layer, not the holder of a measurable CoPoS production share.

*Uniqueness: yes for patterning, no for measurement — the question splits by process step.*

| Panel step | ONTO tool | Uniquely qualified? |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Lithography (patterning) | JetStep S3500/X500 | **Yes by physics, not by monopoly.** Round-wafer steppers (ASML/Canon/Nikon) cannot expose a 600mm+ rectangular panel or correct die-shift / CTE-mismatch / warpage. JetStep is purpose-built for it — but the lead is *first-mover qualification*, contestable by SUSS MicroTec and adapted Canon/Nikon/SCREEN panel tools, not a structural lock-in. |
| Inspection / metrology | Dragonfly, Iris, 3Di | **No — normal competition.** Adapt to panel format (the "double exposure" alongside litho) but compete head-on with KLA (high-resolution tier) and Camtek (mid-range / China). |

*Growth: a sized call-option, not a base case.* Scenarios against the ~$200M base:

| Scenario | Franchise by ~2028-29 | Condition |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Stagnation | ~$200M | CoPoS slips past 2029, or Canon/Nikon/SUSS capture the panel-litho share ONTO pioneered |
| Base | ~$350-450M | Two qualified suppliers convert to 2027 volume; ONTO holds a plurality as CoPoS reaches 515mm HVM (~20 interposers/panel, 12-16 HBM stacks) |
| Bull | ~$600M+ | JetStep becomes panel-litho tool-of-record at AP7; TAM compounds along the 310→515→750mm panel roadmap; Firefly/inspection attach rides along |

Even the bull case (~3x today) is ~30-40% of *current* revenue — differentiated, not company-transforming on its own. The larger engine stays AP inspection/metrology (>50% 2026E) and advanced nodes (~25%); panel litho is the asymmetric option layered on top, which is why the HIGH conviction trigger keys on panel backlog crossing ~$350M. Its value is not its size today but that it is the one layer where ONTO plays offense against incumbents who must re-tool — not defense against a monopoly that out-resolves it.

## Bull Case

ONTO compounds through 2028 as the diversified picks-and-shovels winner of *two* intensity waves plus a format-transition option:

(a) **AP sustains >50% growth and broadens** — Dragonfly G5 ramps across its 15+ application / 10+ customer pipeline, 3Di rides sub-6μm bump adoption, Iris films metrology scales, and the >$240M HBM VPA anchors 2026-27 visibility. AP becomes a durable >$750M franchise.

(b) **Advanced nodes compounds ~25%** — Atlas G6 wins broaden across GAA logic (2nd customer secured) and HBM4/advanced DRAM, with TSV metrology a new leg; advanced nodes doubles again toward $400-450M as 2nm GAA HVM and HBM4 DRAM ramp.

(c) **The panel-litho option pays off** — TSMC's CoPoS pilot (VisEra ~June 2026) confirms panel-format litho demand; JetStep converts its two qualified AI-packaging suppliers into volume in 2027 and wins CoPoS panel-litho tool-of-record positions into the AP7 2028-29 HVM ramp. The ~$200M panel franchise re-rates to a multi-hundred-million growth driver in a layer where round-wafer incumbents cannot directly compete.

(d) **Margins inflect** — operating margin crosses 30% by Q4 2026 and holds, non-GAAP GM stays 56%+, and the net-cash balance sheet funds R&D-across-four-fronts without dilution.

**Valuation framework:** if 2026 lands >$1.3B (+30%) and 2027 grows ~20% to ~$1.6B at >30% OM, non-GAAP EPS reaches ~$9-10 in 2027. Holding ~40x (a slight de-rate from 46x as growth normalizes) → ~$360-400; sustaining 46x on accelerating panel-litho optionality → ~$420-460 (+25-37%). The bull case is a *quality-of-growth re-rate*: the market reclassifies ONTO from "sub-scale challenger" to "path-agnostic transition winner with a unique litho call-option," and the multiple holds while earnings compound.

## Bear Case

ONTO de-rates hard as the AP-capex wave digests and the competitive reality reasserts:

(a) **KLA crushes the high end and the duopoly story cracks** — the sharpest short take alleges ONTO *failed* TSMC CoWoS-L qualification at the tighter bump pitch and that KLA's repurposed process-control tools out-resolve ONTO wherever they are pointed at AP inspection. KLA's AP revenue (+70% to $950M) is taking exactly the highest-value HBM4/hybrid-bond work; ONTO is left with cost-sensitive, margin-capped mid-range volume. The "Onto + Camtek = 65%" share erodes as KLA invades from the top and China-domestic entrants take the bottom.

(b) **China is structurally Camtek's, not ONTO's** — US manufacturing blocks ONTO from selling AP tools freely into mainland China, the fastest-localizing, highest-volume AP market; Camtek ships there at scale. A slice of ONTO's modeled AP TAM is unwinnable, and tightening export controls widen the gap.

(c) **2026 is an AP-capex peak, not a structural step** — after a flat 2025 (+1.8%), the +30% 2026 guide is a cyclical AI-packaging spike. 2027 digests; AP growth decelerates below +20%; advanced-nodes orders normalize after the GAA pull-forward; the HBM VPA proves to have front-loaded demand.

(d) **The multiple is the position's own worst enemy** — at ~46x forward / ~65x trailing after a +174% run, with the Street's target only ~5% above spot and the P/E ~326% above its 5-year median, ONTO prices in flawless execution. Any guide-down, GAAP-margin slip (already 50.1% vs 53.7%), or panel-litho timing slip triggers multiple compression *and* estimate cuts simultaneously — the classic high-multiple semicap drawdown. Past cyclical lows show the downside volatility is severe; a re-rate to even 28-30x forward on flat 2027 estimates is ~-35% to -40%.

(e) **Panel-litho optionality fails to convert** — CoPoS slips right (TSMC panel-yield miss pushes HVM past 2029), or ASML/Canon/Nikon take the panel-litho share ONTO pioneered, and the ~$200M franchise stays ~$200M. The single most differentiated piece of the bull case evaporates.

The world where ONTO loses money: you buy the vault's "uncovered panel-litho gap" *after* the market already paid up for it, the AP cycle rolls over in 2027, KLA confirms high-end AP dominance, and the 46x multiple halves.
