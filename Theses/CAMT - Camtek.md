# Camtek

## Summary

The vault already reached a verdict on Camtek: [[Sectors/Advanced Semi Metrology]] (2026-05-27) flagged it as the "most contestable moat, highest China exposure" specialist priced "~33x, near KLA," arguing the multiple gap "should be wider." Since then the market did the work: CAMT has de-rated from a ~$190 spring peak / ~49x forward to ~$144 / ~37x forward on repeated "sell-the-news" pullbacks (down ~7% on Q1 results, down ~7.5% on a $105M order win). So the live question is no longer "is it overpriced" (that excess has largely bled off) but "is the HBM4-metrology tool-of-record franchise plus a hybrid-bonding option worth a roughly-KLA multiple on the single most China-concentrated, most cyclically-levered, most competitively-squeezed member of the process-control field." Within HBM 3D metrology Camtek holds a **qualification-gate-like lock** the "contestable specialist" label understates (tool of record at all three HBM makers, >$260M of committed 2026-27 orders, 2027 delivery discussions already underway), but that franchise sits on a business where China is 49% of revenue and rising, KLA is invading the high end (Morgan Stanley models Camtek HBM growth decelerating 31%→23% on KLA competition), Nova out-invests it from a larger base, and a thin recurring-revenue cushion makes it de-rate hardest in any digestion. Medium conviction: a genuinely strong niche franchise and a fair-not-cheap price, but a semi-cyclical you time, not a compounder you hold.

## Key Non-consensus Insights

**1. The vault's own "over-valued near KLA" bear is already largely spent: CAMT is the mirror image of ONTO, and that repricing is the most important fact today.** Six weeks ago the sector note argued Camtek's ~33x deserved a wider discount to KLA. The market has since resolved the tension in the opposite direction from ONTO: where ONTO re-rated up (+174%) and killed the vault's "cheap" entry, CAMT has de-rated down, from ~$190 / ~49x forward to ~$144 / ~37x forward, a ~25% drawdown despite a stream of record-order announcements. At ~37x forward on rising 2027 estimates (consensus $663M, Morgan Stanley $716M), Camtek is now priced roughly in line with KLA (30-36x) and below Nova (~34-49x), no longer an obvious "monopoly multiple on a contestable business." The consensus and the vault both still carry the "expensive" prior; the valuation excess has quietly corrected while the order book strengthened, so the risk/reward is less asymmetric-negative than the stale house view implies. The setup rewards buyers of cyclical de-ratings, not sellers of euphoria.

**2. "Tool of record for HBM4 3D metrology at all major manufacturers" is a qualification-gate-like lock the "contestable specialist" framing structurally cannot see.** The sector note buckets Camtek as "(i)/(iii) contestable: AP inspection, China-exposed." But the specific claim management makes, and that the >$260M multi-year VPA from two HBM makers corroborates, is that Camtek is the tool of reference for 3D metrology at all major HBM players (SK Hynix, Samsung, Micron) for HBM4. In that narrow niche, this is closer to model #2 flavor (ii), a qualification gate only one vendor cleanly crosses, than to a commodity. HBM 3D metrology (bump height/coplanarity, TSV, RDL overlay, warpage on a stacked die worth thousands of dollars) is failure-sensitive enough that the qualified incumbent is sticky across a node, and Camtek's Hawk platform (built for wafers with 500M microbumps + hybrid bonding) extends the lock into HBM4→HBM5. The caveat that keeps this from being a KLA-grade moat: it is a gate on a younger, HBM-cycle-levered discipline without KLA's 25-year defect-library substrate, but within HBM metrology specifically, Camtek is a near-monopoly the market prices as one contestable name among four.

**3. China is a barbell, not a uniform risk, and the market prices two opposite exposures as one negative number.** Camtek's 49%-and-rising China revenue decomposes into two vectors pointing in opposite directions. Vector A (bullish): mainstream / OSAT advanced-packaging demand that US-domiciled peers, ONTO (US manufacturing) and KLA, are increasingly export-blocked from serving, handing Camtek (Israel) a structural share gift as Western competitors retreat from exactly the fastest-localizing AP market. Vector B (bearish): that same segment is the one most exposed to domestic substitution: Skyverse (state-backed; Redwood-900 detecting 10nm defects, shipped 2025) is climbing the AP-inspection curve fastest where the optics barrier is lowest. These are a race, not a level (does Camtek harvest the US-peer-retreat share faster than domestic entrants displace its installed base?), yet the sell-side collapses both into "China = risk." It is a timing question with a genuinely two-sided distribution, not the one-directional overhang the consensus prices. (Nuance the ONTO thesis got slightly wrong: Camtek is subject to US EAR via de-minimis US-content rules (the tightened SME controls carry no de-minimis floor for advanced-node-destined tools), so Camtek is less constrained than a US firm, not unconstrained.)

**4. Camtek is the purest, highest-beta public way to own the HBM/AP-inspection intensity wave, which is simultaneously the appeal and the trap, and classification (model #13) is the entire investment.** Unlike KLA (compounder cushioned by a ~$4B service annuity) or ONTO (diversified across inspection + metrology + panel litho + software), Camtek is a near-pure HBM/AP-inspection play (~60-70% advanced packaging, ~50% AI/HPC) with a thin recurring-revenue base. In an up-cycle it out-grows the whole field (highest growth of the trio); in a digestion it de-rates hardest, and the Q1'26 operating-margin dip to 25.5% (from ~30%) plus the reflexive "sell-the-news" reactions show the market is already pricing cyclical nervousness. The sector's most expensive mistake (#13) is treating a semi-cyclical AP-S-curve specialist as a compounder because the growth looks structural at the top of an order surge. Owned correctly, Camtek is a cyclical you accumulate into digestion fear and trim into euphoria, the exact opposite of buy-and-hold KLA.

**5. Hybrid bonding is the multi-year option under the cycle noise: it converts the contestable inspection position toward a metrology-intensity gate.** The market fixates on the near-term HBM3E→HBM4 transition air-pocket (Samsung inventory digestion). Underneath, the HBM4→HBM5 hybrid-bonding shift (sub-10μm → sub-5μm bump pitch, copper-recess metrology) multiplies 3D-metrology intensity per stack (~3x at HBM4 bond-line, ~2x again at HBM5 per [[Research/2026-05-11 - HBM Packaging Equipment Stack - MR-MUF to Hybrid Bonding Transition - deep-dive]]), and Camtek's Hawk platform is purpose-built for it (500M-microbump / hybrid-bond inspection + 3D metrology). Hybrid-bonding revenue is "moderate" today with commercial volumes guided to 2027+. As hybrid bonding scales, per-stack metrology content and stickiness both step up, and the discipline shifts from "contestable optical AP inspection" toward "physics-and-modeling 3D metrology intensity", the one path where Camtek's niche gate widens rather than commoditizes. This is the structural leg that could earn the multiple; it is also the leg most dependent on a smooth HBM4→HBM5 cadence.

## Outstanding Questions

**1. Is the HBM4 3D-metrology "tool of record at all major manufacturers" a durable per-node qualification gate, or a share position KLA/Nova can contest at HBM5?** The entire "insight #2" rests on this being sticky. Morgan Stanley already models Camtek HBM growth decelerating 31%→23% into 2027 specifically on intensifying KLA competition, and Nova won a leading-foundry AP-metrology evaluation (Nova WMC) in July 2026. *Resolution: named HBM4/HBM5 metrology tool-of-record disclosures at SK Hynix/Samsung/Micron; whether the >$260M VPA renews and expands; SEMICON competitive intelligence; Camtek 3D-metrology share trajectory 2026→2027.*

**2. How much of the >25% H2-2026 acceleration and the 2027 ramp ($663-716M) is committed backlog versus forecast that can push right?** Guidance leans on "record orders" and $260M+ of HBM visibility, but Samsung's advanced-packaging capex pause and HBM3E→HBM4 inventory digestion are live near-term headwinds. If H2 order conversion slips, both 2026 estimates and the forward multiple compress together. *Resolution: book-to-bill and backlog composition in Q2/Q3 2026 prints; HBM-maker capex guidance; whether Q1-Q2 2027 delivery discussions convert to firm POs.*

**3. Does China revenue race up (US-peer-retreat share gift) or down (domestic substitution + tightening export controls) over the next 8 quarters?** At 49% and rising, China is the swing variable for the whole thesis, and it has a genuinely two-sided distribution. *Resolution: quarterly China revenue mix; Skyverse / domestic-entrant win disclosures at Chinese OSATs; BIS rule changes on AP/mature-node SME; Camtek's own commentary on China order durability (Q3 2025 already flagged China "revenue-share contradictions").*

**4. Can Camtek's ~50-51% gross margin and ~30% operating margin hold through a digestion, or is the thin recurring-revenue base a margin-reversion trap (model #6)?** Q1'26 OM already fell to 25.5% from ~30%. KLA's peak margins hold in downturns because a $4B annuity + qualification stickiness resist commodity reversion; Camtek lacks that cushion. *Resolution: GM/OM trajectory across the 2026 digestion; service-and-software revenue mix disclosure; pricing commentary as KLA/Nova compete in overlapping AP categories.*

**5. Is the customer concentration (two HBM makers driving the $260M VPA, three HBM makers = the whole HBM thesis) a survival-test exposure (model #10) rather than "visibility"?** A multi-year VPA is a positive, but the flip side is that a single HBM-maker capex pause or an allocation shift toward a competitor's metrology tool removes a large, abrupt slice. *Resolution: customer-concentration disclosure in the 20-F; whether HBM orders broaden beyond two named makers; Samsung's re-engagement timing.*

**6. Does Camtek have a credible seat in the CoWoS→CoPoS panel transition, or does it cede panel to ONTO's litho-anchored position and to larger players?** Camtek's Golden Eagle covers FO-PLP panel inspection/metrology up to 650×650mm, a real foothold, but Camtek has no panel lithography (ONTO's JetStep differentiator), and TSMC's CoPoS pilot (June 2026 line completion, 2028-29 HVM) will set tool-of-record positions. *Resolution: TSMC CoPoS panel inspection/metrology tool selections; Camtek panel-level order disclosures; whether Golden Eagle scales with FOPLP HVM.*

**7. What is Camtek worth in a genuine down-cycle: what is the trough multiple and earnings base?** The bull case is a continuation call; the IC needs the downside. At ~37x forward with a thin annuity, a semi-cap digestion historically compresses these names to the mid-20s multiple on falling estimates. *Resolution: prior-cycle CAMT trough multiple/earnings; the 2019 and 2022-23 drawdown magnitudes; sensitivity of 2027 EPS to a 15-20% HBM order pushout.*

## Business Model & Product Description

Camtek designs and sells the **optical inspection and 3D metrology tools** that find defects and measure features in advanced packaging, the back-end 2.5D/3D stacking, HBM, chiplet, and hybrid-bonding steps where AI accelerators are assembled. Camtek is the quality auditor of the package, not the wafer: where KLA polices front-end leading-edge wafer defects near-monopolistically, Camtek owns the youngest, fastest-growing slice of process control (bump / RDL / microbump / TSV / interposer inspection + 3D metrology) that barely existed as a commercial category before ~2020. It is a focused pure-play, the opposite of ONTO's diversified breadth: no lithography, no front-end logic OCD franchise, thin software, just the deepest specialization in AP optical inspection and 3D metrology, concentrated where HBM stacking multiplies inspection touchpoints per die.

Founded **1987 in Migdal HaEmek, Israel**, beginning in printed-circuit-board automated optical inspection (AOI): the PCB-vision heritage is the DNA behind its 2D/3D optical-inspection edge. Founder **Rafi Amit** has led as CEO/Chairman across most of the company's history; **Lior Aviram** was appointed Executive Chairman (effective June 2025), a governance/succession evolution to watch. Founder-led, long-tenured, focused: a [G-5] corporate-quality profile (low bureaucracy, single-franchise focus) with a still-maturing separation of chairman/CEO roles.

**Revenue segmentation** (FY2025 ~$496.1M; Camtek reports by end-market rather than product line: the economics are best captured by application mix):

| Application bucket | ~% of rev | Growth character | What it is |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| AI / HPC advanced packaging | ~50% | The engine — HBM, 2.5D/CoWoS, chiplet | Eagle/Hawk inspection + 3D metrology for AI-accelerator packaging; the >$260M HBM VPA lives here |
| Other advanced packaging | ~15-20% | Cyclical-growth | Fan-out, SiP, RF, CIS, compound-semi packaging |
| Non-AP / mainstream (CMOS sensors, mature) | ~30-35% | Cyclical / China-weighted | Legacy AOI + mature-node/PCB-adjacent inspection; a disproportionate share of the China 49% |

**Key products:**
- **Hawk**, the high-end platform (launched Feb 2025, SEMICON Korea): inspection + 3D measurement of wafers with up to **500 million micro-bumps** and hybrid-bonding structures; the HBM4 / chiplet / hybrid-bonding workhorse that expands Camtek's TAM into KLA's high-resolution zone. >$50M initial orders (Nov 2024); a $25M single-IDM order (part of $45M aggregate) in 2026; enhancements for higher throughput due early 2027.
- **Eagle** (incl. Eagle G5): the volume 2D/3D AP inspection + metrology franchise: bump height/coplanarity, RDL line/space, microbump and TSV inspection at production throughput.
- **Golden Eagle**: panel-level (FO-PLP) inspection/metrology up to **650×650mm**, Camtek's seat at the panel-packaging table (relevant to CoPoS), though inspection/metrology only, with no panel-lithography analog to ONTO's JetStep.
- **MicroProf**, advanced 3D metrology: total-thickness-variation (TTV), wafer warp, TSV metrology, RDL overlay, the measurement suite that underpins the "HBM4 tool-of-record" claim.

**Order signal (FY26):** unprecedented Q1'26 order intake: OSAT orders alone >$90M in the quarter, >$260M HBM VPA (two makers, 2026-27), and a late-June >$105M multi-system award ($55M AI + $50M Hawk). Management is already discussing Q1-Q2 2027 deliveries. The order momentum is the strongest single support for the H2'26 / 2027 acceleration, and the market's repeated failure to reward it is the strongest tell that ownership is nervous about cyclicality.

**Balance sheet:** ~$670M cash vs ~$488M convertible debt (net cash ~$362M) after a Q3 2025 refinancing ($500M new converts issued, $267M old repurchased, an $89M one-time GAAP loss that distorts trailing GAAP EPS/ROE). FY2025 FCF ~$112M. Financially sound and self-funding, though less fortress-like than ONTO's ~$850M net cash / zero-debt profile.

## Industry Context

Camtek sits in **advanced-packaging process control**, the inspection + 3D-metrology sub-stratum of the back-end, and the fastest-growing pocket of a fast-growing category (process control is rising from ~9% of WFE at 28nm toward 16-19% at 2nm/HBM4 because every architectural transition adds inspection passes without removing any; full treatment in [[Sectors/Advanced Semi Metrology]]). The defining structural fact of the sub-sector: **process control is not one market.** It is a leading-edge inspection monopoly (KLA, 56-63% process-control share, defect-library + Klarity/RAPID software moat) bolted to a contestable metrology field (Nova, ONTO) and an emerging advanced-packaging battleground (Camtek, ONTO) that KLA is now invading from the top.

**Where Camtek sits and who has leverage:**
- **Camtek's home turf**, 2D/3D optical AP inspection + 3D metrology (bump/RDL/microbump/TSV/HBM), is the youngest, least defect-library-dependent, and therefore most contestable segment of process control, which is exactly why it grows fastest and why its moat is thinner than KLA's. The "Onto + Camtek ≈ 65% of AP inspection" figure the vault carries is a specialist-duopoly simplification of the 2D/3D optical AP-inspection niche; external market data show the broader metrology/inspection market is KLA-led and fragmented (top-5 ≈ 61%), so the clean-duopoly framing overstates concentration: treat it as "two leading AP-inspection specialists," not a locked two-player market.
- **The squeeze from both sides is the defining competitive dynamic.** From above: KLA is invading high-resolution AP inspection/metrology (AP revenue +70% to ~$950M), and its sub-0.5nm copper-recess defect resolution wins the highest-value HBM4/hybrid-bond work; Morgan Stanley explicitly attributes Camtek's 2027 HBM deceleration (31%→23%) to KLA. From scale: Nova (~$880M revenue, 57.6% GM, ~$1.6B cash) out-invests Camtek in R&D, has a deeper dimensional-metrology franchise, and won a leading-foundry AP-metrology evaluation (Nova WMC) in July 2026. Camtek is the smallest of the four ($496M) defending the most contestable ground.
- **The China axis is Camtek's single biggest differentiator and biggest risk simultaneously** (see Insight #3). It is the one of the four with both its largest growth market and its most credible substitution threat in the same geography, the double-edged exposure that should structurally cap the multiple it deserves relative to KLA, even after the recent de-rate.

**Structural forces reshaping the sub-sector** (from the sector note, applied to Camtek):
- **AP TAM-creation** (bond-line, copper-recess, panel categories that did not exist before ~2022): Camtek's core growth engine; management targets a ~$2B addressable market by 2027.
- **HBM4→HBM5 hybrid bonding (2026-2030)**: the architecture shift (#8) that remaps AP metrology intensity; the multi-year prize under Camtek's Hawk platform (Insight #5).
- **CoWoS→CoPoS panel transition (2028-29 HVM)**: Camtek plays via Golden Eagle inspection/metrology, but not patterning; a smaller, more contested seat than ONTO's litho-anchored position.
- **China substitution asymmetry**: the sub-sector's sharpest China risk lands squarely on Camtek (AP inspection is the least optics-bound, most policy-localizable segment), the mirror of KLA being nearly insulated.
- **The integration/bundling threat** (AMAT PVI in-line metrology, AMAT-BESI Kinex D2W, LRCX Equipment Intelligence) pressures standalone metrology, a slower-moving TAM compressor at the mature/mid edge that touches Camtek's non-HBM lines more than its HBM franchise.

## Bull Case

Camtek compounds through 2027-28 as the purest, highest-torque AP/HBM-inspection winner, and the market re-rates the quality of growth it de-rated on cyclical fear:

(a) **HBM4 tool-of-record converts to durable share.** The >$260M VPA from two HBM makers ramps, Camtek holds tool-of-record 3D-metrology status across all three HBM manufacturers into HBM4 HVM, and 2027 revenue reaches the Morgan Stanley $716M (vs consensus $663M) as Q1-Q2 2027 delivery discussions convert to firm orders. HBM metrology becomes a genuine per-node qualification gate.

(b) **Hybrid bonding scales the intensity step (Insight #5).** As HBM4→HBM5 hybrid bonding moves to volume (2027+), Hawk's 3D-metrology content per stack steps up ~3-5x, shifting Camtek's mix toward the stickier physics-and-modeling metrology tier and widening the niche gate.

(c) **China races up, not down.** Camtek harvests the AP share US peers (ONTO/KLA) are export-blocked from, faster than domestic entrants (Skyverse) displace it: 49% China holds or grows on mainstream/OSAT AP localization, and the feared overhang proves a tailwind through 2027.

(d) **Margins hold and the multiple re-rates.** Non-GAAP GM stays ~51-52%, OM recovers toward ~30% as volume returns in H2'26, and the market reclassifies CAMT from "cyclical priced near KLA" to "structural HBM-metrology gate," holding ~37-40x on compounding earnings.

**Valuation framework:** if 2026 lands ~$565M (+14%) and 2027 reaches ~$700M (+24%) at ~30% OM, non-GAAP EPS approaches ~$4.50-5.00 in 2027. Holding ~37x → ~$165-185 (+15-29% from ~$144); a quality-of-growth re-rate to ~42x on proven HBM4-gate durability → ~$190-210 (+32-46%). The bull case is the market paying up again for a franchise it just marked down on cycle fear that did not materialize.

## Bear Case

CAMT de-rates further and estimates crack as the HBM order surge digests and the competitive/geographic reality reasserts:

(a) **The HBM3E→HBM4 transition air-pocket is real and near.** Samsung's inventory digestion and advanced-packaging capex pause (the specific near-term bear) push H2'26 order conversion right; the ">25% H2 vs H1" guide misses; and because ~50% of revenue is AI/HBM, a single-quarter HBM pushout craters both the 2026 print and the 2027 ramp the multiple depends on.

(b) **KLA takes the high end; Nova takes the metrology scale.** KLA's resolution edge wins the highest-value HBM4/hybrid-bond work (the reason MS models 31%→23% deceleration), and Nova, bigger, better-capitalized, fresh off a foundry AP-metrology win, contests the dimensional-metrology tier. Camtek, smallest of the four, is squeezed from premium and scale simultaneously, and the "tool of record" status proves node-specific and re-contestable at HBM5.

(c) **China is structurally Camtek's biggest liability, not its gift.** BIS tightens AP/mature-node SME controls (no de-minimis floor already applies to advanced-node-destined tools), and/or Skyverse-class domestic entrants take material share at Chinese OSATs. 49% of revenue is the most China-concentrated exposure in the group, a double-cut that no other process-control name carries at this magnitude.

(d) **Thin recurring revenue = margin-reversion trap (model #6).** Without KLA's ~$4B annuity, Camtek's ~30% OM reverts hard in digestion (Q1'26 already showed 25.5%); the semi-cyclical downcycle compresses margin and multiple together, the classic high-beta semi-cap drawdown.

(e) **The multiple is still not cheap after the de-rate.** ~37x forward / ~45x EV/EBITDA on a near-pure-cyclical specialist prices in the H2 acceleration and the 2027 ramp; a base-rate-consistent reversion to the mid-20s forward on flat 2027 estimates is another ~-30% to -40% from ~$144.

The world where CAMT loses money: you buy a "de-rated" ~37x specialist, the HBM order book proves front-loaded, Samsung's digestion drags into 2027, KLA confirms high-end AP dominance, China revenue erodes on controls and substitution, and a ~$144 stock re-rates to the mid-20s on cut estimates, a compounding -40%+.

## Risks

**Thesis risks** (the investment case is wrong):

1. **KLA and Nova compress Camtek's AP niche from both ends.** KLA takes the high-resolution HBM4/hybrid-bond tier; Nova's scale + fresh foundry AP-metrology win contests dimensional metrology. The "HBM4 tool-of-record" proves node-specific and Camtek's share erodes at HBM5: the core of insight #2 breaks. *Resolution: named tool-of-record disclosures; 3D-metrology share trajectory; SEMICON intelligence.*

2. **China erodes (controls + substitution), the double-cut.** BIS tightening blocks more of Camtek's accessible AP TAM and/or Skyverse-class domestic entrants take share at Chinese OSATs. At 49% of revenue, this is the largest single-factor thesis risk. *Resolution: BIS rules; quarterly China mix; domestic-entrant win disclosures.*

3. **The HBM order surge is a cyclical peak, not a structural step.** Samsung digestion + HBM3E→HBM4 transition gap push order conversion right; the >25% H2 guide misses; 2027 estimates prove front-loaded. *Resolution: book-to-bill; HBM-maker capex; VPA renewal.*
