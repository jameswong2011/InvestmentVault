# MU - Micron Technology

## Summary

$1.04T (12 Aug, ~$924; 52-week $113–$1,255) after FQ3-26 printed $41.46B revenue, 84.6% GAAP gross margin, $28.24B NI, $24.67 EPS, and guided FQ4 $50.0B ± $1.0B at ~86% GM — a single quarter that exceeds every full year through FY24. The market reads that print plus 16 Mar IR HBM4-12H HVP and Huang's 1 Jun "all three qualified / in production" as a US HBM4 winner that has joined the contracted-memory re-rate. The variant: Micron is a **qualified third source on the bench, not cubes on Rubin boards**. First-wave bits stay ~70/30/0 (SK Hynix / Samsung / MU) until a Q3 board meter; no post-1 Jun allocation print exists. Queue ≠ rent — SK Hynix removed LTA caps, Micron did not — so physical tightness can persist while MU's contracted upside is capped. Industry L1 (DRAM no longer as cyclical) is a hypothesis whose falsifier is 2018 destock, not the base rate; the historian analog is 2017–18 tightness-identified → 2019 destock. GB300 LPDDR5X sole-source is dated H2 2026; value-layer monopoly is none from 2027. This file is process hygiene for the orphan MU notes, not a hedge on [[Theses/000660 - SK Hynix]] (that kill is Samsung >35% of Rubin, not MU share). Conviction low: the print is real, the allocation and the rent are not, and $1.04T at 84.6% GM is the 2018 setup with better optics.

## Key Non-consensus Insights

- **Qualified third source ≠ first-wave cubes**
  - **Consensus:** 16 Mar IR ("HBM4 36GB 12H in high-volume production, designed for Vera Rubin") plus Huang 1 Jun GTC Taipei ("all three vendors have been qualified… all three are in production") prices MU as a Rubin HBM4 supplier. Sell-side interpolates "the remainder" after 60–70/25–30 SKH/Samsung.
  - **Variant:** Qualification and "in production" put MU on the bench. They do not put cubes on Rubin boards. Vault and SemiAnalysis first-wave bits are ~70/30/0 until a Q3 board meter; no allocation print has landed after 1 Jun. HBM4 revenue >$1B in FQ3 can be lead-customer / non-Rubin / pre-board inventory — the 8-K does not disclose HBM as a percent of revenue, so mix cannot be audited. 000660's kill is Samsung >35%, not MU taking a slice.
  - **First confirming observable [2026-10]:** Q3 2026 Rubin board meter (Nvidia ship-quarter disclosure or a teardown/BOM series) showing MU cubes on boards at a printed >0% of first-wave HBM4 bits.
  - **Falsifier:** Q3 board meter confirms ~70/30/0 — same condition as → LOW.

- **Queue ≠ rent — SKH took the caps off, MU did not**
  - **Consensus:** TrendForce 1Q26 DRAM contracts +93–98%, 2Q +58–63%, 3Q US-cloud band +13–18% plus "sold out through 2027" language = scarcity rent accruing to all three names equally. Baker's mid-single-digit P/E vs WFE ~40× is the cheap-memory pitch.
  - **Variant:** Shanaka's split is load-bearing. The queue is physics + quals + delivery priority; the rent is LTA structure + residual spot + political tolerance. By 3Q26 large US clouds were already inside multi-year agreements that limited further repricing to 13–18% after the 1Q violence. SK Hynix removed LTA price caps (Jul 2); Micron did not. MU can sit in a tight physical queue and still have its incremental dollar capped. "Long scarcity is not long rent."
  - **First confirming observable [2026-09]:** MU IR discloses LTA-cap removal on the SKH pattern, or residual spot re-opens enough that MU's realized DRAM ASP tracks uncapped SKH rather than the 13–18% protected band.
  - **Falsifier:** Next two contract prints stay inside the protected band while SKH's uncapped book keeps widening the realized-ASP gap — same condition as → LOW / rent-roll leg of the bear.

- **L1 is a hypothesis; the falsifier is 2018 destock, not a new base rate**
  - **Consensus:** Memory triopoly + HBM LTAs + wafer inelasticity have retired the cycle. Mid-cycle multiples migrate 7–10× → 15–25×. FQ3 84.6% GM / FQ4 ~86% and $1.04T are mid-cycle, not peak.
  - **Variant:** L1 is dated personal theory, not the outside view. The reference class is every DRAM cycle since 1995, and the historian analog is specific: 2017–18 tightness-identified (TTID) → 2019 destock. Peak margins + peak capex + peak share price have never held together for 24 months in this industry. CXMT at ~7% global DRAM revenue already changes OEM negotiation posture (Apple testing iPhone/MacBook lines) without touching HBM. The 3:1 wafer penalty that tightens commodity DRAM is the same mechanism that, once HBM wafers reverse, floods the residual market.
  - **First confirming observable [2026-12]:** Two consecutive quarters of DRAM contract prices still rising with OEM inventory days flat-to-down — the analog fails to rhyme.
  - **Falsifier:** 2018-style destock — two consecutive quarters of DRAM contracts −20%+ and rising OEM inventory days — same condition as → CLOSE.

- **GB300 LPDDR5X sole-source is dated H2 2026; VLM is none from 2027**
  - **Consensus:** SOCAMM / LPDDR5X on GB300 (and SOCAMM2 on Vera) is a platform lock — Micron as the only memory name qualified across HBM4 + SOCAMM2 + Gen6 SSD, a layer the market has not finished pricing.
  - **Variant:** The sole-source, if it still holds, is a dated H2 2026 fact. From 2027 the layer is contestable: Samsung and SK Hynix are in the SOCAMM/LPDDR5X qualification pool, Vera-class CPUs re-open the socket every generation, and a sole-source on a JEDEC-family part is a qualification gate with a half-life, not interface control. Value-layer monopoly tests fail on non-rivalry (real incremental wafer cost), fail on standard control (JEDEC), and the AI-overlay "infrastructure widening" does not convert a time-bounded sole-source into a layer. Score: none 2027+.
  - **First confirming observable [2026-12]:** A 2027 GB300-class or Vera-successor BOM that still lists MU as sole LPDDR5X/SOCAMM source.
  - **Falsifier:** Dual-source of GB300 LPDDR5X before YE2026, or a 2027 platform that drops the sole-source — same condition as → CLOSE.

- **No IR 8-Hi SKU, and the 8-K hides the mix that would prove otherwise**
  - **Consensus:** Rubin Ultra's 8-Hi HBM4 despec is a cycle break / HBM revenue cliff (or, on the bull side, a volume unlock that MU captures as the third source).
  - **Variant:** Damnang's volume-unlock math (output ≤1.63×, HBM $ holding 81.5–98% of planned 4E) is a sold-out-market coordination, not a cycle death. Relative winner among the three is Samsung (standard 8-Hi yield/capacity). Micron has no IR 8-Hi HBM4 SKU — the disclosed stack is 12H 36GB, with 16H 48GB sampled. Combined with no HBM % of revenue in the FQ3 8-K, the market cannot tell whether the $41.46B / 84.6% GM print is HBM rent, commodity-DRAM rent, or a one-quarter ASP spike. Opacity is the tell, not a rounding omission.
  - **First confirming observable [2026-09]:** FQ4 8-K or IR deck discloses HBM as a percent of revenue, or an 8-Hi HBM4 SKU appears in IR.
  - **Falsifier:** Next two prints still omit HBM % and still list no 8-Hi SKU while Samsung's 8-Hi volume is the despec winner — mix stays unauditable.

A teardown series, Nvidia ship-quarter supplier comment, or a named OEM BOM is the only meter. If the meter prints 0%, the "remainder" interpolation dies and this file stays a coverage note. Answered by: Q3 2026 board meter.

- **When, if ever, does MU remove LTA caps?** SKH did on 2 Jul. MU's book is still capped. The 3Q26 +13–18% US-cloud band is the rent ceiling under current paper. Without a cap-removal disclosure, FQ4 $50B / ~86% GM can be the last uncapped-looking quarter as protected agreements roll. Answered by: MU IR/earnings language on LTA structure, or a realized-ASP gap vs SKH that cannot be explained by mix.

- **Is the 2018 destock analog already in the second derivative?** 1Q +93–98% → 2Q +58–63% → 3Q protected +13–18%. That is the same shape as late-2017 / early-2018, when prices were still up and the destock was two quarters away. What would make 2026 different is OEM inventory days staying flat while AI bit demand absorbs the 3:1 HBM wafer penalty. Answered by: two more TrendForce contract prints + hyperscaler inventory-days commentary (Amazon already named "higher memory costs" as a 2026 capex driver — the opposite of destock, until it isn't).

- **What is HBM as a percent of FQ3 / FQ4 revenue?** >$1B HBM4 shipped is a unit claim, not a mix claim. Data-center revenue >$25B in FQ3 can be HBM + server DDR5 + LPDDR/SOCAMM + SSD. Without the split, 84.6% GM is an average that can collapse from the commodity side while HBM holds, or the reverse. Answered by: the first 8-K or IR deck that prints the HBM %.

- **Does the GB300 LPDDR5X sole-source survive into 2027?** Dated H2 2026. Vera SOCAMM2 is a second socket, not an extension of the same sole-source. A 2027 dual-source or a Vera-successor that re-opens the socket is the VLM kill. Answered by: 2027 platform BOM or a Samsung/SKH SOCAMM qualification print.

- **Does Apple–CXMT change commodity pricing behavior before it changes share?** CXMT ~7% of global DRAM revenue, DDR5/LPDDR5X in production, HP/Acer already qualifying outside the US. The TSPA claim is procurement leverage, not near-term HBM displacement. If Apple qualifies, Samsung/SKH/MU lose a negotiation chip on the residual commodity book that is currently subsidising the 84.6% blended GM. Answered by: Apple qualification confirmation or a documented dual-source award.

- **Does MU ever ship an 8-Hi HBM4 SKU, or is the despec a Samsung volume event?** No IR 8-Hi SKU today. If Rubin Ultra's 8-Hi is the volume path and MU stays on 12H, MU misses the one architectural move that raises stack count from scarce wafers. Answered by: an IR 8-Hi SKU or a teardown showing MU 8-Hi on Ultra boards.

- **What does $1.04T require as an endgame, and is that endgame mid-cycle or peak?** ~$50B FQ4 NI run-rate ($31 EPS × ~1.15B) against $1.04T is ~7× peak-quarter annualized earnings and ~21× LTM. The multiple is "cheap" only if $80B+ NI is mid-cycle. The 2018 analog says it is peak. Answered by: FQ1-27 and FQ2-27 GM vs the 86% guide — the first two prints after the $50B quarter.

## Business Model & Product Description

Micron is the US leg of the DRAM/NAND triopoly: design, fabricate, stack, and sell memory as components and modules. Analogy: **SK Hynix is the qualified-gate landlord collecting uncapped rent on the Rubin floor; Micron is the third-source warehouse whose door is unlocked and whose loading dock is still assigned to the first two.** The print is a warehouse selling every crate in stock at spot-plus; the thesis question is whether the next lease is uncapped.

**Reported mix (FQ3-26):**

| Line | Revenue | % | What it is |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| DRAM | $31.33B | 76% | DDR5, LPDDR5X/SOCAMM/SOCAMM2, GDDR, HBM3E, HBM4 12H |
| NAND | $9.94B | 24% | Data-center SSD (>$5B in FQ3, +100%+ QoQ), client/auto UFS, QLC 245TB |
| Other (NOR) | $0.19B | — | Immaterial |
| **Total** | **$41.46B** | **100%** | Data-center >$25B (annualized >$100B) |

**HBM.** HBM4 36GB 12-high on 1β, >2.8 TB/s, >11 Gb/s pin, 20% better W than HBM3E; >$1B HBM4 shipped in FQ3; ramp "twice as fast" as HBM3E 12H. HBM4E on 1γ (first EUV node) with TSMC base-die, volume calendar 2027. HBM3E 8-high and 12-high in production. Management targets HBM share "close to DRAM share" because of the wafer-trade ratio — a deliberate ceiling, not a bid for SKH's 57%. No IR 8-Hi HBM4 SKU. No HBM % of revenue in the 8-K.

**Server / LP DRAM.** 1γ 16Gb LPDDR5X in HVP at a lead smartphone OEM; 24Gb sampling. LP5X SOCAMM2 in HVP, 48–256GB, designed for Vera Rubin NVL72 and standalone Vera (up to 2TB / 1.2 TB/s per CPU). GB300 LPDDR5X/SOCAMM sole-source dated H2 2026. 256GB DDR5 RDIMM (1γ + 3D die-stack) sampled to server enablers.

**NAND.** G9 PCIe Gen6 SSD in HVP; 245TB QLC shipping; Gen5 QLC client SSD lead-customer qualified. Competes with [[Theses/SNDK - SanDisk]] and [[Theses/285A - Kioxia]] on enterprise SSD, not on HBF (SKH+SNDK workstream — MU is not in that standard).

**Capex / cash.** FQ3 net capex $7.1B, adj. FCF $18.3B; FQ4 capex ~$10B. Cash + investments $30.2B, long-term debt $5.14B + current $0.58B → net cash ~$24.5B. Diluted shares ~1.15B. Dividend $0.15.

**Margin-weighted heuristic (un-audited, because HBM % is missing):** treat FQ3 as ~$25B data-center (HBM + server DRAM + SSD) at the 84.6% blended GM and ~$16B residual (mobile/PC/auto/client NAND) at a lower but still-cycle-elevated GM. The entire blended 84.6% is one-cycle-old. A 2018-style destock hits the residual first and the contracted data-center book second; LTA caps determine how much of the second bucket reprices.

## Industry Context

Sits in [[Sectors/DRAM & HBM Memory]] as the #3 DRAM name and the only liquid US listing in the HBM triopoly. Competitive tension with [[Theses/000660 - SK Hynix]] is process hygiene, not a hedge: 000660's kill is Samsung >35% of Rubin, and first-wave bits are ~70/30/0 until the Q3 meter.

| Vendor | HBM packaging | Rubin first-wave (until Q3 meter) | LTA posture | 2027 architectural bet |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SK Hynix | MR-MUF | ~70% | Caps **removed** (2 Jul) | MR-MUF through 16-Hi / first 20-Hi; Kinex dual-use |
| Samsung | TC-NCF → hybrid | ~30% | Share-recovery pricing | 8-Hi volume + hybrid 20-Hi; ~80% "golden yield" print (Aug 10) |
| Micron | TC-NCF pragmatic | **~0%** | Caps **still on** | HBM4E 1γ + TSMC base-die CY27; no IR 8-Hi |

**Qualification-gate vs allocation-gate.** #2 in [[Industry - Semiconductors]] distinguishes capacity scarcity from a gate only one vendor crosses. HBM4 qualification of all three (Huang 1 Jun) moved the gate from "who is allowed" to "who is on the board." That is a weaker moat. SKH's remaining edge is package execution (signal integrity / jitter) and the uncapped LTA book, not exclusive qualification.

**Reverse scaling.** A commodity wafer yields ~3× the bits of an HBM3E 12-Hi wafer, ~4× at HBM4. Every wafer MU shifts to HBM tightens DDR5/LPDDR — the 3:1 penalty that printed 84.6% blended GM. The same ratio is the destock amplifier when HBM wafers reverse.

**CXMT.** ~7% global DRAM revenue, DDR5/LPDDR5X MP, not an HBM4 peer. Apple testing iPhone/MacBook + HP/Acer non-US quals = fourth name in the OEM pool. Pricing-power dilution on the residual book, not a 2026 HBM event.

**Architecture responses that cut HBM bits.** Qualcomm HBC (LPDDR via TSV, 133 TB/s/card claim), Rubin PTX 9.4 3.125-bit LUT, AMD–Cerebras prefill/decode split. Higher $/byte improves the economics of using less HBM. These are watches, not 2026 base-case killers.

**Value-chain leverage.** Upstream: ASML EUV (1γ), TSMC (HBM4E base-die + CoWoS attach), [[Theses/BESI - BE Semiconductor Industries]] (hybrid-bonding tools MU will need at 16-Hi+). Downstream: [[Theses/NVDA - Nvidia]] is the allocation referee; AMD/custom-ASIC are the second book. MU has no equivalent of SKH's Namics EMC exclusivity and no HBF standard seat.

## Bull Case

Four things have to go right, and none of them is the FQ3 print (that already happened):

1. **Q3 board meter breaks 70/30/0.** MU cubes on Rubin boards at ≥10% of first-wave bits. Qualification converts to allocation. The "remainder" interpolation becomes a printed number.
2. **LTA caps come off.** MU matches SKH's 2 Jul move. Incremental DRAM ASP escapes the 13–18% protected band. Queue starts paying rent to MU, not just to SKH.
3. **L1 holds through 2027.** No 2018 destock. Contract prices flatten at a high plateau instead of reversing. CXMT stays out of Western HBM and does not reprice the residual book. 84.6–86% GM fades to a 50–60% mid-cycle floor rather than a 20–30% trough.
4. **GB300 LPDDR5X sole-source extends, or Vera SOCAMM2 becomes a second dated monopoly.** VLM stays "none 2027+" unless a 2027 BOM reprints sole-source; the bull does not need VLM, it needs the dated rent to roll one more year.

**Bull valuation:** $80B mid-cycle NI × 15× (L1 re-rate, not 25×) = $1.2T, plus a $200–400B option if the board meter and cap-removal both print — call it $1.4–1.6T / $1,200–1,400 by end-2027. That is 15–35% above $1.04T, not a double. The multiple expansion is already mostly done (ATH $1,255). The bull is "peak earnings are mid-cycle," not "the stock is cheap."

## Bear Case

The world where this loses money is not "HBM demand dies." It is **2018 with a larger numerator**.

1. **Destock.** Historian analog: 2017–18 TTID → 2019 destock. The second derivative is already in: +93–98% → +58–63% → +13–18% protected. Two more quarters of OEM inventory rebuild (the Amazon "higher memory costs" capex line flipping to "we are consuming buffer") and DRAM contracts print −20%+. Blended GM falls 84.6% → 50% in two quarters, then 30% if the residual book gaps. FQ4 $50B / ~86% is the last print of the old regime.
2. **Rent rolls while the queue stays tight.** SKH's uncapped LTAs keep collecting; MU's capped book does not. Physical scarcity persists (HBM wafers still short) and MU's incremental dollar is contractually limited. The Shanaka sentence in one line: the market can be tight and normalizing at the same time, and MU is on the normalizing side of the paper.
3. **Q3 meter confirms 0%.** First-wave 70/30/0 holds. Samsung's 8-Hi volume unlock (no MU IR 8-Hi SKU) plus ~80% golden-yield path take the incremental Rubin Ultra bits. MU's HBM4 >$1B stays a lead-customer / non-Rubin number. 000660's kill (Samsung >35%) can fire without MU ever entering the board-level share table.
4. **CXMT + commodity.** Apple qualifies. Residual DRAM dual-sources. The 76% DRAM mix that printed 84.6% GM loses the negotiation chip that made the residual expensive. HBM holds; the blend does not.
5. **Multiple.** $1.04T at ~21× LTM / ~7× peak-quarter is the 2018 setup: "cheap on next-twelve-months" into a destock. Trough NI $15–25B × 10× = $150–250B, −75% to −85%. Even a "soft landing" ($40B NI × 12× = $480B) is −50% from here.

**Bear valuation:** $250–480B / $220–420. The stock has already shown the left tail once this year ($1,255 → July washout → $924). A second destock leg does not need a new narrative; it needs the analog to complete.

## Risks

**Thesis risks (the case is wrong):**

1. **Board-meter miss.** Q3 prints ~70/30/0. Qualification-as-allocation was the consensus error this file already names; a miss confirms low, it does not surprise it. Trigger: Q3 meter.
2. **L1 was right.** No destock through 2027, caps come off, 50–60% mid-cycle GM holds. Then low was too low and the file underwrote a coverage note instead of a position. Trigger: two more +contract quarters + cap-removal.
3. **HBM % was the missing bull number.** A later disclosure shows HBM already 30%+ of revenue at 60%+ GM, making the blend more durable than the 8-K opacity implied.

**Position risks (the case is right and the stock still goes up, or the case is right and you still lose timing):**

4. **Crowding / reflexivity.** $1.04T, Strong Buy, Baker still in the top 5, July SALP unwind already cleared the weak hands once. A squeeze on destock-late positioning can re-test $1,255 before the analog completes. [G-3]
5. **000660 / Samsung headlines.** Samsung >35% is a 000660 event. MU will trade with the memory complex on that print even if MU's own first-wave share stays 0%.
6. **Taiwan tail.** CoWoS attach is in Taiwan. A kinetic event zeros HBM delivery for 12–24 months. Unhedged. Same tail as 000660.
7. **July-style leverage unwind, round two.** SALP was structure, not demand. Another 4–5× AI book can gap MU 20–40% without a destock. Position risk, not thesis risk.
