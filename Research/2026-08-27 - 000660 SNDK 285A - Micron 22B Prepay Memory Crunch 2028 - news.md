# Micron's Customers Are Putting Up $22 Billion, and Its CEO Says Data Centers Want 50% More Memory Than It Can Ship

## Thesis Delta
Consensus still argues the memory upcycle is "priced for peak" (MU at ~6× NTM in the article; 000660 trailing ~7×) because new fabs will flood 2027 — this 24 August Motley Fool recap of Sanjay Mehrotra's CNBC/Cramer interview is the **customer-money** version of that debate: **$22 billion of deposits and related commitments under the first 16 take-or-pay Strategic Customer Agreements**, 14 of which total **~$100 billion minimum contracted revenue** (mostly CY2026–late 2030), **data-center customers want ~50% more than Micron will commit**, and Mehrotra **expects 2027 tighter than 2026 with industry DRAM/NAND tight beyond 2027** because Boise wafer-one is mid-2027 / late-2028 and New York later. That **supports** [[Theses/000660 - SK Hynix]] L1 (contracted DRAM, not a 2018-style spot cycle) and Outstanding Q5's "does oversupply arrive in 2026?" with a **peer-CEO 2028** stamp — without being an SKH Rubin-share print (HIGH/LOW/CLOSE no-touch). **Supports** [[Theses/SNDK - SanDisk]] / [[Theses/285A - Kioxia]] NAND tightness and LTA/NBM floors as industry structure, not SNDK-specific. MU is not a holding; this is a three-supplier-scarcity read-through.

## Summary
Daniel Sparks (Motley Fool, 24 August 2026) uses Mehrotra's Boise-site interview to separate the Q3 print ($41.5B revenue, $28.2B NI, ~$50B Q4 guide, GM 84.6% → ~86% guided) from the **contract architecture**. Alongside the late-June fiscal Q3 report (period ended 28 May), Micron disclosed 16 SCAs across data center, consumer and auto; Mehrotra told Cramer more have been signed since. Take-or-pay: specified volumes, paid whether taken or not. Most last five years (CY2026–late 2030); auto generally three. Fourteen of the first sixteen = ~$100B minimum revenue at contracted floors; management expects actuals well above.

Largest contracts: **price ceiling** near Q2 2026 spot for existing products, **price floor** for the term. Even at the floor, Micron claims GM "well above our peak quarterly margins in any past cycle" — vs FY2023 revenue $15.5B and a $5.8B loss. Coverage is still partial: the 16 agreements are ~20% of DRAM volume and ~one-third of NAND; management wants ≥50% of revenue under SCAs once the rest are signed. Spot still dominates.

Supply: Mehrotra expects 2027 tighter than this year. Boise fab 1 wafers mid-CY2027, fab 2 late-CY2028; New York (broke ground January) later. Industry DRAM and NAND expected tight beyond CY2027. Fool's valuation aside (stock ~$967, ~22× TTM / ~6× NTM) is MU-specific colour: the market is already fading the profit run-rate. The load-bearing inference for holdings is that **customers posting $22B of cash/commitments are underwriting a shortage that lasts into the contracts' window**, not a 2026 fade.
