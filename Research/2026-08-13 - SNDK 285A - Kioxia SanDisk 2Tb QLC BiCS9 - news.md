# Kioxia + SanDisk 9th-Gen 2Tb QLC: CBA, 6-Plane, 4.8 Gb/s I/O

## Thesis Delta
Consensus prices SNDK as an HBF-option plus NAND-upcycle multiple, and 285A as a layer-count laggard. Joint IR shows 9th-generation 2Tb QLC on a *proven array* bonded to new CMOS (CBA) — density/performance without a full new-cell capex cycle — plus 6-plane architecture and 4.8 Gb/s NAND I/O (+33% vs 8th-gen 2Tb QLC). That is the capital-efficient scaling the [[Theses/SNDK - SanDisk]] and [[Theses/285A - Kioxia]] theses already claim; HBF is *not* in this PR. Mental-model triggers: Industry-Semiconductors #2 (qualification-grade vs commodity NAND) + #14 (reclassification via AI-eSSD); [G-7] ROIIC of CBA vs new-fab bit growth.

## Summary
August 12, 2026 SanDisk/Kioxia PR: 9th-generation high-performance 2Tb QLC 3D flash for AI/data-intensive apps. CBA (CMOS directly Bonded to Array) pairs an advanced CMOS wafer with a proven memory-array platform. Vs 8th-gen 2Tb QLC: higher write/read bandwidth from 6-plane architecture, better write/read power, NAND interface 4.8 Gb/s (+33%). Framed as capital-efficient NAND scaling for cloud. Kioxia CTO Hideshi Miyajima: AI expanding from generative → agent/physical, data use more diverse. No HBF, no 245TB SSD, no 1PB 2027 figures in this release — those live in other FMS/roadmap materials; do not import.
