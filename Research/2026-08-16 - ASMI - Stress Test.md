# ASMI — Stress Test (Short Case)

## Thesis Delta

Conviction (frontmatter `high`) is not supported by the thesis's own evidence. Five of eight load-bearing bull assumptions rate 🔴. The short case does not need ASMI's ALD moat to be fake — it needs three true statements the thesis underweights: (1) the "POR annuity" ASMI is credited with is the **thinnest service-revenue floor in the WFE basket**, so the vault's own SEMICAP-insulation argument applies to ASMI *least*; (2) the 1.4nm re-rate catalyst is **company-claimed, un-third-party-verified, and revenue-recognized in 2028+** (TSMC A14 HVM 2028; Samsung SF1.4 slipped to 2029) while the multiple is paid on 2026 earnings *today*; (3) the demand pillar is **already cracking in a way the thesis omits** — a Q2 2026 order miss and a stock that fell on a record print. Verdict: reassess **conviction high→medium** — which is exactly what the thesis's own Summary ("Conviction medium pending verification of 1.4nm POR slot count") and its unreconciled 05-26 / 06-01 / 07-09 Log flags already say. The stress test converges with the thesis's own bookkeeping.

## Summary

**Thesis Vulnerability Summary (single biggest reason it fails).** ASMI is priced as a de-risked structural compounder — ~39x NTM, `conviction: high` — at the exact moment its three load-bearing pillars are simultaneously unverified or deteriorating, and the re-rate that was supposed to be "gravy" is, *by the thesis's own admission, "half-done"* (NTM 39x "sits at the top of that band"). The multiple-expansion leg is largely spent; the downside is a 35%+ de-rate (39x→25x, the deposition-tool through-cycle average) onto a now-*higher* €23.1 EPS base that a 2027 hyperscaler-capex air-pocket directly threatens. The April EPS mark-up €16→€23.1 did not de-risk the position — it raised the bar the 2027 print must clear while the multiple compressed on the *upgrade*, not a POR downgrade. A quality franchise can still be a bad *position* when the mispricing it was bought for has closed.

**External evidence (performed).** Q2 2026 (reported 28 Jul): record €1,003M revenue, record FCF, record margins — **stock fell**. Multiple outlets: "Stock Slides Despite Record Cash Flow And Margins." The clean bear signal is not the revenue (a beat) but the **order miss** and management's "visibility in the China market remains low." Post-print broker action split: Berenberg (€1,140), JPMorgan (€1,174), Barclays (€1,200) raised PTs (Buy/OW); other desks cut on a projected 2026 WFE decline (DRAM equipment −16%) and China weakness. Short interest is negligible (~0.0% of float) — no squeeze setup, and no crowded-short cover to fear: this is a pure fundamental/valuation short into broad long positioning. MATCH Act cleared the House Foreign Affairs Committee 22 Apr 2026 and — unprecedented vs prior BIS rulemakings — extends controls to **post-sale servicing of the installed base** (calibration, software, spares, field support), with a 150-day allied-alignment clock and statute-locking. The servicing-ban tail the thesis flags is advancing, not receding, and ASMI's China mix is >30% (FY25), not the ~20% the sector note still carries.

## Evidence

### Evidence Against (tagged `[vault-blind-spot]` / `[consensus]` — lead with blind spots)

1. **`[vault-blind-spot]` The "annuity" is equipment revenue, not a service annuity — ASMI has the least downside floor in the basket.** The thesis's #1 non-consensus insight reframes ASMI from "cyclical equipment vendor" to "structurally compounding annuity … closer to EUV-tool economics ASML enjoys." But the sector-note Key Metrics table lists ASMI **Service Revenue % = "Low"**, vs KLAC ~30%, LRCX ~30% (CSBG $7.2B), AMAT ~25% (AGS). The vault's own AI-Bubble insulation thesis ([[AI Bubble Risk and Semiconductor Valuations]] 2026-05-11) says the SEMICAP basket only draws down ~25% (not ~50%) in an AI-capex pause *because* "30-40% of revenue is service annuity that does not depend on new tool sales." That mechanism **applies to ASMI least** — its POR "annuity" is *node-lifecycle equipment* revenue (new tools shipped as a fab ramps), which is exactly the line that gets cut when capex slows. Sector note §Macro shifts is explicit: in the China airpocket "tool sales are the primary at-risk line." ASMI is ~all tool sales. The thesis credits ASMI with an insulation it structurally does not have.

2. **`[vault-blind-spot]` The thesis narrates "core uncontested" while its own LOW trigger names the layer being probed right now.** Bull-case pillar and Insight #4 assert Lam ALTUS Halo / AMAT Trillium "attack adjacencies, not the core." But the thesis's own → LOW trigger fires if either "wins POR at TSMC 1.4nm for any HKMG-critical layer (**work-function metal, dipole, or capping**)," and the thesis's own Mental Models section records that AMAT's Endura Trillium (launched 2026-04-08) "targets WFM/*dipole*" and "dipole is now the named contested layer to watch." The body says the moat is safe; the trigger and the model-log say the exact moat layer is under active assault. That gap is the thesis defending a conclusion its own risk apparatus contradicts.

3. **`[vault-blind-spot]` ASMI trades at a premium to the sector note's own "best risk/reward" name, unaddressed.** The Semiconductor Capital Equipment sector note (§Investor heuristics, Insight #2) calls AMAT "the most mispriced large-cap in semicap … Best risk/reward in the sector," at ~31x vs ASMI's ~39x — a ~15-turn discount to the sector's *preferred* WFE name. AMAT is also **#1 in epi** (ASMI is #2), i.e. it leads ASMI's own advertised "second engine," and is the vehicle probing ASMI's ALD adjacencies (Trillium, Mo interconnect). The thesis holds ASMI at HIGH conviction without ever answering why to own the premium name over the sector's own top pick that is simultaneously attacking two of ASMI's growth vectors.

4. **`[vault-blind-spot]` The thesis omits the Q2 2026 order miss and the LOW trigger monitors the wrong variable.** The 08-15 metrics-pass and the thesis body narrate the €1,003M revenue beat and the €1,100M Q3 guide, but not the **order miss** that made the stock fall on a record print. Separately, the → LOW trigger reads "consolidated 2027 hyperscaler capex … declines >10% YoY." If datacenter *power* (not compute) becomes the binding 2027–2029 constraint — the thesis of the linked [[Sectors/Natural Gas Fuel Cells]] note, [[Macro & Technology/800VDC Adoption]], and the BTM-generation research — hyperscaler capex can stay *nominally flat-to-up* while its **mix shifts away from WFE** toward power/cooling/transformers/fuel cells (Bloom ~€4B rev, Eaton 228 GW backlog, Korean transformer trio ₩32T). ASMI's revenue can degrade as WFE's *share* of AI capex falls without the total-capex trigger ever firing. The thesis has **no power/energy dependency** in its risk map at all.

5. **`[consensus]` 1.4nm POR is unverified and its revenue is 2028+, yet 39x is paid on 2026 earnings.** The single highest-impact datapoint (Outstanding Q1) — incremental (not defended) 1.4nm POR slots — is by the thesis's own words "company-claimed, not third-party verified" (+1 Mo layer, SAM $450–500M). External: TSMC A14 mass production is **2H 2028**; Samsung SF1.4 slipped to **2029**. The catalyst that justifies the re-rate is 2–3 years of HVM away; today's 39x rests on N2, not 1.4nm. Known, but the *duration mismatch* is underweighted.

6. **`[consensus]` China / MATCH Act servicing-ban tail is live, advancing, and larger than modeled.** >30% China (FY25) vs the sector note's stale ~20%; MATCH Act cleared committee, extends to servicing the installed base, statute-locking. A retroactive servicing ban is the thesis's own → CLOSE condition (installed-base China impairment >8% of revenue). Well-flagged, but the exposure is worse than the sector note carries.

7. **`[consensus]` Demand is a cluster-wide second-derivative and it is cracking.** ASMI is a 1.5–2x-beta second derivative on hyperscaler→TSMC capex. Cluster/adjacency peers show parallel stress: [[Research/2026-04-23 - NVDA - Stress Test]] (6/10 🔴), [[Research/2026-04-19 - TSM - Stress Test]] (8/9 🔴), TSM Q2 "beat-and-raise the market refused to pay for" (−5% on a record print = "AI capex fatigue"), and [[AI Bubble Risk and Semiconductor Valuations]]'s dated **Q3-2026 hyperscaler capex/cash-flow crossover** (Epoch AI). ASMI's Q2 order miss + stock-fell-on-record is the same signal reaching ASMI directly.

## Contradiction Check

**Internal contradictions found (Phase 2 scan):**
- **Conviction vs evidence.** `conviction: high` while the Summary ends "Conviction medium pending verification of 1.4nm POR slot count," the 07-09 Log explicitly flags "frontmatter high vs 05-26/06-01 entries 'medium' for /status reconciliation," and the #1 Outstanding Question is unresolved. High conviction with the single most important datapoint unverified is the textbook red flag.
- **Bull Case vs Bear Case (epi).** Bull lists CFET/epi as a compounding "second engine"; Bear Case says CFET is "pushed to ~2032+, deferring the epi franchise re-rating beyond the relevant investment window." The thesis argues both that epi is a near-term engine and that it isn't.
- **"Core uncontested" vs the → LOW trigger.** Covered in Evidence Against #2 — the body's competitive claim contradicts the thesis's own trigger and Mental Models log.
- **Key Metrics internal consistency.** ~39x NTM + FCF Yield ~1.6% + Revenue Growth +8.2% LFY (Street +29% 2026E is a forward mark, not realized): a high multiple resting on a *guided acceleration* the Q2 order miss puts in question. The bar was raised (EPS €16→€23.1) as the multiple compressed — the position now carries earnings risk it did not at €16 EPS.

**Cluster context (Phase 2.4 — idiosyncratic first, per confirmation-bias mitigation).** ASMI is in **no formal `_graph.md` cluster**; its adjacency peers are AIXA, BESI, NVDA, TSM. Peer stress is demand-side and Taiwan-tail (NVDA 6🔴, TSM 8🔴), i.e. **cluster-wide**. Classification: ASMI's *largest* failure modes (AI-capex deceleration, Taiwan) are **cluster-wide** — ASMI falls with the complex, not alone. The **idiosyncratic** failure modes that differentiate ASMI's downside are the ones to lead on: thin service annuity (#1), dipole POR contest (#2), premium to AMAT (#3), the omitted order miss + mis-specified LOW trigger (#4). Primary stress remains target-thesis-driven; peers only confirm the demand pillar is fragile across the book.

**Verdict.** The thesis is not broken — the ALD POR moat is real and the franchise is high-quality. But **conviction: high is not supported.** The mispricing ASMI was bought for (POR annuity mis-read as cyclical) has largely closed into 39x; the insulation it is credited with is the basket's thinnest; the catalyst is unverified and 2028+; and demand is showing the first crack the thesis omits. Conviction should be **medium**, pending third-party 1.4nm POR confirmation — the exact state the thesis's own Summary and Log already record but the frontmatter never reconciled.
