## Thesis Delta

Tom's Hardware (Sedaily/Korea Herald): SK hynix resumed Dalian/Solidigm investment after a four-year pause. Weighing NASDAQ listing of a Solidigm minority stake (unofficial up to $7B) while retaining control — Aug 5 filing only said “considering various ways,” IPO not confirmed. Solidigm ~quarter of DC SSD market; 245TB-class SSDs into 2027.
