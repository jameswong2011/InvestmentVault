## Summary
Crypto Briefing reported on August 26, 2026 that Kioxia plans a new manufacturing fab in Iwate with a ¥1T investment. Kioxia Holdings is "pouring more than ¥1 trillion" into a new manufacturing building at Kitakami Plant, Iwate, aimed at next-generation NAND for AI/cloud SSDs. Kitakami already has Fab1 (2020) and Fab2 (September 2025, 332-layer BiCS FLASH). Sample shipments of 332-layer product began 3 July 2026; full-scale production waits on market-condition evaluation. CEO Hiroo Ota calls Kitakami strategically ideal. The ¥1T building is presented as extending the campus to a third building.

Spending blueprint restates Investor Day maths: average annual capex ~¥470 billion FY2026–FY2028, +66% from FY2025. Japanese government approved ~¥243 billion of subsidies in 2024 for advanced 3D NAND expansion. The article closes on 3D NAND as the SSD media for AI data centers versus HDD, and on Kioxia's December 2024 TSE listing after the 2018 Toshiba sale. No node beyond "next-generation," no wafer-start add, no JV vs wholly-owned split, and no timeline to first wafer in the third building.
