# Market Memo: A Tale of Two Heroes

## Thesis Delta

Consensus priced the late-July AI-semiconductor air-pocket as a demand/thesis break (HBM miss, optics fade, "memory bear") → Irrational Analysis reconstructs it as Situational Awareness LP (SALP / Leopold Aschenbrenner) running **4–5× gross, non-factor-neutral** long-AI / short-anti-AI getting margin-called, then Citadel-brokered book sale ("seppuku"), with street-known concentrated longs in [[Theses/285A - Kioxia]] and [[Theses/000660 - SK Hynix]] amplifying the cascade (Bloom print faded the moment SKH convulsed). [G-13] the mispriced tape variable is **forced-seller plumbing**, not HBM bits or ad-ROIIC; [G-4] "bubble-pop violence with more bubble to go"; Semis #18: do not read a positioning air-pocket as a structural demand roll. IA still ultra-core [[Theses/LITE - Lumentum]] / [[Theses/INTC - Intel]] / [[Theses/TSEM - Tower Semiconductor]]; long-only prefers Samsung DRAM over SKH on foundry+design **HBM base-die co-design** — a hypothesis that cuts against SKH winner-take-most, not a [[Theses/MU - Micron Technology]] promotion.

## Summary

The memo is a leverage-and-plumbing reconstruction of two weeks of AI-complex tape, not a product or TAM update. Part 1 is the 2021 Archegos analog: Bill Hwang's family office used **total return swaps** so prime brokers (not the client) held the stock, stacked the same handful of correlated names (legacy media + Chinese tech) across ~7 banks that never compared notes, then blew up when ViacomCBS announced a $3B follow-on on 23 March 2021. VIAC dumped, Discovery dumped on correlation, Archegos tried to sell itself for ~24 hours, then banks block-traded the book. Credit Suisse ate a $4.7B hit plus another ~$700M on leftover Discovery blocks. Hwang was convicted 20 November 2024. IA's operational lesson is not "don't lever" — it is that prime-broker due diligence has no analog to a mortgage credit bureau: one inter-bank phone call would have shown the same concentrated TRS book at multiple primes.

Part 2 maps that template onto SALP. IA's typology (25+ contacts) is that **allowable leverage scales with net, gross, and what you own**. Pod shops (Citadel, Point72, Balyasny, Millennium) run 5–10× at **net 0%** under strict factor rules — a 5% single-day book draw gets the PM walked out. A regular hedge fund might sit at ~3×, net ±15%. A conservative family office might sit at 1.5×, net 50%. SALP ran **4–5× gross at unknown net** with shorts that were **not close to factor-neutral**: long AI winners (Intel, SK Hynix named), short AI losers (Adobe / traditional SaaS named as the toy). Those shorts were anti-correlated with the longs, so they added the same AI-factor bet rather than hedging it. Pod leverage + family-office risk rules. Street social split on the 30+ contacts: ~80% hostile to SALP, ~20% still "no margin call" as of 18:00 on Wednesday 29 July; IA's contemporaneous read was margin-called but only −20–30% and still alive. Four hours later the FT piece landed and that read died.

The unwind mechanism is labeled hedge-fund seppuku: primes (Goldman and others) are the short sword via margin calls; Ken Griffin / Citadel buys the book at a steep discount and ends the public liquidation. A SALP fund-letter snippet is shown (image, not transcribed). SMH was +50.1% YTD; IA's trading book (typically 150–190% gross and net, shorts "for fun") was +56.8% YTD — SALP still ahead YTD before the Citadel cut. Liquid-capital read: thin. WSJ (*Situational Awareness Down 67% in July in AI Stock Rout*) reports SALP considered selling Anthropic shares for ~$3B cash and then reversed. IA treats a contemplated private-share sale as the tell that the public book needed cash and that LPs blocked the sale.

Chronology, reconstructed from those 30+ contacts: weak SALP-trouble / LP-capital rumors around Wednesday 22 July; weekend chatter that Kioxia would gap Monday if Leopold was selling; Monday 27 July Kioxia "violently" dumped — the entire hedge-fund community already treated Kioxia and SK Hynix as massive SALP longs (shares vs TRS vs other leverage **unknown** to anyone but SALP and the primes). Tuesday 28 July [[Theses/BE - Bloom Energy]] printed a blowout; the pop faded after hours the moment SK Hynix "convulsed" on a miss. IA day-traded Bloom for ~$40K and sold when SKH weakened, inferring SALP would keep selling Bloom. Wednesday 29 July: daytime "orderly unwind" claims, evening chatter worse, FT ~21:00 — IA marks SALP as probably dead. Twelve hours later CNBC's Faber (30 July) reports steep AI losses; WSJ corroborates, disputing only the "all public positions" clause. Tape showed the book sale in real time as Faber published. Thursday was the violent green once the street decided self-immolation had finished; Friday still up, then faded on Iran-war fears (IA de-levered after hours). Market-state claim in one line: last week's move was **bubble-pop-level violence with more bubble left**.

Positioning after the clear: trading book being rebuilt, "very fluid." Ultra-core unchanged: Lumentum, Intel, Tower. Core: Bloom and Semtech (sizing uncertain). AXTI is a "giga-long" — it cracked one to two months earlier because China banned total return swaps and Chinese / Hong Kong institutions became "mini-Leopolds." [[Theses/AEHR - Aehr Test Systems]] flagged as high-beta after a smash-hit print/guide, "violent." Ciena and Nokia both liked on long-haul optics; IA cannot pick winner or size. [[Theses/AAOI - Applied Optoelectronics]] has "torque" as a USA-based transceiver name, same violence problem as AEHR. One share of [[Theses/CBRS - Cerebras Systems]] for an activist campaign, deferred to a later post. Long-only (day-job FCF): happy with the book except Samsung — wants to buy a lot more. Leopold preferred SK Hynix; IA prefers Samsung DRAM on **internal foundry + design team co-designing the HBM base die better than SK Hynix**. [[Theses/MU - Micron Technology]] is not named as a long, a hedge, or a first-wave HBM winner. [[Theses/META - Meta]] is not named at all; the complex-wide claim that still hits Meta is that the air-pocket was positioning, not a hyperscaler-capex demand break.

## Framework / Mental Model

IA does not score a TAM. It names a **three-attribute leverage typology**, an **Archegos TRS multi-prime analog**, and a **Citadel seppuku** end-state.

**Allowable-leverage triad** (toy capital $10M):

| Attribute | Low-risk illustration | High-risk illustration | SALP (IA, 25+ sources) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net exposure | Long $10M / short $10M = 0% | Long $20M / short $0 = 200% | Unknown; IA says net "does not matter" if shorts are anti-correlated |
| Gross exposure | Long $10M / short $10M = 200% | Long $20M / short $10M = 300% | **4–5×** |
| What you own | "Boring" staples (KO/PEP pair) | Volatile, correlated, high-beta | Long AI winners (INTC, SKH named) / short AI losers (Adobe/SaaS named) |

Worked pair-trade examples in the source: long $100M AMD / short $100M NVDA is **not** factor-neutral (CPU mix, FPGAs, NVDA networking). Long $1B KO / short $1B PEP is the textbook factor-neutral pair a risk department will lever. SALP did the AMD/NVDA version at the factor level (AI vs anti-AI), then levered it like a pod.

**Institutional-rule map** (IA "directional" numbers, not a survey):

| Vehicle | Leverage | Net | Risk rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multi-manager pod | 5–10× | 0%, factor-constrained | ~5% single-day book draw → fired |
| Regular hedge fund | ~3× | ±15% | Softer |
| Conservative family office | ~1.5× | ~50% | Owner capital |
| SALP | 4–5× gross | Unknown | Family-office risk + pod leverage |

**Archegos analog (mechanism, not moral):** TRS = economic exposure without owning the shares; prime owns the stock; 13F/position opacity; multi-prime stacking of the **same correlated names**; no inter-bank credit bureau. Archegos trigger was a $3B VIAC follow-on (management selling richness). SALP trigger was an AI-complex drawdown into a non-neutral 4–5× book; instrument mix (stock vs TRS vs "other leveraged thing") is explicitly unknown.

**Seppuku / book-sale end-state:** primes inflict the mortal wound (margin calls). Citadel buys the book at a discount and stops the public cascade. Thursday violent green is the observable that the sale finished. Contemplated Anthropic ~$3B sale-then-reverse is the liquid-capital diagnostic: you only sell the sacred private position to defend the public book.

**Mini-Leopold extension:** a China TRS ban forced HK/China institutions to unwind the same way, earlier, in AXTI — same plumbing, different geography.
