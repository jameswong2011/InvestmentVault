# Cloudflare prices $2.5B 0% convertible notes due 2031

## Thesis Delta
Consensus already treats [[Theses/NET - Cloudflare]] as a fortress-balance-sheet, high-multiple platform story — Key Metrics still print Cash $4.1B and a reverse-DCF that required 25–30% revenue CAGR plus FCF-margin doubling at the ~$268 / ~$95B / ~30x trailing sales snapshot (2026-07-12 numbers refresh; initiation was ~$183 / ~$65B) — and the street/thesis already know NET runs a convert stack and a five-deal-in-twelve-months M&A pace (Outerbase, Arroyo, Human Native, Astro, Replicate), with the SASE deep-dive naming Forcepoint (~$1–1.5B est.) or Netskope ($5–8B post-IPO) as the only realistic DLP/CASB catch-up shortcuts. This 10–13 August 2026 8-K is not a product, Act IV, SASE, or bookings print: it is a Rule 144A $2.50 billion 0% Convertible Senior Notes due 15 August 2031 issue at an initial conversion rate of 2.0123 Class A shares per $1,000 (implied conversion price ~$496.94, a 60% premium to the $310.59 NYSE last sale on 10 August 2026), $2,462.3 million net proceeds including the full $325.0 million Goldman Sachs / Morgan Stanley greenshoe, $259.5 million of capped calls struck at $496.9438 and capped at $854.1225 (175% premium), remaining proceeds earmarked for general corporate purposes including working capital, capital expenditures, repayment of outstanding indebtedness, and potential acquisitions and strategic transactions, and a concurrent Second Amendment to the 17 May 2024 Citibank revolving credit agreement that sets the cash/AFS deduction cap in the consolidated total net leverage ratio at $2.0 billion. The Notes rank equal to the already-outstanding $1.125 billion 0% converts due 2026 and $2.00 billion 0% converts due 2030. NET has **no formal Conviction Triggers section** (gap flagged in the thesis Mental Models pass and in [[Research/2026-07-10 - NET - Stress Test]]). Informal stress-test kill switches — Act IV revenue still undisclosed by the FY2027 guide while P/S >25x; DBNR/NGR <115%; 2027 Gartner SSE still niche-player while Zscaler AI-security ARR clears ~$750M; a fourth multi-hour global outage or Google deprioritization of Cloudflare-fronted sites — are **not touched**. Hypothesis: this is cheap (0% coupon, no accretion) five-year dry powder and a 2026-convert refinance option at a 60% conversion premium, not a thesis-moving operating print; the $2.0 billion leverage-ratio cash cap means most of the new cash does **not** reduce covenant leverage, and the $310.59 print is a further multiple-regime re-rating versus the thesis’s ~$268 / initiation ~$183 levels.

## Summary
On 10 August 2026 Cloudflare entered a purchase agreement with Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC, as representatives of the several initial purchasers, to issue and sell $2.175 billion aggregate principal amount of 0% Convertible Senior Notes due 2031, plus a $325.0 million option exercisable for settlement within a 13-day period beginning on, and including, the first day the Notes are issued. The Initial Purchasers exercised that option in full on 11 August 2026. The Notes were issued on 13 August 2026 pursuant to an indenture dated 13 August 2026 between the Company and U.S. Bank Trust Company, National Association, as trustee, in a Rule 144A private offering to persons reasonably believed to be qualified institutional buyers (Company sale to Initial Purchasers under Securities Act Section 4(a)(2); resale under Rule 144A). Neither the Notes nor any Class A shares issuable on conversion have been registered; any conversion shares are anticipated to be issued under Section 3(a)(9) because no commission or other remuneration is expected to be paid in connection with conversion.

The Notes will not bear regular interest and the principal amount will not accrete. They may bear special interest under specified circumstances relating to the Company’s failure to comply with its reporting obligations under the Indenture or if the Notes are not freely tradable as required by the Indenture. Maturity is 15 August 2031, unless earlier redeemed, repurchased, or converted. They are senior unsecured obligations. StockTitan’s Rhea-AI overlay tagged the filing High / Neutral and listed the same headline figures.

The initial conversion rate is 2.0123 shares of Class A common stock per $1,000 principal amount, equivalent to an initial conversion price of approximately $496.94 per share — a premium of approximately 60% to the last reported sale price of $310.59 per share of Class A common stock on the NYSE on 10 August 2026. The conversion rate is subject to adjustment under the Indenture. Following a make-whole fundamental change, or if the Company issues a notice of redemption, the Company will in certain circumstances increase the conversion rate by a number of additional shares for a holder that elects to convert in connection with that make-whole fundamental change or that elects to convert Notes that are called (or deemed called) during the related redemption period.

The Notes will be convertible at the option of the holders at any time prior to the close of business on the business day immediately preceding 15 May 2031 only under specified circumstances: (1) during any calendar quarter commencing after the calendar quarter ending 31 December 2026 (and only during such calendar quarter), if the last reported sale price of the Class A Common Stock for at least 20 trading days (whether or not consecutive) during a period of 30 consecutive trading days ending on, and including, the last trading day of the immediately preceding calendar quarter is greater than or equal to 130% of the conversion price on each applicable trading day; (2) during the five business day period after any five consecutive trading day period (the “measurement period”) in which the trading price per $1,000 principal amount of Notes for each trading day of the measurement period was less than 98% of the product of the last reported sale price of the Class A Common Stock and the conversion rate on each such trading day; (3) if the Company calls the Notes for redemption, at any time prior to the close of business on the second scheduled trading day immediately preceding the redemption date, but only with respect to the Notes called (or deemed called) for redemption; and (4) upon the occurrence of specified corporate events. On or after 15 May 2031, until the close of business on the second scheduled trading day immediately preceding the maturity date, holders may convert all or any portion of their Notes, in multiples of $1,000 principal amount, regardless of those circumstances. Upon conversion, the Company may satisfy its conversion obligation by paying and/or delivering cash, shares of Class A Common Stock, or a combination of cash and shares, at the Company’s election.

Except in the case of a cleanup redemption, the Company may not redeem the Notes prior to 20 August 2029. The Company may redeem for cash all or any portion of the Notes (subject to the partial redemption limitation), at its option, on or after 20 August 2029, if the last reported sale price of the Class A Common Stock has been at least 130% of the conversion price then in effect for at least 20 trading days (whether or not consecutive) during any 30 consecutive trading day period (including the last trading day of such period) ending on, and including, the trading day immediately preceding the date on which the Company provides notice of redemption. In addition, subject to certain conditions, the Company may redeem for cash all, but not less than all, of the Notes at any time if the aggregate principal amount of the Notes that remains outstanding is less than $200.0 million (a “cleanup redemption”). The redemption price for any optional redemption or cleanup redemption is 100% of the principal amount of the Notes to be redeemed, plus any accrued and unpaid special interest to, but excluding, the redemption date. If the Company elects to redeem fewer than all of the outstanding Notes, at least $100.0 million aggregate principal amount of Notes must be outstanding and not subject to redemption as of the relevant redemption date (the “partial redemption limitation”). No sinking fund is provided.

Upon a fundamental change (as defined in the Indenture) prior to maturity, subject to certain conditions, holders may require the Company to repurchase all or a portion of the Notes for cash at 100% of the principal amount to be repurchased, plus any accrued and unpaid special interest to, but excluding, the fundamental change repurchase date.

The Notes rank senior in right of payment to any indebtedness expressly subordinated to the Notes; equal in right of payment to any unsecured indebtedness that is not so subordinated, including the Company’s $1.125 billion aggregate principal amount of 0% convertible senior notes due 2026 and $2.00 billion aggregate principal amount of 0% convertible senior notes due 2030; effectively junior in right of payment to any secured indebtedness to the extent of the value of the assets securing such indebtedness, including any borrowings under the Credit Agreement; and structurally junior to all indebtedness and other liabilities of current or future subsidiaries (including trade payables).

Events of default that may result in acceleration: (1) default in any payment of special interest, if any, when due, continuing 30 days; (2) default in payment of principal when due at stated maturity, upon redemption, upon any required repurchase, upon declaration of acceleration, or otherwise; (3) failure to comply with the conversion obligation upon exercise of a holder’s conversion right, continuing five business days; (4) failure to give a fundamental change notice when due, continuing five business days, or notice of a specified corporate event when due, continuing two business days; (5) failure to comply with Indenture obligations with respect to consolidation, merger, purchase, conveyance, transfer, and lease of assets; (6) failure for 60 days after written notice from the trustee or holders of at least 25% in aggregate principal amount of Notes then outstanding to comply with any other agreement in the Notes or the Indenture; (7) default by the Company or any significant subsidiary with respect to any mortgage, agreement, or other instrument under which there may be outstanding, or by which there may be secured or evidenced, any indebtedness for money borrowed in excess of $200.0 million (or its foreign currency equivalent) in the aggregate, whether now existing or hereafter created, (i) resulting in such indebtedness becoming or being declared due and payable prior to its stated maturity or (ii) constituting a failure to pay the principal of any such debt when due and payable at its stated maturity, upon required repurchase, upon declaration of acceleration or otherwise, in each case after any applicable grace period, if such acceleration shall not have been rescinded or annulled or such failure to pay or default shall not have been cured or waived, or such indebtedness shall not have been paid or discharged, within 30 days after written notice to the Company by the trustee or to the Company and the trustee by holders of at least 25% in aggregate principal amount of Notes then outstanding; and (8) certain events of bankruptcy, insolvency, or reorganization of the Company or any significant subsidiary. If an event of default other than clause (8) with respect to the Company occurs and is continuing, the trustee by notice to the Company, or holders of at least 25% in aggregate principal amount of the outstanding Notes by notice to the Company and the trustee, may declare 100% of the principal of and accrued and unpaid special interest, if any, on all Notes then outstanding to be due and payable. If an event of default described in clause (8) with respect to the Company occurs, 100% of the principal of and any accrued and unpaid special interest on the Notes then outstanding will automatically become due and payable.

Net proceeds were approximately $2,462.3 million after deducting the Initial Purchasers’ discounts and commissions and the Company’s estimated offering expenses, including the full greenshoe. The 11 August 2026 pricing press release (Exhibit 99.2) had guided approximately $2.14 billion net proceeds assuming no exercise of the $325.0 million option. The Company used $259.5 million of the net proceeds to pay the cost of the Capped Call Transactions. The 11 August pricing release had guided approximately $225.8 million of net proceeds for the base (pre-greenshoe) capped calls. Remaining proceeds are intended for general corporate purposes, which may include working capital, capital expenditures, repayment of outstanding indebtedness, and potential acquisitions and strategic transactions.

On 10 August 2026, in connection with pricing, the Company entered into privately negotiated Base Capped Call Transactions with certain financial institutions (the “Option Counterparties”). On 11 August 2026, in connection with the greenshoe, it entered into Additional Capped Call Transactions with each of the Option Counterparties. The Capped Call Transactions have an initial strike price of $496.9438 per share, subject to adjustments, corresponding to the initial conversion price. They will cover, subject to customary anti-dilution adjustments, the number of Class A shares that initially underlie the Notes. They are expected generally to offset potential dilution to the Class A Common Stock upon any conversion of Notes and/or reduce any cash payments the Company would be required to make in excess of the principal amount of such converted Notes, with such offset and/or reduction subject to a cap based on the cap price. If the market price per share, as measured under the capped-call terms, exceeds the cap price, there would nevertheless be dilution and/or there would not be a reduction of such potential cash payments to the extent the market price exceeds the cap. The cap price is initially $854.1225 per share — a 175% premium over the $310.59 last reported sale on 10 August 2026 — and is subject to certain adjustments. The 10 August intention press release (Exhibit 99.1) had said the cap would initially represent a premium of at least 150%. The Capped Call Transactions are separate transactions with the Option Counterparties, are not part of the terms of the Notes, and do not affect any holder’s rights under the Notes or the Indenture; holders have no rights with respect to the capped calls.

The Company has been advised that, in connection with establishing their initial hedges, the option counterparties or their respective affiliates expect to purchase Class A shares and/or enter into various derivative transactions with respect to the Class A Common Stock concurrently with or shortly after pricing, which could increase (or reduce the size of any decrease in) the market price of the stock or the notes at that time. They may also modify hedge positions by entering into or unwinding various derivatives and/or purchasing or selling Class A shares or other Company securities in secondary-market transactions following pricing and prior to maturity (and are likely to do so following any early conversion, repurchase, or redemption, to the extent the Company unwinds a corresponding portion of the capped calls, or if the Company otherwise unwinds all or a portion of the capped calls, and during the observation period for conversion on or after 15 May 2031). That activity could cause or avoid an increase or a decrease in the stock or note price, which could affect the ability of noteholders to convert and, to the extent it occurs following conversion or during any observation period, the number of shares, if any, and value of the consideration received on conversion.

On 10 August 2026 the Company also entered into a Second Amendment to its Revolving Credit and Guaranty Agreement dated 17 May 2024 (as amended by a First Amendment dated 11 June 2025) with Citibank, N.A. as administrative agent and collateral agent, the other loan parties, and the lenders. The Amendment sets the cap on unrestricted cash, cash equivalents, and available-for-sale securities deducted from consolidated funded indebtedness in the calculation of the consolidated total net leverage ratio for certain purposes under the Credit Agreement at $2.0 billion. Additional details of the Credit Agreement were previously disclosed in the Company’s 8-K filed 21 May 2024. Certain of the Lenders and/or their affiliates have engaged in, and may in the future engage in, commercial banking, investment banking, and other banking and/or financial services with the Company or its affiliates, and have received or may receive customary fees and commissions.

The Purchase Agreement includes customary representations, warranties, and covenants by the Company and customary closing conditions. The Company agreed to indemnify the Initial Purchasers against certain liabilities.
