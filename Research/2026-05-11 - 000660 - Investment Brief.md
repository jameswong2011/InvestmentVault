# 000660 — SK Hynix

*DRAM & HBM Memory · Draft · Conviction: Medium · 2026-05-11*

## The Pitch

The market is debating whether SK Hynix's HBM monopoly persists (sell-side $1.43M target extrapolates 57% share) or erodes (Gemini canvas: 62%→45% by 2030, "Incumbent Erosion"). Both frames miss the trade. HBM4's foundry-fabricated logic base die — new this generation — converts a 70% Nvidia Rubin allocation into a multi-GPU-cycle lock-in (Rubin → Rubin Ultra → Feynman, H2 2026 → 2028), anchoring $70–120B 2026 operating profit even as share regresses. At 8.1x EV/EBITDA against a 72% Q1 op margin (memory-industry record), you pay cyclical-trough multiples for a 3-year monopoly cash machine plus two unpriced asymmetric calls.

## Why Now

- **Q1 2026 just printed the highest op margin in memory-industry history** (2026-04-23): $37.9B revenue, 72% op margin, $27.1B op profit — ~60% of LTM op profit booked in one quarter
- **Q3 2026 = central catalyst**: Nvidia Rubin initial-ship HBM allocation discloses the share split that gates the bull/bear debate; kill trigger is Samsung >35%
- **H2 2026 = HBM5 insurance test**: BESI Kinex hybrid-bonding qualification at SK Hynix; pass = HBM5 lead preserved, fail = Samsung leap-frogs at 2028 transition

## The Non-Consensus Edge

Foundry logic base dies (TSMC 3nm for SK Hynix HBM4, Samsung Foundry 2nm internal for Samsung HBM4, replacing legacy memory-process buffer dies) convert per-cycle GPU memory share into multi-cycle architectural lock-in. Once Nvidia/AMD specify a base-die process for Rubin, porting to a different supplier requires full re-validation — supplier choice is structural, not preferential. Custom HBM (HBM4 + ASIC base die) is forecast at ~33% of total HBM market in 2026, growing 82% YoY. The Q3 2026 Rubin allocation isn't a single-cycle data point — it's a 3-cycle commitment that locks H2 2026 → 2028 cash flows. Samsung's Vera Rubin "best scores" qualification compresses SK Hynix sole-source premium but doesn't open Samsung a path to >35% allocation absent another base-die transition. Consensus models the share as cyclical because it priced HBM3-era dynamics where supplier swaps were near-frictionless. HBM4 is not HBM3.

## What Kills It

Samsung HBM4 captures >35% of Nvidia Rubin allocation in the first two shipping quarters (Q3–Q4 2026). Verifiable via Nvidia disclosure + Samsung's own Q3 HBM revenue line. That single data point validates Gemini's Incumbent Erosion path one cycle ahead of schedule and invalidates 3-cycle lock-in. Probability per vault: 25–30% — Samsung passed Vera Rubin qualification with "best scores" but 1c DRAM yield is at 50% pilot vs 70% target; allocation depends on yield ramp through H2 2026.

## Conviction & Sizing

Medium — supported by Q1 2026 print + base-die lock-in + two unpriced options (HBF $5–10B TAM with SanDisk co-developed at OCP; Solidigm 2027 IPO at Kioxia-comparable 8–10x EBITDA against $9B 2021 cost basis = $6–15B hidden value crystallization). Falsifiable kill trigger gates upside until Q3 2026 Rubin disclosure. → HIGH if Rubin allocation ≥60% SK Hynix AND BESI Kinex passes 16-Hi by Q4 2026. → CLOSE if Samsung HBM5 hybrid-bonding crosses 70% yield at 16-Hi by mid-2027 AND CXMT qualifies HBM2e/HBM3 at a hyperscaler by end-2028.
