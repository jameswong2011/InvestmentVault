# Gemini is Cooked but GCP is Cooking

## Thesis Delta
Consensus still prices Alphabet as an AI “Big 3” frontier lab whose cloud is a second-order option on Gemini → SemiAnalysis (5 Aug 2026 leadership overhaul) implies **DeepMind has exited frontier contention** (SOTA odds “dropped to zero”) while **Thomas Kurian’s GCP won the internal compute war**, financializing TPUs into third-party IaaS/TaaS and gross-basis system sales. No `[[Theses/GOOGL]]` exists — price the name as a high-growth infra vendor (2027 GCP EPS ~+$3; sell-side 2027 growth ~64% vs SA mid-100s [G-13]) rather than AGI duration, with [G-8] allocation and [G-5] talent-gravity as hypotheses that the overhaul just printed.

## Summary
The 5 August 2026 DeepMind overhaul is SemiAnalysis’s confirmation print, not the start of the story. Demis Hassabis is off day-to-day operations; Jeff Dean (Chief Scientist, Gemini co-lead, TPU program founder, “undisputed GOAT of Google engineering”) leaves with Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals to found Discovery Loop; Koray Kavukcuoglu, the last remaining Gemini co-lead and former DeepMind CTO, replaces Demis. SA’s verdict: “DeepMind is no longer a frontier lab.” Google will keep shipping models; the probability of retaking SOTA is treated as zero. The winner of the announcement is neither Anthropic nor OpenAI — it is Google Cloud. Gemini and GCP used to fight for the same wafers. Kurian won.

The 2026 collapse was visible before the press release. Gemini 3 Pro at end-2025 was “arguably the best model in the world” and forced Sam Altman’s OpenAI “code red.” SA’s 9 July Tokenomics institutional note already logged the subsequent gap: Gemini 3.5 Flash a “total flop”; the then-upcoming 3.5 Pro only ~Opus 4.5 — “quite literally worse than GLM 5.2, much less Fable 5 and GPT 5.6.” Google has since silently canceled 3.5 Pro and is bridging with Gemini 3.6 Flash, which SA ranks below Muse Spark 1.2, Grok 4.5, and tier-1 Chinese open-source; Gemini sits 8th–9th depending on the scoreboard, and Gemini 4 is not expected to reverse it. MSL and SpaceXAI are forecast to pass Google on quality by year-end. OpenAI has “overcome their pre-training issues”; a larger model code-named “Doug” is in flight. First-party token growth has decelerated in lockstep: 10 → 16B tokens/min in 1Q26 (+60%), then 16 → 22B in 2Q26 (+38%), taking 1P API revenue growth with it. The Vertex/Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform print is stronger only because third-party models (Claude) are running on it — a platform mix shift, not a Gemini mix shift.

The mechanism SA names is **conviction, not talent scarcity in the abstract**. Compute is “the lifeblood of AGI-pilled labs,” and those labs pre-pay multi-GW even when the API P&L cannot yet carry it — Meta and SpaceX keep clawback optionality on excess capacity. Google sold the opposite contract: enormous TPU volume to Gemini’s fiercest competitors on long-term deals **with no path to return silicon to DeepMind**. More than 20% of total TPU *shipments* from 3Q26–4Q27 go directly to Anthropic (shipment = supplier revenue recognition on baseboard/package to the downstream manufacturer, not when chips are operational). That 20% excludes the hundreds of thousands of TPUs GCP already rents to Anthropic and the “many hundreds of thousands more” committed to Anthropic and Meta over the next six quarters. Kurian is not AGI-pilled: he has argued TPUs should become “general purpose infrastructure” for Citadel, the Department of Energy, and generic HPC, and he described selling compute to Anthropic as the natural act of a “platform company.” The same executive just won the political fight over compute allocation. The steelman — clean house, blank-slate team, maybe acqui-hire SSI or Thinking Machines — is rejected as culture, not personnel. DeepMind had a chatbot a year before ChatGPT and was barred from shipping it for fear of disrupting Search. Tibo, a nine-year Google/DeepMind veteran, left in July 2024, co-led OpenAI’s original coding-agent team, and now runs Codex; Google’s July 2025 Windsurf acqui-hire has “not exactly… much wind in [its] sails.” DeepMind itself was the original AI acqui-hire; twelve years of hindsight is the prior. Discovery Loop follows the David Silver / Ineffable Intelligence playbook from November 2025: leave Google, raise billions from outside investors **and Google Ventures**, then spend the money on **Nvidia GPUs inside GCP**.

Tokenomics sizes the capital-allocation reveal. Gemini ARR was ~$12B in 2Q26. By end-2027 SA has GCP doing >$73B of third-party AI ARR in IaaS/TaaS **plus** ~$120B of TPU *system* sales — ~$200B of external sales at high-30s EBIT versus a $12B first-party model business. Last-quarter GCP growth printed 82%; ~$1.2B of that was estimated TPU system sales (new line: full TPU systems sold to external SPVs that run datacenters for Anthropic and peers), booked **gross** at ~$35B/GW, so core cloud grew in the low 70s. System-sale EBIT sits in the low 30s versus core cloud; blended GCP is still expected mid-to-high 30s. A >$150B TPU-systems backlog is what drives SA’s 2027 GCP growth to the **mid-100s versus sell-side ~64%**, adding ~$3 of Google EPS in 2027. Another ~$250B of TPU bookings is estimated as incremental GCP RPO if more multi-GW deals land. How investors capitalize the backlog is left open. The closing frame is the IBM / Intel / Charter analog: invent the transformer, then choose the financially intelligent short-run (sell the pipes) and become “no better than a glorified neocloud.” Once the impressive numbers print, stopping is share-price suicide. TPU TCO leadership is treated as perishable — “less aggressive technology choices versus say Nvidia,” core builders gone — and the TPU edge is an open question, not an axiom ([G-10] reference class; VLM incentive-dilution / talent-gravity test).

## Contradiction Check
No Alphabet thesis exists — this note cannot fire a GOOGL Conviction Trigger; it *is* the seed for one: **AGI-optionality vs infra-financialization**. The falsifier SA itself leaves open is TPU TCO/tech leadership holding after the builder exodus.

Challenges [[Theses/NVDA - Nvidia]] §Outstanding Questions (“How durable is the CUDA moat as hyperscaler ASICs mature for inference?”) and §Key Non-consensus Insights (CUDA general-purpose vs TPU/Trainium workload-specific). The same piece is two-handed: Discovery Loop + Silver-pattern neolabs **buy Nvidia GPUs on GCP**, which is incremental NVDA demand routed through Alphabet’s cloud; simultaneously >20% of TPU *shipments* plus hundreds of thousands of rented TPUs going to Anthropic/Meta is the merchant-ASIC supply the NVDA question is asking about. SA’s own closer — TPU TCO “may crumble… less aggressive technology choices versus say Nvidia” — is a hypothesis that *supports* the CUDA-generality insight if it prints, and a share-erosion print if it does not. NVDA has no Conviction Triggers section to name.

Supports [[Theses/TSM - Taiwan Semiconductor]] §Summary durability-of-HPC-demand (more TPU units, whether 1P or sold-through, still consume leading-edge wafers + advanced packaging) and loads the live [[Theses/TSM - Taiwan Semiconductor]] §Conviction Triggers → LOW packaging fork: “a named production-scale CoWoS-alternative win (e.g., Google TPU v9 volume on Intel EMIB-T).” A >$150B TPU-systems backlog plus >$250B speculated incremental bookings is exactly the volume that forces the CoWoS-vs-EMIB routing question; it does not by itself say which foundry/OSAT wins.

Challenges [[Theses/CRWV - CoreWeave]] §Summary premise (hyperscaler capex cannot keep pace; NVDA redistributes allocation to non-hyperscaler operators to keep the cloud layer fragmented) and Insight #4 (hyperscaler in-housing as the asymmetric risk). GCP selling full TPU systems into Anthropic SPVs at ~$35B/GW, plus IaaS/TaaS on a path to >$73B 3P AI ARR, is a **named hyperscaler acting as a neocloud** — the same competitor class the CRWV Mental Models section already flagged after Meta Compute (Jul 2026). [[Theses/CRWV - CoreWeave]] §Conviction Triggers → HIGH’s Anthropic-diversification leg is contaminated: Anthropic’s incremental compute is being sourced as TPU systems and GCP rental, not only CRWV GPU hours.

Challenges [[Theses/NBIS - Nebius Group]] §Summary (sold-out GPU-cloud execution bet; Meta/Microsoft as de-risked demand) and §Conviction Triggers → CLOSE (Meta $15B flexible tranche not drawn as MTIA ramps). Meta is simultaneously a NBIS anchor **and** a committed TPU renter for “hundreds of thousands” of chips over the next six quarters. SA’s “glorified neocloud” line on GCP is the scale version of the layer-renter problem both neocloud theses already carry (VLM layer-renter test).

Supports [[Theses/AVGO - Broadcom]] §Summary (Anthropic–Google TPU lock through 2031; 3.5GW framing) and the custom-XPU demand flywheel: TPU system sales at ~$35B/GW and a >$150B backlog are AVGO design-services + SerDes volume if Broadcom remains the training-part partner. Contests the in-sourcing-overstated insight at the *share* margin: financializing TPUs as merchant SKUs plus the MediaTek cost-down vehicle in [[Theses/2454 - MediaTek]] §Summary / Insight #1 is how Google turns TPU from a captive research tool into a priced platform — volume-positive for 2454’s convex inference option, mix-negative for AVGO’s Google-share path (Mental Models: 95% → 65% by 2028 is the live debate).

[[Theses/META - Meta]] has no Conviction Triggers section. The source does not move the 97%-ads core; it does add Meta as a multi-quarter TPU-rental sink alongside MTIA/CoreWeave, i.e. a third compute-sourcing sleeve rather than a clean in-house-or-neocloud binary.

Fired as hypotheses, not verdicts: Generalist [G-5] culture/talent gravity, [G-8] capital allocation (Kurian over DeepMind), [G-10] IBM/Intel/Charter base rate, [G-13] 64% vs mid-100s GCP growth, [G-4] Perez frenzy (sell the installed substrate); Semis #10 (Anthropic as TPU-shipment anchor), #13 (reclassify GOOGL from AGI compounder to infra/neocloud), #18 (do not read 82% GCP as structural SOTA); Automation Lens A anti-fit (key-person / tribal DeepMind) and Lens B (GCP as compute vendor); VLM infrastructure-layer widening vs incentive-dilution / talent-gravity on the TPU itself.
