# Sandisk Investor Day: FY28–30 Model ~80% GM / ~75% OM / ~50% FCF; 8 NBM Customers

## Thesis Delta
Consensus still argues whether [[Theses/SNDK - SanDisk]] 65–67% near-term GM guide is cyclical pricing or a structural model — this 13 August 2026 company IR (“Sandisk In Focus”) publishes a FY2028–FY2030 framework of mid-to-high-teens revenue growth, ~80% non-GAAP GM, ~75% non-GAAP OM, ~5% opex/revenue, and ~50% adjusted FCF margin, plus New Business Model contracts with eight customers covering ~50% of FY2027 bits and ~two-thirds of FY2028 bits, 100% excess-cash return after reinvestment, BiCS9 QLC as the first two-dimensional CBA derivative, BiCS10 QLC +60% bit density vs BiCS8, and a 1.2 zettabyte enterprise-flash TAM by 2030. That is a step-function above the thesis’s “65%+ would be unprecedented” line. SNDK has no formal Conviction Triggers section (gap). Hypothesis: NBM take-or-pay is the mechanism that would make 80% GM durable; without disclosed pricing floors and customer names the model is still a company slide.

## Summary
David Goeckeler frames current results as 18-month execution of the post-spin strategy: CBA NAND, systems expertise, diversified portfolio, capital-efficient operations, full-stack control. The technical story is two-dimensional scaling — custom derivatives without a full new-cell capex cycle. BiCS9 QLC pairs a proven BiCS8 array with BiCS10-class CMOS; BiCS10 QLC is cited at 60% higher bit density than BiCS8. AI inference and KV cache are said to make the data center “dramatically more storage intensive,” with enterprise data-center flash TAM to 1.2 ZB by 2030.

The commercial story is NBM: committed volumes, enforceable contracts with minimum financial guarantees, structured pricing. Eight customers; ~50% of bits in FY2027 and ~two-thirds in FY2028. HBF is mentioned as “gaining momentum” with an industry ecosystem forming — no tape-out date in this PR (see the companion Crypto Briefing note). CFO Luis Visoso: optimize for growth, sustainability, and returns; return 100% of excess cash after investing in the business; confidence “grounded” in multi-year NBMs.
