# TSM Q2 2026 Results — Beat-and-Raise the Market Refused to Pay For

## Thesis Delta

- **All three legs of the [[Theses/TSM - Taiwan Semiconductor|TSM]] Mental-Models "Jul 16 live test" resolved POSITIVE — and the stock fell anyway.** (1) FY26 revenue growth guide raised >30% → "slightly above 40%"; (2) first 2027 capex signal delivered: 2026 capex raised $52–56B → **$60–64B** and CFO Huang: "CapEx in the next three years will be even more significantly higher than the past three years"; (3) 2H26 GM dilution quantified: N2 steep ramp ~3–4pts + overseas fabs 2–3% early-stage widening to 3–4% — unchanged framing, absorbed by a 67.7% print. Yet TSM fell ~5% on the print and sits ~11% below the $474.71 high at ~$421 (mcap ~$1.92T, 07-22). The ceiling was raised fundamentally; the market did not re-rate. Conviction impact: unchanged (high) — but the *shape* of the position changed: forward asymmetry partially restored mechanically (price −11%, estimates +7–10%).
- **The thesis Bull Case arrived two years early — targets need re-basing, not celebrating.** Bull case modeled 2028 EPS $16–17/ADR at 25x = $400+. Actual: FY26E EPS builds to ~$17.5/ADR (H1 actual $7.76 + Q3 guide; derived) — the 2028 bull earnings are being delivered in 2026, and $421 = ~24x FY26E is the bull-case multiple on those earnings *today*. Both bull ($380–420) and bear ($170–200) brackets are now dead letters; the re-armed question is whether 2027–2028 EPS compounds another leg (capex signal says capacity for it) or the margin/capex peak is in.
- **Margin regime: Insight #5 ("pricing power rising, not topped") over-delivered — and inverted the consensus fear.** GM 67.7% (guide 65.5–67.5%, +910bps YoY), OM 60.3%, net margin 55.6% — all records; thesis's stretch 2028 GM case (62–64%) exceeded in Q2 2026. Q3 guide 65–67% is the first sequential GM decline of the cycle. Consensus fear rotated from "margin ceiling" (2024–25) to "margin peak" (now). The non-consensus read: the decline is arithmetic dilution from N2 ramp + Arizona buildout — capacity-investment math, not pricing erosion — while pricing power continues (fourth consecutive annual hike cycle signaled, N2 sold out). 2027 test: GM holding ≥63–64% through peak dilution would falsify the peak narrative.
- **Packaging bottleneck confirmed binding — Wei: capacity "so tight that now it's limiting my customers' growth," and TSMC is *welcoming* EMIB-T overflow.** Advanced packaging now 10–20% of capex. This cuts both ways: (a) confirms CoWoS-annuity pricing power (Insight #1) and Semis-#1 bottleneck economics at maximum intensity; (b) materializes the EMIB cost-arbitrage vector flagged 2026-05-12 — Google's 9th-gen TPU is reportedly evaluating Intel EMIB to skirt CoWoS constraints, and CoWoS orders are spilling to Intel and Taiwanese OSATs (Tom's Hardware/Wccftech, 07-17). Wei's "welcome" is either restraint discipline (cede low-margin overflow, keep the high-ASP core) or the first crack in packaging share — record both, monitor which.
- **Intel counter-signal cluster (post-print week) reopens a refuted bear leg.** TrendForce (07-17): Intel may build up to **90% of Nova Lake compute tiles on 18A**, scaling back TSMC's role — a direct reversal of the 05-12 datapoint (Nova Lake >90% on TSMC N2). Moomoo (weak sourcing): 18A yield ~85%, orders from NVIDIA/OpenAI. Neither confirmed; both untested. The Mental-Models "Intel 18A threat REFUTED" line should downgrade to reversal-watch, not verdict.
- **Silicon shield upgraded again (Insight #2): +$100B Arizona → $265B total US commitment** — several more 2nm-and-below fabs plus advanced-packaging plants, buildout "as fast as possible," 13 new leading-edge/AP fabs also planned in Taiwan. New capital-structure signal: openness to bond issuance — first hint the buildout exceeds pure FCF funding. Overseas GM dilution guidance unchanged despite the larger footprint.
- **Platform mix: the AI-capex-cyclical rotation is complete.** HPC 66% of revenue (+20% QoQ) vs smartphone 22% (−4% QoQ) — the thesis body's HPC 59%/smartphone 28% frame is a full regime stale. Agentic AI driving CPU resurgence — Wei: whatever CPU architecture wins, "they are almost all TSMC's customers." Node mix: N5 33% / N3 30% / N7 11% / **N2 3%** (first material quarter); advanced ≤7nm = 77%.
- **A14 cadence lands where A16 chatter was expected**: A14 pre-production 2027, volume 2028; A13/A12 derivatives 2029. A16/Feynman exclusivity went unmentioned on the call — the thesis's Insight #3 is untested this quarter, not confirmed.

## Summary

TSMC's Q2 2026 (reported 07-16) was a records-across-the-board beat-and-raise: revenue NT$1,270.38B (US$40.2B), +36.0% YoY and +12.0% QoQ; net income NT$706.56B, +77.4% YoY; diluted EPS NT$27.25 (US$4.31/ADR, ~$0.42 above consensus); GM 67.7% above the top of guidance; OM 60.3%. Management raised FY26 revenue growth guidance to "slightly above 40%" (implying ~$168–172B) and capex to $60–64B, committed an additional $100B to Arizona ($265B total US), and framed AI demand as a "multi-year megatrend" with conviction "very high" — extending the demand horizon to "2029, 2030" and declining to raise the mid-to-high-50s% AI-revenue CAGR only because the figures "continue to increase." Q3 guide: $44.6–45.8B (+~12% QoQ, +~37% YoY), GM 65–67%.

The analytical event is not the print — it is the market's reaction to it. Against a maximal fundamental confirmation (every 2026-07-09 "live test" leg resolved positive), TSM fell ~5% on the day and sits ~11% below its early-July high, amid an AI-complex de-rate ("AI capex fatigue" — sell-side margin-peak and foundry-competition fears). Two readings compete. Bull: the thesis's own "quality, fully priced" caution (spot had exceeded the $380–420 bull target) is being unwound by price while earnings estimates rise — asymmetry mechanically restored without any thesis deterioration. Bear: the market is front-running a 2027 AI-capex second derivative (Meta's late-July guide is the next test) and a structural margin peak (N2 dilution 3–4pts + overseas dilution stacking, first sequential GM decline guided). The quarter itself supplies evidence against the bear mechanics — utilization full, N2 sold out, packaging demand exceeding supply, pricing hikes sticking — but none against the flows.

Second-order signal worth isolating: TSMC's packaging scarcity is now openly re-routing demand to competitors (EMIB-T "welcomed" by Wei; Google TPU v9 reportedly evaluating EMIB; overflow to OSATs). A monopolist voluntarily ceding overflow while raising its own capacity 10–20% of a record capex budget is consistent with the deliberate-restraint/pricing-discipline pattern (per the 04-24 and 06-03 SemiAnalysis reads) — but it creates the first at-scale qualification pathway for a CoWoS alternative, which is how packaging-share erosion would *start* if it ever does.

## Contradiction Check

- **Beat-everything vs −5%**: the single-week price action does not falsify either the fully-priced read or the capex-fatigue read; Meta's late-July capex guide (next week, per [[_catalyst.md]] window) is the first discriminating datapoint. Do not mark the re-rate question resolved.
- **Nova Lake 18A pull-back (TrendForce) vs Nova Lake >90% N2 (SemiWiki, 05-12)**: direct contradiction, both secondary sources, neither confirmed by Intel/TSMC. Recorded as reversal-watch, not fact. Same for Moomoo's "18A yield 85% / NVDA-OpenAI orders."
- **"Welcoming EMIB-T" as restraint vs as share loss**: management framing serves TSMC either way; the falsifier is a *named* production-scale CoWoS-alternative win (e.g., TPU v9 volume on EMIB) — that would convert restraint into erosion.
- **Margin peak vs dilution arithmetic**: Q3 GM guide decline is fully explained by disclosed dilution vectors; but a 2027 GM print <63% *with* continued price hikes would mean the dilution is worse than guided — that is the falsifying datapoint for Insight #5's extension.
- **Capex "significantly higher" vs AI-bubble timing** ([[AI Bubble Risk and Semiconductor Valuations]]): TSMC is committing multi-year supply into a demand curve its own customers must keep validating; if the 2027 hyperscaler capex second derivative turns negative, the raised capex becomes the utilization-cliff mechanism the CoWoS-overbuild bear leg described. The CoWoS supply-demand gap (20%→10% by end-2026) remains the dated milestone to watch.
