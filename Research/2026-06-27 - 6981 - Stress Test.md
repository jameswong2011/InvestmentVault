# 6981 Murata — Stress Test (Short Case)

*Adversarial test of the HIGH-conviction thesis. Mental-models lenses applied per CLAUDE.md mandatory gate: [[Generalist - Overview]], [[Industry - Semiconductors]], [[Lens - Value Layer Monopoly]], [[Lens - Automation & AI Readiness]] — held as hypotheses-to-test, not verdicts.*

## Thesis Delta
The +40-50% upside is a **re-rate** (22.6x → 28-30x), but the vault's own FMP refresh (2026-05-23) shows Murata already at **~59x NTM P/E / ~37x EV/EBIT NTM** — roughly double the thesis's own anchor — so there may be no re-rating headroom left. The earnings path that would justify even the current multiple (demand-led 43%-of-sales) is **unfunded**: management guides capex ~¥330B vs the ¥550-700B the bull case requires. 2/7 bull assumptions 🔴, 5/7 🟡, 0 🟢. Conviction impact: **weakened — reassess HIGH** (the conviction level itself is unchanged pending user decision via `/status`).

## Summary
**Thesis Vulnerability Summary.** The single biggest reason this investment fails: it is a *cyclical priced as a compounder, where the upside is a re-rate that has already happened.* The thesis frames Murata as "priced as a smartphone-cycle name at 22.6x P/E… the multiple re-rates to 28-30x as durability becomes consensus." But the vault's own 2026-05-24 Semiconductor Portfolio Rebalancing — using a 2026-05-23 FMP pull — records Murata at **P/E NTM 59.5x, EV/EBIT NTM 37.3x, ROIC NTM +9.1%, Rev Gr CFY −1.2%, EPS Gr CFY −6.2%**, and concludes "**TRIM Low (~2%) → 1-2%** — structural-volume thesis is real but ROIC 9% + negative growth makes the rich multiple uncomfortable." The thesis §Key Metrics (22.6x P/E, 17.1x EV/EBIT) and the rebalancing (59.5x / 37.3x) cannot both be right, and the thesis has never reconciled the gap. If the FMP anchor is even directionally correct, the entire variant perception ("market prices ≈zero of the mix shift") collapses: the stock already embeds the AI/EV content-growth story at a premium AI-component multiple, so the +40-50% requires earnings to *actually triple along the demand-led path* — which in turn requires a capex doubling management is explicitly not funding (¥250B + ¥80B "emergency" = ~¥330B guided vs ¥550-700B/yr required). Strip the re-rate and the unfunded earnings path away, and what remains is a ~¥1.8T-revenue mature passive-components maker, currently shrinking (rev −1.2%, EPS −6.2%), earning ~9-10% ROIC, that the vault's own portfolio process wants to cut to a 1-2% position. The mental-models read (below) compounds this: mean-reversion-vs-trend, ROIIC × runway, base rates, and #13 classification all fire *against* paying a premium multiple here.

## Evidence

### Evidence Against (5 points, vault-cited)

1. **The valuation anchor is internally contradicted — and it is the entire upside thesis.** §Key Metrics carries P/E 22.6x and EV/EBIT 17.1x (FY26 OP ¥258B / EV ¥4.4T). The vault's most recent quant refresh ([[Research/2026-05-24 - Semiconductor Portfolio Rebalancing - synthesis]], FMP 2026-05-23) shows **P/E NTM 59.5x / EV/EBIT NTM 37.3x** — implying NTM EBIT ~¥118B, roughly half the thesis's ¥258B. The likely culprit is depressed near-term GAAP earnings (the −¥105B annual pricing headwind + the Q3 ¥43.8B SAW-filter/Resonant goodwill impairment, both from [[Research/2026-05-29 - Earnings Transcripts vs Thesis - 6 Holdings - synthesis]]). Whichever number is right, the thesis chose the cheaper one without reconciliation, and on the FMP figures the "priced as a smartphone-cycle name" premise — the foundation of the +40-50% re-rate — fails outright. *(Caveat: the rebalancing note flags FMP NTM noise elsewhere, e.g. TSM P/E "0.8", LITE/SNDK EV/EBIT anomalies — so the 59.5x is not gospel. But the burden is on the bull case to prove 22.6x is the right anchor, and it hasn't; the contradiction is unresolved.)*

2. **The demand-led 43%-of-sales bull case is unfunded by management's own capex.** The thesis's headline path (AI-MLCC → 43% of sales / 62% of OP by FY35, static-EV EV/EBIT collapsing to ~4.3x) is explicitly gated on "capex catches up (the swing assumption) — ~¥550-700B/yr FY28-32." Murata's actual FY27 guide ([[Research/2026-05-29 - Earnings Transcripts vs Thesis - 6 Holdings - synthesis]]) is **¥250B + ¥80B "emergency" server-MLCC capacity = ~¥330B** — less than 60% of the floor of the required range, and the "emergency" framing confirms structural under-build, not a deliberate volume land-grab. The thesis's own §Risks concedes the alternative is the **supply-capped ~24%-of-sales path** with incremental AI demand flowing to Samsung Electro-Mechanics / TDK. The live reality is the downside path, yet conviction is HIGH on the upside path.

3. **The "100% in-house powder" capital-intensity moat is partly overstated at the exact frontier the thesis says is growing fastest.** [[Research/2026-06-05 - AI-Grade MLCC Upstream Pricing Power - deep-dive]] finds high-spec AI-grade sub-100nm BaTiO₃ is "increasingly merchant-sourced" from Sakai Chemical / Nippon Chemical, and argues the single-source upstream materials suppliers (release film: Toray-led ~87%; BaTiO₃; ultra-fine Ni powder) hold **more acute pricing power than the MLCC makers**, because Murata must pass cost through to NVIDIA/Apple while the materials supplier does not. The thesis's §Key Non-consensus Insight #6 ("capital intensity is the moat") and §Industry Context "in-house powder" claim are nuanced by this: Murata is a layer that pays rent *upward*, and the cleanest shortage-rent capture may sit one layer above it.

4. **Pricing is a net headwind, not the "firming" the thesis implies.** §Key Non-consensus Insight #5 states "pricing on 008004 has firmed for three consecutive quarters." The transcripts ([[Research/2026-05-29 - Earnings Transcripts vs Thesis - 6 Holdings - synthesis]]) show pricing is still a **net −¥105B annual headwind** — commodity-case declines outrunning the premium-case hikes, with volume/utilization/mix doing the heavy lifting. The April-2026 +15-35% price hike is real but confined to the AI-server/auto-grade/RF slice; the blended MLCC book is still de-pricing. The moat protects a thin premium tier while the bulk of revenue erodes.

5. **The quant profile is the inverse of the vault's preferred names, and the portfolio process already wants to cut it.** ROIC ~9-10% (barely above cost of capital), current revenue −1.2% and EPS −6.2%, capex/revenue ~14% rising to 18-20%. [[Research/2026-05-24 - Semiconductor Portfolio Rebalancing - synthesis]] places Murata in the "**Semi-cyclical compounders / Expensive vs through-cycle**" quadrant, S-curve location "late mainstream = mean-reversion regime," and actions it **TRIM to 1-2%**. A HIGH-conviction thesis sitting on a name the same vault's portfolio logic rates a 1-2% trim is a conviction-vs-evidence misalignment.

## Framework / Mental Model
**Applied:** [[Generalist - Overview]] (always) · [[Industry - Semiconductors]] (sector) · [[Lens - Value Layer Monopoly]] (008004 layer position) · [[Lens - Automation & AI Readiness]] (physical/tacit-yield overlay). Per the READING PROTOCOL, each is a hypothesis to test, and cross-model agreement is the trigger to disconfirm.

- **Mean-reversion vs trend-continuation (Generalist; Industry #7/#13/#18).** Hypothesis: Murata is a late-mainstream cyclical being narrated as a structural compounder. 1Y +247% + the April-2026 +15-35% price hike = "units up + prices up = SHORTAGE" (Industry #7) = late up-cycle, not early. The thesis's own FY30-31 margin give-back concedes embedded cyclicality. Misapplying trend-continuation to a cyclical at the top is "the second most expensive equity research mistake."
- **ROIIC × runway (Generalist).** Hypothesis: incremental capital earns only ~9-10% ROIC while the demand-led path demands ¥550-700B/yr capex — deploying near cost-of-capital is value-neutral unless the shortage premium is durable (it gives back FY30-31 by the thesis's own model). This is the killer model: the vault's edge is ROIIC × runway, and here ROIIC is mediocre and the runway is a capital-intensity arms race.
- **Expectations investing / reverse-DCF (Generalist).** Hypothesis: the "priced at 22.6x, prices zero mix-shift" premise is contradicted by the vault's FMP NTM multiples (~59x P/E, ~37x EV/EBIT). If the higher anchor holds, the mix-shift is already priced and the re-rate upside is illusory — the operating variable to monitor is whether clean FY26 OP reconciles to FMP NTM EBIT.
- **Base rates / outside view (Generalist).** Hypothesis: a ¥1.8T-revenue mature passive maker sustaining ~11% rev CAGR + AI-MLCC 12× to FY35 is a positive outlier to Mauboussin's growth-persistence base rate — starker given *current negative* revenue/EPS growth. The thesis extrapolates a steep S-curve from a company presently contracting.
- **Qualification-gate monopoly (Industry #2).** Hypothesis (partly supportive): 008004 + AEC-Q200 are genuine structural gates — but flavor (i) "capacity scarcity, cycle-dependent pricing power" describes the 2027-29 shortage, while only the premium slice enjoys flavor (ii) durable monopoly pricing. The thesis conflates the two.
- **#13 classification (Industry).** Hypothesis: the vault rebalancing classifies Murata "semi-cyclical compounder (mature)"; paying a premium multiple at late-mainstream is the #13 misclassification error if the structural-compounder read is wrong.
- **Layer-renter disqualifier + pricing-power evidence (Value Layer Monopoly §1B/§2).** Hypothesis: Murata owns the 008004 layer but pays rent *upward* to single-source materials (release film / BaTiO₃ / Ni powder) that may capture more shortage rent; the "100% in-house powder" claim is nuanced by merchant-sourcing of AI-grade powder; pricing power shows up only in a thin slice (blended pricing −¥105B). Net: a WEAK-to-MODERATE layer monopoly; cleanest rent may be upstream. Alpha test fails if the variant perception ("market prices zero") is contradicted by the 59x NTM multiple.
- **Semiconductor split overlay (Automation & AI Readiness §6).** Hypothesis: Murata's edge is tacit yield/chemistry knowledge — durable moat (good for durability) but Anti-fit on operator-automation, so the margin-expansion case *cannot* lean on AI operating leverage; it rests on mix + shortage alone. Conviction-neutral on this axis, with a caveat against bull margin paths that assume automation.

## Contradiction Check
Internal contradictions surfaced by the Phase 2 scan (thesis-internal, not external):

1. **Valuation: §Key Metrics 22.6x P/E vs vault FMP 59.5x NTM P/E** — unreconciled; the cheaper number underwrites the entire re-rate thesis.
2. **Capex: §Bull/§Key Metrics require ¥550-700B/yr vs management's ¥330B guide** — the §Risks "supply-capped ~24%" path is the live reality, but conviction sits on the 43% path.
3. **Pricing: §Insight #5 "firmed three consecutive quarters" vs transcript net −¥105B headwind** — "firming" is true only for the small-case slice.
4. **Moat: §Insight #6 "capital intensity / 100% in-house powder" vs upstream deep-dive "AI-grade powder increasingly merchant-sourced"** — the deepest moat claim is partially self-cancelling (Murata is deepening captive supply via the MF Material JV, so forward-basis it holds; spot-basis it's overstated).
5. **Conviction vs evidence: HIGH conviction vs ROIC 9% + negative current growth + 10 unanswered Outstanding Questions + vault rebalancing TRIM-to-1-2%.**
6. **Conviction-trigger coverage gap:** the LOW/CLOSE triggers cover Chinese 008004 closure and small-case ASP/lead-time/GM — but the *actual* near-term failure modes (JPY appreciation, capex-underfund → supply-capped reversion, AI-capex digestion, de-rate from an already-premium multiple) have **no falsifiable trigger**. A thesis can degrade materially without ever tripping its own reassessment framework.

**Supports existing conviction (recorded for balance):** AI-MLCC demand ramp (440k/rack, 3.3× by FY30, confirmed +15-35% price hike, 24-40wk lead times) is real and corroborated; FY27 OP guide +34.8% to ¥380B runs *ahead* of the thesis's own margin schedule. The structural-volume pillar is not broken — but it is a *content-growth-in-a-shortage* story, which is a cycle trade dressed as a compounder, and the valuation already reflects it.
