## Summary
Benzinga, 14 August 2026 4:36 AM, recapping Digitimes Friday: Nvidia Corp (NASDAQ: NVDA) is accelerating next-generation Feynman AI-platform development, pushing Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (NYSE: TSM) to fast-track advanced chipmaking and packaging capacity ahead of an expected 2028 launch. Nvidia is shifting research and supply-chain resources toward Feynman even as Vera Rubin enters mass production and ramp-up.

Feynman is expected to feature 3D chiplets, TSMC System on Integrated Chips (SoIC), high-bandwidth memory (HBM) and co-packaged optics (CPO) — a major architectural evolution from Rubin. Industry sources expect Feynman to use TSMC's upgraded 2nm-class A16 process. The platform could push NVLink bandwidth beyond 1,000 terabytes per second, or 1 petabyte per second, as Nvidia builds increasingly large AI clusters.

The accelerated roadmap adds pressure on TSMC advanced packaging. TSMC is accelerating construction at AP7 in Chiayi and AP8 in the Southern Taiwan Science Park, while preparing capacity for SoIC, CoWoS-L and next-generation CoPoS. SoIC stacks chips vertically, potentially improving bandwidth while reducing latency and power consumption. The report said TSMC could increase monthly SoIC capacity to approximately 50,000 wafers by the end of 2027, up significantly from earlier plans. TSMC and Nvidia did not immediately respond to Benzinga's request for comment. Digitimes original was not retrieved; no additional Digitimes figures are used.

Nvidia reports second-quarter results on Aug. 26. Bank of America analyst Vivek Arya, in a Monday note, maintained Nvidia as a "top pick" and kept a $350 price target (56.3% upside from $223.96 at the time). BofA forecasts quarterly revenue $94 billion to $95 billion, about $3–4 billion above the company's $91 billion guidance, and expects third-quarter guidance of $107–108 billion vs consensus roughly $104 billion. Nvidia has also begun working with major financial institutions on an AI infrastructure financing platform targeting more than $500 billion in third-party capital (already in vault: [[Research/2026-08-12 - NVDA - Nvidia 500B AI Infrastructure Financing Platforms - news]]).

Price action: Nvidia closed $225.30 Thursday, up 0.54%; Friday premarket $225.25, down 0.02% (Benzinga Pro).
