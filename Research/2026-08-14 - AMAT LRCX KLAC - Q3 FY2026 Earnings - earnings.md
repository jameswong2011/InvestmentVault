# Applied Materials Q3 FY2026: Record $9.12B; DRAM 26%; China 28%; Q4 $10.25B ±$0.5B

## Thesis Delta
Consensus still frames [[Theses/AMAT - Applied Materials]] as the cheap WFE cohort name with China/ICAPS drag — this 13 August 2026 Q3 FY2026 print (quarter ended 26 July) is a record $9.12 billion (+25% YoY), 13th straight YoY GM expansion (GAAP 50.3% / non-GAAP 50.4%), record non-GAAP EPS $3.50, Semiconductor Systems $7.04 billion with DRAM mix 26% (from 22%), China 28% (from 35%), and Q4 guide $10.25 billion ±$500 million / non-GAAP EPS $4.02 ±$0.20, plus six new DRAM/AP tools and EPIC expanded to 11 engagements including Broadcom. That is a DRAM/HBM and leading-edge foundry-logic 2H tape, not an ICAPS-trough tape. Trigger touch (flag only): AMAT → HIGH needs ALD share at TSMC N2 + HBM $2B run-rate + ICAPS acceleration in 2 of 3 quarters + EPIC partner at TSMC/Intel/Samsung “embedded” scope — this PR does not disclose HBM dollars or N2 ALD share; Broadcom-as-EPIC-innovation-partner is not the named-foundry embedded-team leg. → LOW if China <20% with AMEC displacement — 28% is still above the handle (down from 35%, not a breach).

## Summary
Gary Dickerson: “highest sequential revenue growth in the company’s history”; raising calendar-2026 Semiconductor Systems expectations; “another strong growth year… in 2027” on customer demand visibility. Brice Hill: 13th consecutive YoY GM expansion; 2H strength “particularly in DRAM as well as leading-edge foundry-logic and advanced packaging”; more manufacturing capacity for demand “through the end of the decade.” Cash from operations $3.04 billion; $860 million returned ($440 million buybacks + $420 million dividends).

Segment: Semiconductor Systems $7,040 million vs $5,564 million; mix foundry/logic/other 67% (was 69%), DRAM 26% (22%), flash 7% (9%); SS non-GAAP OM 38.0%. AGS $1,781 million vs $1,463 million; OM 30.1%. Other/Display $294 million; operating loss $118 million. Geography: China $2,506 million / 28% (was 35%); Taiwan 22%; Korea 17%; U.S. 15% (was 9%). Headcount 38.9k vs 36.1k.

New tools: Enhanced Centura Prime Epi (strained S/D for DRAM/HBM); Opta Quad CMP (hybrid-bond TTV); Nokota VMax 2 ECD with Adaptive Pattern Tuning; Producer Avila 2 PECVD (stress-balanced dielectrics around TSVs for 12/16-Hi HBM); VeritySEM 7AP and SEMVision G7AP (AP metrology/defect). Logic/memory: Centris Spectral SiN ALD (microwave); Producer Selectra Mo Etch (3D NAND wordline). SENZ ambient visual platform for AI glasses. Corporate: EPIC +Broadcom (AP), UC Berkeley, SCREEN; $500 million Tampines campus (Singapore cleanroom >2×); JDA with EssilorLuxottica.
