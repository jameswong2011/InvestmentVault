# Cloudflare Achieves FedRAMP High for U.S. Government Cloud

## Thesis Delta
Consensus still frames NET as a developer-edge/SASE laggard vs PANW/Zscaler (Gartner Visionary, DLP/CASB gaps). FedRAMP High (plus GovRAMP Moderate and stated DoD IL4 intent) is a *regulatory fixed-cost* gate that lets Cloudflare for Government attach to civilian High-impact workloads — not a SASE feature catch-up. Supports [[Theses/NET - Cloudflare]] public-sector TAM option; competitive read-through to [[Theses/PANW - Palo Alto Networks]] Fed/SASE share. Mental-model triggers: Value Layer Monopoly §1 “Regulatory fixed-cost moat”; [G-6] switching-cost / compliance lock-in; [G-12] cohort (gov is a different CAC/retention curve).

## Summary
Cloudflare press release announced August 12, 2026: FedRAMP High authorization for Cloudflare for Government, so agencies and partners can adopt the platform for High-impact federal systems. David Mihalchik (VP U.S. Public Sector) cites FedRAMP High + GovRAMP Moderate + intent to pursue DoD IL4 as the stack that lets them bring the same platform to civilian and (later) DoD missions. This is an authorization milestone, not a bookings print — no ARR, agency-count, or ACV figures in the release.
