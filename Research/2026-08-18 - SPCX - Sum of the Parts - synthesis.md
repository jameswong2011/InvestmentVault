# SPCX — Sum of the Parts (user-assumption stack vs vault-consistent)

## Thesis Delta

User-assumption SOTP is **~$2.1T** — roughly A+B at $140 (~$1.9T), ~1.9× Class A (~$1.12T). The two derived slices the thesis did not carry as standalone values: **Launch ex-internal Starlink is a ~$40B railroad** (~$2B Falcon EBITDA, 18–22×), not a second growth equity; **Starlink including marine/air/mobile is ~$400B**, of which mobile is a **$150–250B terminal / $150B today-SOTP**, not a $1T fourth carrier. The $1.5T locked on Grok + neocloud is what makes the sum a $2T stock — and is the same unproven pool the thesis already refuses to treat as printed. Conviction **unchanged (medium)**. Vault-consistent column: **~$0.8T**.

The $1.3T gap between the two columns collapses on one variable the thesis already names: CSA duration vs GPU/turbine D&A. GW count, Grok-bot demos, and Terafab filings do not.

## Summary

SpaceX at ~$140 is still two capital pools. This note prices them as six slices under a user-specified bull stack (Grok $1T, neocloud $500B, Terafab $100B, investable-horizon optionality $0, multi-generational ∞ excluded) and derives the two missing numbers — third-party launch and Starlink-with-mobility — from the Q2 file plus TAM work.

Launch is already printed ex-Starlink: internal constellation flights are eliminated in consolidation. Q2 launch services were $648M on 10 customer missions ($65M ASP, Falcon 9 list). Falcon contribution before Starship R&D is ~$350–450M/quarter; Starship (~$3B FY2025 / $1.08B in Q2) is what makes the Space segment lose money. Third-party launch TAM is $15–20B; SpaceX already has 80–90% of commercial mass and prices off the next-best alternative, not its own $15–28M reused cost. The surplus on the 28 internal Q2 flights sits inside Starlink’s 60.5% margin — adding “shadow launch” double-counts the railroad and the toll road.

Starlink is three businesses. The printed annuity (fixed + Starshield, $10.4B adj. EBITDA run-rate) is ~$230B at 22×. Marine + aviation are the under-penetrated cash — Gwynne’s “aviation still <10% penetrated,” IFC TAM only $3–6B, maritime SATCOM $7–8B — another ~$70B of incremental EBITDA not in the current run-rate. Mobile is the slice that can move the equity, and it is not a fourth carrier. Three products: wholesale D2C (live via T-Mobile on bands phones already speak), EchoStar-band D2C (65 MHz, $17–19.6B, needs new modem silicon), and femtocell-on-dish (Shotwell’s answer to Evercore’s $100B tower-build question). Apple is required for EchoStar-band iPhone radios, not for T-Satellite or femtocells. Eventual mobile equity is $150–250B; a Verizon-clone $400B+ requires the plant they said they would not build.

Grok $1T and neocloud $500B are accepted as the user’s scenario, then haircut in a second column. Application-layer Grok is contestable even if the bot works (VLM §3). Neocloud $500B is SemiAnalysis 10 GW × $50B/GW against 1.4 GW printed, 90-day paper, Anthropic at ~$15/W-yr, and a ~15–20% shot at 10 GW energized compute by Dec 2027 ([[Research/2026-08-18 - SPCX - 10GW Datacenter Pipeline Feasibility - deep-dive]]). Terafab $100B is a successful-IDM outcome on a $16.8B filing with no tool POs. Future Earth = $0 and multi-generational = ∞ is the correct split: Starship/HLS/Mars/orbital compute are either already inside Starlink/AI1 or East India Company paper. Pricing them in a 3–5 year SOTP double-counts Starship three times.

## Framework / Mental Model

SOTP as an expectations inversion ([G-13]): A+B at ~$1.9T already embeds something close to the user stack. The mispriced operating variable is still CSA duration, not GW count and not a hidden launch jewel.

Fired as hypotheses to test, not verdicts:

- **[G-7] ROIIC × runway** — Connectivity ROIIC is printed (60.5% on owned launch). Falcon incremental 60–75% on a $15–20B TAM with no Jevons cut to third-party price. AI runway is $15.8B/qtr capex whose incremental return is undisclosed.
- **[G-10] base rates** — $1T Grok is inside-view; few #3–4 model labs have ever been worth $1T. 10 GW energized compute in one year has no reference-class precedent.
- **[G-14] Jevons ownership** — Starlink internalized cheap launch because SpaceX owns Falcon. The AI campus rents NVDA/HBM/turbines. Do not transfer the Starlink multiple onto colo.
- **[G-6] / [G-12]** — Starlink fixed is software-like (low incremental cost, high switching). Mobile cohort economics are unprinted (T-Satellite $10/mo is a wedge, not a curve). Aviation/marine are high-ARPU, low-churn enterprise cohorts — the June/August price extractions are the first retention stress test.
- **[G-3] mean-reversion vs trend** — Connectivity is the compounder claim; AI capex is the cyclical claim wearing compounder clothes until duration prints. Launch is a mature monopoly on a small TAM (mean-revert the growth, not the share).
- **[G-4] Perez** — neocloud $500B is frenzy-phase infrastructure priced as deployment-era harvest. Over-build is functional; the colo layer is usually not the winner.
- **VLM §1/§2** — Launch + Starlink: owned layer, STRONG FIT (capital-scale + vertical integration). AI colo: layer-renter masquerading as layer-owner. Grok: application layer. Conglomeration disqualifier fires on the *blend*; platform envelopment (Starlink → mobile via owned spectrum + dishes) is the legitimate exception, and only on Connectivity.
- **VLM §3** — Launch/connectivity/orbital infrastructure: moat widening. Grok/X/Cursor: moat dissolving. The $1T Grok line is the slice AI makes *more* contestable.
- **Industry-Semis #2 / #10 / #17** — Terafab $100B assumes a qualification-gate crossing that has no named node, no anchor, no tool POs. New-entrant fabs do not materialize at tight prices on a 5–7yr clock.

Disconfirming check (READING PROTOCOL): every model converges on “Connectivity real, AI unpriced-as-proven.” That agreement is the cue to run the user’s $1.5T AI stack as a real scenario, not to dismiss it. The bull that makes $2.1T right: Grok bot becomes a default surface, NVDA exclusive + permitting-arbitrage converts into *duration*, Apple ships EchoStar-band NTN. The single falsifier for the user stack: first CSA termination cascade, or Apple stays with Amazon/Globalstar *and* aviation/marine churn on the 2× price.
