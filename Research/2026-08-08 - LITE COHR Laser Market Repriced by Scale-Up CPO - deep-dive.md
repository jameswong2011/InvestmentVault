## Summary
Clarifies pluggable vs CPO vs NPO: CPO cuts port power ~30W→9W but raises yield/service risk; NPO is a parallel architecture pending 400G/lane socket physics. Three misconceptions: (1) CPO shrinks optics TAM—coexistence + port growth; (2) CPO cuts laser revenue—NVIDIA's 4x is EML vs CPO apples-to-oranges; (3) scale-out CPO timing is the investable call—within scale-out, 2030 market is $70–74B regardless of CPO share. Laser die CAGR 24% (scale-out) vs up to 61% with scale-up. Watch accelerator scale-up optical IF announcements, engine/laser module orders, high-power laser revenue mix.
