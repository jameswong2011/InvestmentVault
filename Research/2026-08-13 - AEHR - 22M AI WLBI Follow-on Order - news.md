# Aehr $22M Follow-On FOX-XP Order from Lead AI-Processor Customer

## Thesis Delta
Consensus still treats AEHR as a SiPh/SiC niche tester whose AI/HBM WLBI story is one-customer and not yet production-ramped. Company PR: $22M *follow-on* production order from the lead wafer-level AI-processor customer — multiple FOX-XP systems (9×300mm wafers each) plus WaferPak contactors and Auto Aligners, ship over the next six months to a Taiwan HVM/OSAT partner. That is the production-capacity confirmation the [[Theses/AEHR - Aehr Test Systems]] AI-processor WLBI thesis needed; it does *not* prove the moat vs Teradyne/Advantest as TAM scales. Mental-model triggers: Industry #2 (qualification-gate / production-tool lock-in); [G-13] expectations (order size vs still-small TAM).

## Summary
August 12, 2026 Aehr PR: $22M follow-on from lead AI training/inference processor customer expanding WLBI capacity. Config: fully automated FOX-XP WLBI, FOX WaferPak full-wafer contactors, FOX WaferPak Auto Aligners; each FOX-XP tests/burns nine 300mm wafers simultaneously. Ship next six months to customer’s high-volume manufacturing partner in Taiwan. CEO Gayn Erickson: customer’s current production plans contemplate capacity *beyond this order*; additional semiconductor companies and hyperscalers engaging after positive wafer-level benchmarks. Driver cited: rising value/complexity of advanced AI devices (burn-in economics). No customer name, no HBM vs logic split, no FY guide change in the PR.
