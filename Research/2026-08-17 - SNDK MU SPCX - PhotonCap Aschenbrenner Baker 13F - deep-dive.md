## Summary
PhotonCap’s 17 August 2026 piece is part two of the 1 August *Compute Short, Memory Long* note. Both 13Fs opened on the 14 August deadline (holdings as of 30 June, filed within 45 days). Aggregators were still serving March 31 as “latest”; the author downloaded the info-table XML and checked that implied unit price (reported value ÷ share count) matched the 30 June close for every name — Micron $1,154.29, Ciena $490.56, QQQ $736.40 — so the parse is internally consistent. A 13F is a slice: US-listed equity longs and options only. Shorts, non-US assets, cash, and private stakes drop out. All weights below are on a *reported-value* basis and can overstate the actual book.

Aschenbrenner’s March 31 book was a double structure: long memory and power, with puts draped across semis — ~$2.0B SMH, ~$1.5B NVIDIA, ~$1B each Oracle / Broadcom / AMD, plus Micron, TSMC, ASML, Intel, and Corning, totaling ~$8.4B underlying. By 30 June that sleeve is gone. The only remaining put is Infosys ~$5M. Micron and TSMC flipped from puts to common: 1.72M Micron puts in March become 4.83M Micron common in June. The replacement is two memory names — SanDisk ~$5.7B and Micron ~$5.6B, 55.6% together — then Bloom Energy ~$1.9B, TSMC ~$1.3B, Nebius ~$1.2B, then CoreWeave, Core Scientific, STMicro, Applied Digital, RIOT, IREN. The layout is “buy electricity and memory and compute comes out.” The empty seat is optics: no coherent equipment, no transceivers, no optical DSP, no connectors. PhotonCap’s timing point is that this is the picture *immediately before* the reported July forced liquidation of the public-equity book. People say he blew up; the author notes year-to-date is still above the average person’s, and with hindsight calls the attempt reckless.

Baker’s Atreides reported value jumped $5.0B → $14.3B while holdings fell 54 → 49. The increment is SpaceX: 27.33M shares, ~$4.6B, 32.6% of reported value, more than five times the gap to Micron on a common-stock basis. PhotonCap’s own SPCX read (first earnings / 10GW hardware chain) is that this is an AI company in a space costume, not an orbital-launch bid — and he does not think Baker’s 32% is the launch business either. Second-largest is the QQQ put: ~$2.3B on 3.2M shares, 16.4%, more than doubled on a contract basis from 1.4M at end-March, *while* QQQ ran $577 → $736 (+27.6%) in the second quarter. One AI-infra bull pulled every hedge off; the other doubled the index put. Remaining top names: Micron ~$800M, Cerebras common ~$600M plus ~$100M calls, Meta calls ~$600M plus ~$200M common, Astera Labs ~$500M, Ciena ~$300M, Credo ~$300M, Coherent ~$200M.

The 29 July trough clustered. Versus the 30 June close: QQQ −10.1%, NVIDIA −5.0%, SanDisk −55.3%, Astera Labs −48.3%, Bloom Energy −45.9%, Coherent −43.7%. ## Framework / Mental Model
**Name:** Decision versus drift — the +1.73% cluster, plus the 13F listing-visibility trap.

**Axes.**

| Test | What it is | How it prints in a 13F | This quarter’s instance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decision | Name-by-name judgment: new sleeve, outright exit, or share-count move that does *not* match the book’s common delta | Share-count % change outliers; names that go to zero or appear from zero *and* cannot be explained by a listing | SpaceX 32.6% (caveat: listing), QQQ puts 1.4M → 3.2M, Astera Labs −63.9%, Lumentum → 0, CoreWeave +162%, Palo Alto +56%, Wix +124%, twenty-name ~$1.1B empty |
| Drift / pro-rata | New money, a new account, or a model portfolio allocated across an *existing* sleeve | Many names, same % share-count increase to the second decimal | Fifteen-name +1.73% cluster (Ciena, Coherent, Nokia, Semtech +1.74%, Rogers, Veeco +1.71%, Tesla, Ambiq, ACV Auctions, Compass, Warby Parker, Taboola, BitGo +1.74%, Amazon +1.69%, Nebius +1.68%) |
| Listing visibility | Pre-IPO / private shares that were never in the 13F appear as “new buys” the quarter the security lists | 13F fragment << 13G beneficial; Class B / lock-up stock | Cerebras 3.11M in 13F vs 10.54M / 6.73M Class B on 13G (10.7% of SO); SpaceX 27.33M / ~$4.6B may be the same trap |
| Slice bias | 13F = US-listed longs and options only | Reported-value weights overstate the live book | All % in this note are reported-value; shorts, cash, non-US, private drop out |

**Distance-layer overlay (connection sleeve only).** Scale-up = inside the rack (electrical / SerDes / connectors). Scale-out = optical transceiver zone inside the building. Scale-across = coherent DCI, building to building. Interconnect = the three together. Pure optics = Lumentum + Coherent; systems-with-coherent = Ciena + Nokia. Methodology constraint: layer dollars are the author’s assignment of 13F values, not revenue exposure; a straddler (Coherent) is counted once. Accumulated weight ≠ this quarter’s ticket: Astera Labs value can rise on a 4.4× stock while share count is cut 63.9%.

**How applied.** (1) Diff every 13F line on *share count*, not on reported value or “new vs sold” aggregator flags. (2) Cluster identical % deltas; treat the cluster as non-judgment unless an independent flow print says otherwise. (3) Cross any “new” mega-position against 13G / Class-B / IPO calendar before calling it a buy. (4) Score hedges only on what a 13F actually reports (contract count, not strike/expiry) and on subsequent layer drawdowns, not on “he was hedged.” (5) Do not read a held +1.73% coherent name as a scale-across *decision*.

**Falsifiers the author will re-open on.** Next 13F: Ciena and Coherent again moving with the cluster → inertia, not an opinion on the layer. Lumentum back in → rewrite the drop. Scale-up vs scale-across weights unchanged → July coherent-DCI demand-opening argument gets no support from this fund.
