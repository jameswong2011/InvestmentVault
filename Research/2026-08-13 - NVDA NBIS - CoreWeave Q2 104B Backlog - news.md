# CoreWeave Q2: $2.58B Rev, $104B Backlog, FY Raise; Losses and Debt Climb

## Thesis Delta
Consensus (and yesterday’s login-gated CNBC/Reuters takes) framed CoreWeave as a levered NVDA derivative whose print would either confirm GPU demand or expose credit stress. SiliconAngle’s free recap: Q2 revenue $2.58B (+112%, slight beat vs $2.56B), adj. loss $1.03 vs $1.20 est., *net* loss $626M (vs $290M), backlog $104B *excluding* ~$25B of intra-quarter new commitments, FY rev raised to $12.4–$13.2B, capex raised to $35–$39B, $35B debt. Demand/pricing for [[Theses/NVDA - Nvidia]] sell-through is confirmed; for held [[Theses/NBIS - Nebius Group]] it is a peer print that tightens the “demand de-risked / supply is the bet” split — CRWV is the built-base credit vehicle, NBIS the energize-the-base vehicle. Mental-model triggers: [G-4] Perez frenzy (debt-funded infra); [G-10] neocloud credit base-rate; Industry #1 bottleneck now power/capex not GPUs.
