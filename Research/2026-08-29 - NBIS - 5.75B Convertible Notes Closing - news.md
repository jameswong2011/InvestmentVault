# Nebius Group Announces Closing of Private Offering of Convertible Senior Notes

## Thesis Delta
Consensus still prices the 2026 NBIS funding gap ([[Theses/NBIS - Nebius Group]] Outstanding Q#2 / Insight #2) as an access-to-capital and ATM-dilution problem, treating the August convert as a marketing event until cash is in the account and reading any large raise as the LOW-trigger >15% dilutive window. This close prints $5.75B of senior unsecured original principal actually issued to Rule 144A QIBs (greenshoe taken in full along the $4.5B launch to $5.0B pricing to $5.75B close path), so the residual gap is now a cash-deployment, accretion-cost, and convert-overhang question rather than a 'can they fund' one, while the concurrent $800M 2029/2031 exchange into ~15.8M Class A is immediate share supply the 'debt-first, dilution-deferred' read has to absorb.

## Summary
On 24 August 2026 Nebius Group N.V. (NASDAQ: NBIS) announced the closing of its previously announced private offering of convertible senior notes in two series, 0.50% notes due 2030 and 4.50% notes due 2034, sold to qualified institutional buyers under Rule 144A, with the initial purchasers exercising in full their options to take an additional $450 million original principal of 2030 notes and $300 million of 2034 notes, so that original principal stands at $3.45 billion (2030) plus $2.3 billion (2034) and $5.75 billion in aggregate. The close PR's headline frames that figure as 'aggregate gross proceeds of approximately $5.75 billion'; the body uses 'aggregate original principal amount', which matters because the 20 August 2026 Form 6-K pricing exhibit (the document the close PR points to for terms) had already estimated net proceeds of approximately $4.94 billion at the $5.0 billion priced size, or approximately $5.68 billion if the greenshoe were taken in full, after initial purchasers' discounts, commissions and estimated expenses. The marketing path itself is a demand print: the 19 August launch was $4.5 billion original principal, the same-day pricing 6-K upsized to $5.0 billion ($3.0 billion 2030 + $2.0 billion 2034) and granted a 13-day option of +$450 million / +$300 million, and the 24 August close confirms that option was exercised in full rather than left on the table.

Concurrently with pricing, in separately negotiated transactions with a limited number of holders, the company agreed to exchange $400 million original principal of its 2.00% convertible senior notes due 2029 and $400 million of its 3.00% convertible senior notes due 2031 for an aggregate of approximately 15.8 million Class A ordinary shares (par value €0.01). The close PR states that participating holders may sell those Class A shares in the open market and/or enter into or unwind derivative transactions tied to hedge positions on the existing notes, and that those activities could decrease (or reduce the size of any increase in) the market price of the Class A shares or the trading price of the company's other securities; the pricing exhibit adds that completion of the new-notes offering was not contingent on completion of the exchanges and that the exchanges were expected to settle on or about 24 August 2026. Use of net proceeds is unchanged from the pricing communication: construction and build-out of data centres, investment to develop the full-stack AI cloud, expansion of the data-centre footprint, procurement of key components including GPUs, and general corporate purposes. The notes and any Class A shares deliverable on conversion or in the exchanges have not been and will not be registered under the Securities Act.

The 20 August 6-K exhibit is where the economic terms live. The notes are senior unsecured obligations issued under indentures with U.S. Bank Trust Company, National Association as trustee, and they bear cash interest on original principal at 0.50% (2030) and 4.50% (2034), payable semi-annually in arrears on 15 February and 15 August, first payment 15 February 2027. Initial conversion rates are 3.1902 Class A shares per $1,000 original principal on the 2030 notes (initial conversion price approximately $313.46, a 40.0% premium to the $223.90 last sale on 19 August 2026) and 3.0802 shares per $1,000 on the 2034 notes (approximately $324.65, a 45.0% premium); conversion rate and conversion price are based on original principal, not on the accreted principal amount, and a make-whole fundamental change increases the conversion rate off a customary table. Maturity is 15 February 2030 and 15 February 2034, at which the original principal plus an amount accreted thereon (the Accreted Principal Amount) is payable unless earlier repurchased, redeemed or converted; the accretion schedule is written so that the 2030 notes reach 110% of original principal at maturity and the 2034 notes reach 125%, which the company translates into effective conversion prices at maturity of approximately $344.81 (54.0% premium) and $405.82 (81.3% premium) respectively.

Prior to the business day immediately before the date two months before each series' maturity, conversion is available only on stated conditions and during stated periods, including a last-sale test at or above 130% of the product of the conversion price and the then-applicable ratio of accreted principal to original principal (the Accretion Ratio); from that two-month window the notes are convertible at any time until the second scheduled trading day before maturity. The company settles conversions in cash, Class A shares, or a combination, at its election (subject to Dutch tax-law conditions). Optional redemption is closed until 21 February 2028 (2030 notes) and 21 August 2028 (2034 notes) except for certain tax-law changes; thereafter, and on or before the 30th scheduled trading day before the relevant maturity, the company may redeem for cash if the notes are freely tradable and the last sale has been at or above 130% of (conversion price × accretion ratio) for a specified period, except that 2034 notes redeemed from 21 August 2028 and prior to 21 August 2029 need 150% rather than 130%. Redemption and fundamental-change repurchase prices are the then-accreted principal plus accrued and unpaid interest on original principal; after a redemption notice, holders may convert before the second business day preceding the redemption date, and no make-whole applies on optional or tax redemption. Thus the package the close confirms is not a 0.50%/4.50% coupon story in isolation: cash coupons run on original principal (~$17.25 million per year on the 2030s and ~$103.5 million per year on the 2034s, ~$120.75 million combined [est.]), while a holder to maturity is owed ~$3.795 billion and ~$2.875 billion of accreted principal respectively ($6.67 billion combined against $5.75 billion original, ~$920 million of accretion [est.]), and the 15.8 million exchange shares are live supply against a 30 June 2026 share count of 271.9 million (~5.8% [est.]), before any later conversion of the new notes (~18.1 million shares if converted at the initial rates on the full $5.75 billion, ~6.7% [est.]).

## Contradiction Check
Supports [[Theses/NBIS - Nebius Group]] Insight #2 / Insight #5 and Outstanding Q#2 on the funding-mix path: $5.75B of cash-in unsecured converts (plus the pricing 6-K's ~$5.68B net estimate) is not GPU-collateralized paper, so the → CLOSE if 'funds the capex gap primarily via GPU-collateralized debt' handle does not fire on this print, and the package is sized for data-centre build, AI-cloud stack, footprint and GPU procurement rather than refinance-only. Challenges the cleaner half of that same insight and the 'dilution deferred' consensus gloss: the $800M 2029/2031 exchange into ~15.8M Class A is equity issued now (holders flagged as free to sell and unwind hedges), the new notes add a stated ~18.1M-share conversion overhang at $313–$325 against a stock that had already sold off into the raise, and the LOW if '>15%-dilutive equity/convert raise in any 6-month window' language remains the live audit (definition pending on whether converts count before conversion; Mar–Aug 2026 window). [G-4] / [G-8] read the close as financial-capital funding of the frenzy build and as a capital-allocation mix choice (unsecured convert + share exchange versus ATM versus prepay versus GPU-secured), not as proof that incremental ROIIC on the Rubin vintage ([G-7] Insight #6) has improved; [G-13] the 22% round-trip from the 14 August high already embeds a convert-overhang expectation, so the close confirms cash rather than a new surprise. Compare the unsecured 144A structure to IREN's same-week GPU-financing / Blue Owl secured package. No conviction or status change.
