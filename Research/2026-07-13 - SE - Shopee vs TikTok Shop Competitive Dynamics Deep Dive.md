# SE — Shopee vs TikTok Shop Competitive Dynamics

## Thesis Delta

**TikTok has disproved the weak version of the Shopee moat: regional scale and seller liquidity did not stop TikTok Shop from doubling standalone Southeast Asian GMV to $45.6B in 2025.** The surviving thesis is narrower and more testable. TikTok controls discovery; Shopee controls a denser transaction-and-retention stack spanning search, checkout, SPX, VIP, advertising, and Monee. Shopee can lose discovery share without losing economic leadership if it converts externally generated demand, retains the buyer, and fulfills the repeat order at lower acquisition and delivery cost.

Conviction remains medium. TikTok's acceleration and Shopee's Q1 2026 sales-and-marketing growth of 40.4% versus 30.2% GMV growth raise the cost of defence. Shopee's content orders, buyer frequency, VIP retention, ad growth, and delivery-cost gains show that the spend is building measurable activity rather than only headline share. The new falsifier is TikTok reaching Shopee-like direct-search, repeat frequency, category breadth, and AOV while Shopee's sales-and-marketing expense continues to outgrow GMV.

## Summary

Southeast Asian platform e-commerce grew 22.8% to $157.6B in 2025. Momentum Works estimates Shopee at 53% share and about $83.2B GMV; TikTok Shop generated $45.6B standalone GMV, and TikTok Shop plus Tokopedia reached roughly $54.7B, or 65.7% of Shopee's GMV. Content commerce expanded from 20% to 32% of regional platform GMV in one year. TikTok is not merely taking existing marketplace demand: its creator graph creates demand for products that lacked prior search volume, with the strongest fit in beauty, fashion, wellness, and other demonstrable categories.

Shopee's defence is to make discovery portable rather than beat TikTok at entertainment. Shopee embeds Live and Video in its app, routes YouTube and Facebook creator content into Shopee checkout, then applies its advantage in assortment, price comparison, delivery, buyer protection, loyalty, advertising, and credit. Q1 2026 content-driven orders exceeded 25% of Southeast Asian physical-goods orders and grew above 50% YoY. SPX processed more than 30M parcels per day in 2025; fulfilment and instant-delivery data show faster delivery can scale while unit cost falls. VIP passed 10M members, retained more than 80%, and generated about 20% of Asian GMV. These metrics support a transaction-layer moat, but Sea has not disclosed contribution after vouchers, free shipping, creator commissions, and selection bias.

The likely structure is a split-layer duopoly with merchant multi-homing. TikTok wins a rising share of discovery-led GMV and seeks to convert that traffic into search and repeat purchasing. Shopee rents discovery from multiple social graphs and seeks to own checkout, fulfilment, repayment, and the second purchase. Merchant listings are not exclusive, so the scarce assets are buyer habit, creator-product matching, conversion data, fulfilment density, and economically positive repeat cohorts.

## Framework / Mental Model

The `/Mental Models` reading protocol treats the table below as hypotheses to test, not conclusions. Agreement across network-effects, value-layer, and cohort lenses triggers the bear-case hunt: TikTok direct-search/repeat growth is the single datapoint most likely to falsify Shopee's claimed transaction-layer durability.

| Funnel layer | Data that compounds | Current controller | Hypothesis to test | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Interest formation | Watch time, saves, creator affinity, emerging trends | TikTok | Creator-product matching creates demand faster than marketplace search | TikTok content growth fails to produce repeat or non-affiliate orders |
| Search and comparison | Queries, clicks, price sensitivity, catalogue depth | Shopee | Habit and assortment keep planned purchases on Shopee | TikTok Shop-tab/direct-search share reaches Shopee-like frequency |
| Checkout and trust | Conversion, returns, disputes, payment behaviour | Shopee | Transaction history raises conversion and reduces fraud/servicing cost | TikTok matches conversion and trust without higher incentives |
| Fulfilment | Route density, warehouse placement, failed delivery, parcel cost | Shopee/SPX | Local physical density is harder to copy than software | TikTok/partners match speed and cost across the same countries |
| Retention | Buyer cohorts, frequency, VIP renewal, cross-category spend | Shopee | VIP and faster delivery turn acquired buyers into higher-contribution cohorts | Retention stays high only while benefits exceed incremental margin |
| Creator monetization | Affiliate ROAS, content supply, product demonstration | TikTok | Native creator economics remain the cheapest source of incremental demand | Shopee's multi-platform affiliate links match acquisition cost |
| Payments and credit | Repayment, limits, merchant cash flow, fraud | Shopee/Monee | Credit raises checkout conversion and deepens repeat economics | Loss-adjusted returns or regulation erase the conversion benefit |
| Agent interface | Intent, delegated choice, preferred merchant/fulfiller | Unsettled | Agents may commoditize TikTok discovery while preserving Shopee execution | Agents own checkout and route around Shopee's transaction layer |

**Mental-model trigger change:** Value Layer Monopoly §1 moves from a weak marketplace-network framing to a medium-fit split-layer framing. TikTok owns a monetizable discovery layer; Shopee can still own the transaction/retention layer. The modifier strengthens only if Shopee's repeat contribution rises with sales and marketing growing no faster than GMV.

### 5. Merchant economics and multi-homing

TikTok Vietnam's current fee stack includes a 6% transaction fee, a 14% default marketplace commission from 3 July 2026, a VND3,000 order-processing fee, shipping adjustments, and affiliate commissions. Shopee Malaysia's terms combine transaction, category commission, platform-support, programme, cross-border, shipping, and tax deductions. Posted commission rates therefore understate the cost of an order on either platform. Merchants will allocate inventory, creator commissions, and advertising to the channel with the best incremental contribution and order velocity, then use direct commerce where customer ownership justifies the extra operational burden.

## Contradiction Check

| Claim at risk | Contrary evidence | Resolution standard |
|---|---|---|
| “Shopee's scale is a moat” | TikTok doubled GMV despite Shopee's incumbent scale | Scale counts only if it produces better repeat contribution and lower fulfilment/acquisition cost |
| “TikTok is only impulse beauty/fashion” | Electronics is already a leading TikTok category; Vietnam share reached ~39% | Track direct-search orders, AOV, repeat rate, and non-demonstrable category mix rather than category labels |
| “Shopee has logistics; TikTok does not” | TikTok uses regional LSPs and Tokopedia/GoTo assets; J&T parcel growth is rapid | Compare like-for-like delivery speed, failed-delivery rate, cost/order, and fulfilment penetration by country |
| “Shopee content neutralizes TikTok” | TikTok owns the native entertainment graph; Shopee rents YouTube/Meta traffic | Shopee must show stable acquisition cost and repeat contribution on externally sourced cohorts |
| “VIP retention proves loyalty” | Discounts can manufacture renewal and spending uplift; selection bias is unmeasured | Require 6/12/24-month contribution after benefits versus matched non-VIP buyers |
| “TikTok's faster GMV means it wins” | Shopee remains larger, is growing in absolute terms, and monetizes ads/transactions while profitable | Judge economics per repeat buyer, not gross share alone |
| “Seller fees create pricing power” | Merchants multi-home and rising platform cuts make direct commerce more attractive | Track seller ROAS, inventory allocation, churn, and price gaps across platforms |
| “Agentic commerce helps Shopee” | Agents could also own checkout and commoditize marketplace search | Shopee benefits only if agents call its fulfilment/merchant/credit rails without taking the transaction layer |

Data disagreement is itself a warning. Momentum Works reports Shopee at 53% of the full platform market and TikTok Shop plus Tokopedia at 65.7% of Shopee GMV; Cube reports 59% and 31% within a three-platform denominator. J&T parcel volumes cover customers beyond TikTok, while SPX volumes are tied more closely to Shopee. None of these datasets discloses platform contribution margin, TikTok direct-search share, or matched buyer cohorts. The analysis therefore uses share as a directional signal and makes unit economics the decision variable.
