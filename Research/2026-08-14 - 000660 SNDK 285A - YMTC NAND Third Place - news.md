# YMTC Takes #3 NAND Shipments at 14% in Q2; Still Behind on Revenue; CXMT DRAM 7%

## Thesis Delta
Consensus still treats Chinese memory as a 2028–29 CXMT DRAM story ([[Theses/000660 - SK Hynix]] Insight #4; [[Theses/MU - Micron Technology]] CLOSE is *qualified HBM*) — this 13 August 2026 CNBC Beijing piece, citing Counterpoint, puts Yangtze Memory (YMTC) third in *NAND shipments* in Q2 at 14%, ahead of Micron and Kioxia, behind Samsung and SK hynix, while still trailing Micron and Kioxia on NAND *revenue* because mix is consumer not data center. Hwang: 15% share is the capex self-funding floor; YMTC “projected to pull further ahead in 2027 and 2028.” CXMT (separate Counterpoint) held 7% of DRAM in Q2, fourth behind Samsung, SK hynix, and Micron. Markets: DRAM ~$100B and NAND $46B in Q1. SK hynix is “resuming investment” at Dalian after a four-year pause (Korean media; company did not comment) — already covered in the 12 August Solidigm/Dalian ingest. 000660 → CLOSE is CXMT *qualified HBM*, not YMTC NAND shipments. 285A is monitoring; this is a share-rank print, not a BiCS roadmap print.

## Summary
Counterpoint data released Wednesday: YMTC third globally in NAND units in Q2, beating Micron and Kioxia. It had briefly beaten Kioxia a year ago, then fell back. Hwang says Q2 third place “carries significant weight” because 2027–28 pull-away is the path, and 15% is the minimum to fund own capex. YMTC is preparing a mainland IPO after CXMT’s Shanghai debut. Despite unit rank, revenue rank is still fifth-ish — consumer mix, while data center is expected to take half of NAND bits by end-2026. CNBC’s second key-point bullet (“YMTC… fourth in DRAM”) is flagged in the extract as a likely YMTC/CXMT mix-up; the body attributes DRAM #4 / 7% to CXMT.
