# Lumentum Q4 FY26: $1.01B Print, Guides $1.25B Midpoint a Quarter Early

## Thesis Delta
Street already prices [[Theses/LITE - Lumentum]] as the 200G-EML chokepoint and treats the $1.25B quarterly / ~40% non-GAAP OM "target model" as a late-FY27 event (thesis still dates $2B/qtr to late 2027) → this August 11, 2026 Business Wire print is $1,006.3M Q4 FY26 and a Q1 FY27 guide of $1.225–$1.275B (mid $1.25B) at 39.5–40.5% OM, which CEO Michael Hurlston says reaches the target model more than a quarter ahead, with OCS, 1.6T cloud modules, ultra-high-power CPO lasers, an initial ELS-module order, and NPO engagements named as incremental in-rack TAM rather than pluggable death. Mixed for [[Theses/AAOI - Applied Optoelectronics]] (1.6T module demand vs merchant-EML dependence) and [[Theses/IQE - IQE]] (InP epi pull-through); hypotheses [G-13] expectations pulled forward, [G-6] realized mix/pricing in 50.4% non-GAAP GM, Semis #1/#8 bottleneck plus architecture remap, VLM §1/§3 EML as infrastructure layer.

## Summary
Lumentum's August 11, 2026 Business Wire (also mirrored at MarketMinute) is the official IR tape for the fiscal fourth quarter and full year ended June 27, 2026 — not the post-call product-mix calendar in [[Research/2026-08-12 - LITE PhotonCap FQ4 2026 First 1B Quarter - deep-dive]]. The wire's claim is operating, not narrative: Q4 revenue printed $1,006.3M (+24.5% QoQ / +109.3% YoY vs $808.4M / $480.7M), non-GAAP GM 50.4% and OM 36.6%, and Q1 FY27 is guided $1.225–$1.275B with non-GAAP OM 39.5–40.5% and non-GAAP EPS $4.05–$4.35. Hurlston's mechanism is that AI compute is raising speed and bandwidth until architects use optical links as primary connectivity; Q4 traction is "broad-based," while OCS and the cloud-module book (1.6T adoption) are "beginning to layer in," and ultra-high-power CPO lasers plus a first ELS-module order plus a breadth of NPO engagements are "the first signs that optics are starting to penetrate in-rack connectivity, significantly upping our optical TAM." The guide sentence is the stock-relevant close: "our Q1 revenue guidance midpoint [is] $1.25 billion, reaching our target model more than a quarter ahead of schedule."

GAAP is not the operating story. Q4 GAAP net loss is $7.2B, or $84.65 per diluted share, versus GAAP net income of $144.2M / $1.50 in Q3 FY26 and $213.3M / $2.96 in Q4 FY25. The hole is a one-time non-cash $7.8B loss on debt extinguishment from equitizing convertible notes ($7,756.6M on the selected P&L). Non-GAAP Q4 net income is $326.3M / $3.23 (101.1M diluted shares) versus $225.7M / $2.37 prior quarter and $63.3M / $0.88 year-ago. Full-year FY26 revenue is $3,014.0M vs FY25 $1,645.0M (+83.2%); GAAP FY26 net loss $6.9B / $92.96 is the same extinguishment; non-GAAP FY26 net income is $782.3M / $8.67 versus $146.4M / $2.06. Thesis Key Metrics still carried FY2026E $2.94B — the wire beats that estimate by ~$74M.

Both reported product types more than doubled year-over-year, and Systems outgrew Components: Q4 Components $649.4M (64.5% of sales, +21.8% QoQ / +102.7% YoY) versus Systems $356.9M (35.5%, +29.7% / +122.6%). FY26 Components $2,005.6M (+79.7% y/y); Systems $1,008.4M (+90.7%). Incremental Q4 dollars drop through at high rates: non-GAAP operating income $368.8M on $1,006.3M versus an implied ~$260M at Q3's 32.2% OM on $808.4M, or ~55% incremental OM on the +$197.9M sequential step [est.]. Cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments are $2.7B ($2,043.5M cash + $694.9M STI), down $433.9M QoQ and up $1.9B from FY25 year-end. The balance-sheet companion to the $7.8B P&L charge is a capital-structure reset: current portion of long-term debt $1,596.9M vs $10.6M year-ago, long-term debt $40.5M vs $2,562.6M, additional paid-in capital $12,430.1M vs $1,986.8M, stockholders' equity $4,643.9M vs $1,134.7M. Working-capital and capacity build sit underneath the print: AR $520.3M vs $250.0M, inventories $691.6M vs $470.1M, PP&E $1,159.1M vs $726.4M, total assets $7,307.5M vs $4,218.7M. Adjusted EBITDA is $406.4M in Q4 and $1,025.8M for FY26. Conference call was August 11, 2026 at 2:00 pm PT; materials at investor.lumentum.com.

Scope limit: this clip names CPO lasers, ELS, NPO, OCS and 1.6T as growth drivers but does not disclose EML lane-speed mix, OCS dollar run-rate, pump-laser allocation, or the dated HVM calendar. Those live in the PhotonCap FQ4 deep-dive and the Irrational Analysis call note; do not treat this wire as independent confirmation of call-only figures.

## Contradiction Check
**Supports [[Theses/LITE - Lumentum]] §Summary and §Bull Case (margin inflection / operating leverage)** — first $1B quarter is now a print ($1,006.3M) and Q1 FY27 mid $1.25B at 39.5–40.5% non-GAAP OM pulls the OFC target model forward by more than a quarter. The thesis still dates a $2B quarterly run-rate to late 2027; this wire does not claim $2B, it claims 40% OM at half that revenue. LITE has no formal Conviction Triggers section (thesis Mental Models already flags that gap); the closest pre-committed object is Outstanding Question 1, already marked resolved-partial on 2026-08-12 via the PhotonCap note.

**Supports LITE §Key Non-consensus Insights (OCS durability; SiPh/CPO as InP-laser demand) and simultaneously keeps §Bear Case (CPO margin compression / contestable CW-ELS layer) live.** The wire's CPO/ELS/NPO language is incremental-TAM framing, not a disclosure that ELS margins replace Cloud Light module margins. No ASP, no ELS GM, no CW-vs-EML split — so Outstanding Question "how does Lumentum's role change under CPO" is named, not closed. [Semis #8] remapping EML → CW/ELS remains a hypothesis; [Semis #2] qualification-gate on 200G EML is not re-proven by a revenue table; [Semis #6] peak-margin non-reversion is the claim the 50.4% / 36.6% print invites and [G-10] base rates (few firms hold 80%+ growth plus 2,000 bps OM expansion) run against.

**Supports LITE §Business Model Systems mix and Challenges the "Components-only arms dealer" caricature.** Systems 35.5% of Q4 and +29.7% QoQ (faster than Components) is the OCS + Cloud Light 1.6T layer Hurlston names. That is bullish for the Cloud Light $1.3B FY2027 Outstanding Question *if* mix is high-ASP 1.6T; it is the exact share AAOI's merchant-assembly book is fighting for.

**Challenges [[Theses/AAOI - Applied Optoelectronics]] §Summary (merchant 200G EML + 1.6T assembler) and leans on Conviction Trigger → LOW (Microsoft LPO volume shifting to Cloud Light / Coherent) plus Risk #4 / Bear (CPO disintermediates pluggables).** LITE naming 1.6T cloud-module adoption and an ELS-module order is demand confirmation for the speed tier AAOI is building 500–700K units/month against, and a vertical-integration threat if Cloud Light takes the same hyperscaler wallet. The wire does not name AAOI, Microsoft, or ASPs — it is not a CLOSE-trigger print. [VLM §2] layer-renter read on AAOI is unchanged: LITE's guide is the chokepoint layer printing; AAOI remains middle-assembly.

**Supports [[Theses/IQE - IQE]] §Summary InP-demand overlay and §Key Non-consensus Insight (SiPh paradox) only as a second-order option.** Faster LITE Systems + CPO/ELS/NPO language raises III-V laser-source wafer demand; the wire has zero epi, substrate, or IQE content. Outstanding Question 4 (how much InP epi IQE actually captures) is untouched. [G-8] capital allocation on LITE is the convert equitization + APIC jump + $433.9M QoQ cash decline — a structure event, not an IQE catalyst.
