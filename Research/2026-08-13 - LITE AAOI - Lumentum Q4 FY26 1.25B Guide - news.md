# Lumentum Q4 FY26: $1.01B Print, Guides $1.25B Midpoint a Quarter Early

## Thesis Delta
Street already treats LITE as the 200G-EML chokepoint; the live debate is *when* the $1.25B quarterly / 40% OM model lands and whether CPO cannibalizes pluggables. Print: Q4 FY26 revenue $1.01B, non-GAAP GM 50.4% / OM 36.6%; Q1 FY27 guide $1.225–$1.275B (mid $1.25B) with non-GAAP OM 39.5–40.5% — management says the target model is *more than a quarter ahead*. Named growth drivers: OCS, 1.6T cloud modules, ultra-high-power CPO lasers, initial ELS-module order, NPO engagements. Directly supports [[Theses/LITE - Lumentum]] scarcity/operating-leverage; mixed for [[Theses/AAOI - Applied Optoelectronics]] (1.6T module demand vs EML-merchant dependence) and [[Theses/IQE - IQE]] epi. Mental-model triggers: Industry #1 (optics bottleneck) + #8 (CPO/NPO remaps interconnect); [G-6] pricing power in sold-out EML; [G-13] expectations (model pulled forward).
