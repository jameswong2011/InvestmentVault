# NBIS: Rubin hour, 5GW funding, and why Nscale $7.3 does not reprice the 2027 book

## Thesis Delta

Consensus (the 22 August essay, and the tape that treated the Nscale–Anthropic $45bn print as a Rubin clearing price) assumes a going-forward cash rate near $12/GPU-hr is both contracted and sufficient to make a 5GW programme self-funding after two buildings, so that 2028–29 EV/EBIT compresses toward ~5× without another primary; the only named Vera Rubin offtake on the tape, Nscale–Anthropic at Monarch, back-solves to ~$7.3/hr and would falsify that if it were the 2027 market. The session's argument is that $7.3 is six-year late-2027 BTM-gas IPO-anchor paper after Microsoft walked a 1.35 GW LOI, ~20–30% below [[Theses/NBIS - Nebius Group]]'s 1–3 year 2026–27 ACV/MW on a cash-rate basis and closer once duration is adjusted (Nscale TCV/MW ~$98m versus ~$60–75m for a three-year $20–25m/MW print), so the working going-forward rate is $10, not $12 and not $7.3. Conviction unchanged (medium): the load-bearing binary remains YE2026 energisation and the LOW dilution definition, not a re-mark of the 2027 book to Anthropic's Nscale hour. HIGH/CLOSE no-touch. Do not import $7.3 into Insight #6 as a disclosed Rubin cash rate.

## Summary

The essay's $12 row on a Foxconn-all-in $47bn/GW Rubin machine, 50% customer prefunding of capex, and a 0→5GW cadence of one gigawatt a year is a two-year equity bridge of ~$33bn at a standing start, not a $117bn lifetime claim on the share count, because two live gigawatts of cash EBITDA (~$19bn/GW-year at $12) more than fund the $23.5bn net cheque for the next building once deferred-revenue amortisation is charged, and the franchise is self-funding from the third building onward; Nebius as it actually sits, with ~1GW guided live by YE2026 and ~$8bn of Q2 cash (~$13.7bn pro forma the 24 August converts), needs $0–5bn incremental equity at that $12 row, which is the [G-7] mechanism in [[Website/2026-08-22 - Neoclouds - The Property Developers of Compute.md]] showing up as a funding statement rather than a multiple. Prefunding is a loan against future rent, not a gift ([[Macro & Technology/AI Datacenter Financing Mechanism Design]]): the $23.5bn advance is ~17% of five-year TCV, later collections stay fat, and treating the advance as permanent capital overstates how much of later EBITDA is still cash by about $5bn at the trough. The $9 row is the actual falsifier of that programme, not the funding structure: below ~$8.22 (seven-year) / $9.27 (five-year) at 8,410 hours the single GPU misses a 15% unlevered hurdle, and at $9 a 5GW build never closes inside five years even with 50% customer money.

The only named Vera Rubin cash-rate print we can back-solve is not a Nebius contract. Nscale's $45bn, six-year Anthropic offtake against ~460 MW of Vera Rubin at Monarch, West Virginia, is ~$16.3m/MW-year and ~$7.3/GPU-hr if 460 MW is IT load at Rubin density (256,831 GPUs/GW at 3.9 kW), ~$7.5 at the fleet model's 8,410 hours, with TCV of ~$98m/MW; at $7.3 the Rubin GPU earns 10.8–12.2% IRR on a five-/seven-year life and 14.2% on Nscale's own six-year term, all below the 15% hurdle, and a 1GW→5GW programme at that rate needs ~$17bn of equity from a $0 cash start, ~$9bn from Q2's $8bn, ~$3.5bn from the $13.7bn convert pro forma. That print is not the 2027 clearing price for [[Theses/NBIS - Nebius Group]]. The live book is still the Microsoft wholesale vintage at ~$3.35/hr and ~$12m/MW on a ~0.22–0.26 GW, ~100k-GPU base against $3.0bn ARR, which is ~$12–13bn revenue/GW and ~$6–7bn adj. EBITDA/GW (49.7% on ARR) and ~$2bn EBIT/GW after ~$1.0bn/yr of group D&A: origination IRR ~17% on the ~$70k Blackwell that was actually spent, ~25% blended with cheap Hopper, and a replacement-cost Blackwell at $3.35 fails. The future signed book, Q2 landmarks at $20–25m/MW ACV, one-to-three years, 50–60% prepaid, 22-month payback, converts to $9–11/hr if the MW are Rubin and $5–7.5 if they are GB300; five-year IRR 18–27% on Rubin, three-year then 55% re-rent ~12% at $9 Rubin. There is still no named Rubin cash rate on a Nebius contract.

We therefore take $10 as the working going-forward rate, inside the signed ACV/MW band if the 2027 MW are Rubin and above both the 15% hurdle and Nscale's duration-adjusted print, and below the essay's $12 contracted anchor. From 1GW live and billing, 1GW/year to 5GW, 50% prefund, unlevered, pre-tax: peak additional equity is $7.8bn if YE2026 cash is $0 and $0 if the $8bn (or the $13.7bn pro forma) is still on the sheet; group EBIT prints $5.3 / $13.2 / $23.7 / $36 / $48bn across 2027–31 as the fleet seasons, and EV/EBIT on a ~$62bn EV held EV-neutral through any raise compresses ~12× / 4.7× / 2.6× / 1.7× / 1.3×. Cash EBITDA per GW is flat at $15.7bn in years 1–5; the EBIT lift in years 4–5 (to $12.3bn/GW from $5.3bn in year 1) is the front-loaded rack schedule rolling off ($40.7k → $13.6k of D&A per GPU), not a higher rent, and year 6 at 55% re-rent is back to ~$5.2bn EBIT/GW. GAAP will be milder than the model (server life stretched 4→5 years in 2026; not 30% in year 1). That mature contracted-GPU EBIT/GW rhymes with today's hyperscaler operating-income per operating IT gigawatt (AWS ~$9.2bn, Azure ~$8.3bn, GCP ~$6.8bn on Q1 2026 operating IT of ~6.2 / 5.4–5.5 / 4.7 GW, calendar Q2 / Microsoft FY26 Q4 run-rates), which is the rhyme that tempts a 'new GPU halls earn what old cloud earns' read; the mechanism is depreciation age and software mix, not hardware efficiency. A new hyperscaler AI GW at the Foxconn sticker would look like Nebius year 1 ($5.3bn EBIT at $10), not AWS's $9bn blend of depreciated halls, Workspace-class SaaS, and reserved IaaS, and it is not fair to mark a 2027 Rubin building to that blend.

Nscale–Anthropic does not reprice the 2027 Nebius book because it is a different object. First tokens, if the date holds, are 4Q 2027, with the 460 MW filled across 2028; the remaining two of three Phase 1 buildings are 2028, Nscale's own site language is Phase 1 early 2028, and Caterpillar's 2 GW of G3500 behind-the-meter gas is 1H 2028 against 864 gensets, so slip is the base rate and the local West Virginia 'January / early 2027' language is builder spin. Microsoft had an LOI for 1.35 GW at the same campus and walked (portfolio review, on-site generation); Google was in talks; Anthropic filled the hole as the binding offtake (Aker disclosure) and the largest line in Nscale's $51bn backlog ahead of a September IPO. Primary discount is time-to-power (late-2027/2028 MW versus 2026 prompt MW), secondary is a six-year lock across Rubin Ultra (H2 2027) and Feynman (2028), not 'Rubin is dead at the first billed hour'. Part of the remaining cash-rate gap is execution risk Anthropic chose to eat; it is not charity to a random entrant (Nvidia / Dell / Nokia / Aker on the cap table; the same megawatts competed by Microsoft and Google). Working assumption: do not mark [[Theses/NBIS - Nebius Group]]'s 2027 book to $7.3. [[Theses/NBIS - Nebius Group]] is Full weight (25%+) in [[Live Portfolio]]; a $17bn common issue against a ~$60bn capitalisation would trip the LOW >15%-dilutive-raise trigger, which is why the rate that is actually signed, and the cash that is already on the sheet, are the variables, not Anthropic's Nscale hour.

## Framework / Mental Model

**Name.** Rubin hour, EBIT per GW, and the duration-adjusted offtake.

**Components.**

1. **Three prices, not one.** Live wholesale (the Microsoft vintage still on the P&L), future-signed ACV/MW (the Q2 $20–25m/MW landmarks), and a named Rubin GPU-hour (Nscale–Anthropic). Converting ACV/MW to $/GPU-hr requires an assumed generation density; Rubin at 3.9 kW and 256,831 GPUs/GW is a different hour from GB300. Mixing IREN–Microsoft GB300 TCV (~$2.91/hr) with a Rubin $12 essay row is the same error as marking Nebius 2027 to Nscale $7.3.
2. **Prefunding is deferred revenue.** A 50% of capex advance is ~17% of five-year TCV at $12, received as construction cash, amortised over the contract. Cash in a year is cash EBITDA + new advances − amortisation of old advances. Peak cash trough, unlevered, is the implied equity raise. Treating the advance as permanent capital understates later cash leakage and overstates how 'self-funding' the franchise is.
3. **Hurdle is unlevered IRR, not accounting ROIC.** 15% unlevered at 8,410 hours is $8.22 (7-year) / $9.27 (5-year). Accounting ROIC (average EBIT / original $183k) prints lower because D&A is front-loaded on racks ($126k: 30% / 22% / 14% then 8.5% of the residual 34% over N−3) and straight-line on shell ($57k over 20 years). EBITDA hides the doubled Rubin depreciation ([G-11]); year-4/5 EBIT/GW is a D&A artefact, not a rent artefact.
4. **EBIT/GW is not a hardware-efficiency score.** Hyperscaler cloud operating income per *operating IT* gigawatt is a mixed SaaS/IaaS, mixed-vintage number on a depreciated estate. Controlled/leased footprint (Jefferies/Aterio AWS ~10.6 GW versus ~6.2 GW IT) is a different denominator. A new Foxconn-sticker AI GW at a hyperscaler would print Nebius year-1 EBIT/GW, not AWS's $9bn blend.
5. **Duration and COD reprice the hour.** A six-year late-2027 BTM-gas print is not a one-to-three-year 2026–27 print. TCV/MW is the duration-adjusted comparison (~$98m Nscale versus ~$60–75m for three-year $20–25m). Time-to-power is the first discount; generation lock across Ultra/Feynman is the second; 'the first billed hour is last-gen' is not the claim.

**Methodology.** Hold capex, hours, opex, life, and prefund at the fleet-model defaults ([[Research/2026-08-05 - NBIS CRWV - Rubin Fleet Economics - Rate Sensitivity Model - deep-dive]]); vary only cash rate and starting GW/cash. Implied equity is the peak trough. Per-GW EBIT is cash EBITDA minus the D&A schedule, not a margin applied to revenue. Offtake prints convert TCV → $/MW-year → $/GPU-hr only after stating the IT-versus-facility MW assumption and the hours convention (essay 8,760 versus model 8,410).

## Key Segments

### The $12 programme is a two-year equity bridge

Fifty per cent of $235bn is the operator's gross share of construction, and it is the number people reach for when they ask how much equity a 5GW Rubin fleet 'costs', but it is the wrong question once gigawatt two is partly paid for by gigawatt one's $19bn of cash EBITDA and gigawatt three is paid for by two live gigawatts, so that by year 3 the run-rate cash from live MW exceeds the $23.5bn net cheque for the next one after amortisation. That is [G-7] (ROIIC × a long runway of places to put it) as a funding statement: the franchise is self-funding from the third building onward at $12 and 50% prefund, and equity is a bridge across buildings one and two, a peak cheque of ~$33bn at a standing start, mid-year billing $23bn, stacked delay $47bn. A developer-cheque view that treats prepay as permanent capital (the essay's '60-cent dollars', no amortisation) prints a $28bn trough, so the deferred-revenue correction is $5bn, not a different answer. Nebius is not a standing start: ~170MW live at YE2025, ~$8bn cash at Q2, ~$13.7bn pro forma the 24 August converts, guided ~1GW connected by YE2026 then >1GW/year from 2027 ([[Theses/NBIS - Nebius Group]]; [[Research/2026-08-13 - NBIS NVDA - Nebius Q2 5GW Power Target - news]]), so the residual hole at $12 once 1GW is live and billing is $0–5bn, and 2026 paper already issued (~$15bn of equity and quasi-equity plus the $775m ABL) is the same order as that hole. A $33bn common-stock raise against a ~$60bn capitalisation is ~35% of post-money and would trip the LOW >15% dilutive-equity trigger if done as a single marketed issue; the cash path only needs ~$24bn in year 1 and ~$9bn in year 2 of a standing-start programme, and the firm has already shown it will fill that shape with premium converts (40–45% above spot) rather than cash equity. Contract-secured facilities at SOFR+2.50% (the July print) would take residual equity to zero; GPU-collateralised debt of the [[Theses/CRWV - CoreWeave]] type is the thing Insight #5 / CLOSE say would collapse the quality spread, and this maths does not require it.

Several things the $33bn still assumes run against the name. Microsoft's original 40% of TCV is only ~$51k/GPU, 28% of Rubin capex not 50%, and the January 2026 add-ons were 0% upfront, so 50% of capex is the Q2 new-deal claim, not the installed Microsoft book, and a programme that silently substitutes the Microsoft vintage's prepay into 2027 Rubin halls is mixing two contracts. A 21% tax on EBIT would deepen the trough by several billion (the model is pre-tax, as the essay tables are), which matters more at $10 than at $12 because the cash-EBITDA-to-sticker ratio is the whole self-funding property. Holding 5GW of powered capacity past year 5 starts to eat refresh capex as the first Rubin cohort ages out of its contract, and $47bn/GW is frozen Rubin-vintage: Feynman-era memory and a second HBM squeeze would lift the sticker faster than $12 lifts the rent, which is the Insight #6 memory-surge point applied to the funding statement rather than to the ROIC table. The reference-class check ([G-10]) is that developers do not ordinarily self-fund 5GW in five years; this one does in the model only because the contracted cash yield (~40% of capital per year at $12) has no analogue in ordinary property, and because the tenant is putting up half the building. If either term normalises, the $33bn becomes the $117bn people think they are looking at, and the 2028–29 ~5× EBIT multiple in the essay is competed away by issuance rather than by the rate. [[Theses/NBIS - Nebius Group]] is Full weight (25%+) in [[Live Portfolio]], so a marketed common issue of even the $7.8bn $10 residual, let alone the $17bn $7.3 residual, is not a modelling footnote: it is the LOW trigger and a book-level NAV event.

### Nscale $7.3 fails the hurdle and the 5GW funding, and it is the only named Rubin hour

The question that follows from the $12 programme is whether $12 is a contracted rate or an essay rate, and the only public Vera Rubin offtake large enough to back-solve is Nscale–Anthropic: $45bn over six years against ~460 MW at Monarch ([[Research/2026-08-27 - NVDA NBIS - Nscale 45B Vera Rubin Anthropic - news]]). $16.3m/MW-year at Rubin density is ~$7.3/hr at 8,760 hours and ~$7.5 at 8,410, which sits below the 15% unlevered line on every life we use ($8.22 at seven years, $9.27 at five) and earns 14.2% even if one grants Nscale its own six-year term. From 1GW live, a 4GW add at $7.3 and 50% prefund still needs ~$17bn of equity with $0 cash, ~$9bn with Q2 cash, ~$3.5bn with convert pro forma: the self-funding-from-building-three property dies, because $7.3 cash EBITDA per GW is no longer 40% of the $47bn sticker. That is the bear case of the essay made concrete, and it is why the print cannot be ignored; it is also why the next question has to be whether the print is Nebius's market.

Two conversions have to be kept honest or the $7.3 hour is a false precision. First, hours: the essay's $12 tables use 8,760, the fleet model uses 8,410 (96% utilisation), and $7.3 versus $7.5 is that gap, not a different contract. Second, MW: 460 MW as IT at 3.9 kW is the *lowest* implied hour; if 460 MW is facility power at a 70% IT fraction the hour moves to ~$10.4 and the 'fails 15%' read is no longer clean, which is why we keep $7.3 labelled as the conservative IT-load case and not as a disclosed GPU-hour. Nscale has not published GPUs, take-or-pay versus best-efforts, or a cash rate; Bloomberg/Reuters/Crypto Briefing give TCV, term, MW, and generation. The hour is ours.

The COD path is the other half of why this paper is cheap. Rubin itself is an H2 2026 Nvidia product; Rubin Ultra is H2 2027; Feynman is 2028. A contract that starts billing in late 2027 and runs six years is selling hours that will be two generations behind the then-current rack for most of the term, which is a duration discount, not a statement that the first billed hour is already obsolete (the user-corrected reading). Microsoft's 1.35 GW LOI at the same campus walked on portfolio review and on-site generation; Google was in talks (Semafor); Anthropic became the binding offtake on Aker's disclosure and the largest line in a $51bn backlog ahead of a September IPO. Caterpillar's 2 GW of G3500 BTM gas, ~864 gensets, 1H 2028, is the physical constraint under the 'late 2027' chip language. Local West Virginia PR that talks January / early 2027 is builder spin against that gas date. First tokens if the date holds: 4Q 2027; 460 MW filled across 2028; slip is the base rate. That is time-to-power as the primary discount, six-year lock as the secondary, and an IPO-anchor as the third; it is not a 2026 prompt-MW print.

### Nebius's actual book is two prices, neither $12 nor $7.3

The live P&L is still a cheap, depreciated, mostly-Hopper-and-early-Blackwell fleet sold wholesale to Microsoft at ~$3.35/hr, which is a high origination IRR on capital that was actually spent (~$70k/GPU) and a failing IRR on replacement-cost Blackwell, and which produces ~$2bn of EBIT per live GW only because the D&A stock is small relative to a $3.0bn ARR on ~0.25 GW. Using that $2bn as a run-rate for Rubin halls is the same category error as using AWS's $9bn OI/GW as a run-rate for a 2027 NVL72 campus. The future signed book is disclosed as ACV/MW, not as a GPU-hour: Q2's four >$1bn TCVs at $20–25m/MW, one-to-three years, 50–60% prepaid, 22-month payback. If those MW are Rubin at 3.9 kW the implied hour is $9–11, which clears 15% on a five-year take-or-pay (18–27% IRR) and is tight on a three-year then 55% re-rent (~12% at the $9 floor of the band). If those MW are GB300 the implied hour is $5–7.5, which is a different generation and a different capex sticker, and which is why ACV/MW is the honest commercial print and GPU-hour is the conversion that smuggles a density assumption. There is still no named Rubin cash rate on a Nebius contract; Insight #6's falsifier (disclosed Rubin cash $/hour + coverage + vintage) has not fired. $10 sits inside the Rubin-equivalent of the signed ACV/MW band, above the hurdle, below the essay, and above Nscale's cash hour; it is a working assumption, not a print.

### The $10 working case: funding and the multiple the essay promised

From 1GW live, 1GW/year, 50% prefund, unlevered, pre-tax, the peak additional equity is $7.8bn if cash is zero and zero if Q2 cash is still there, which is the difference between tripping the LOW dilution trigger with a marketed issue and not. Group EBIT $5.3 → $48bn over 2027–31 is the compounding the essay sold at $12, delayed one turn of rate and still producing EV/EBIT of ~4.7× in 2028 and ~2.6× in 2029 on a ~$62bn EV held EV-neutral, which is the same disbelief-multiple the essay read at $12 (~6.5× / ~5×) and which [G-13] says the price is already trying to decide. The raise being EV-neutral is a modelling convenience, not a prediction that a $7.8bn issue would not move the stock; it is the statement that *if* the 1GW lands and *if* the $10 band holds and *if* cash is not zero, the residual primary is not the 5GW story. 2026 capex is still Blackwell-heavy at a guided $20–25bn against ~$8bn cash, so the 2026 window can still force paper even if the 2027–31 Rubin programme does not; that is Outstanding Q#2, not this model's 1GW→5GW residual.

### Years 4–5 EBIT/GW is D&A, not rent

Cash EBITDA per GW at $10 is $15.7bn in every contracted year. EBIT is $5.3bn in year 1 and $12.3bn in years 4–5 because racks take 30% in year 1 and 8.5% of the residual later, so D&A/GPU falls $40.7k → $13.6k, a $7bn/GW swing that is mechanical. Year 6 at 55% re-rent is ~$5.2bn EBIT/GW, which is year-1 economics again with a cheaper machine, and which is the number that should be compared to a digestion-case hyperscaler OI/GW, not the year-4 peak. GAAP is milder than the model (the 2026 life stretch from four to five years on servers), so reported EBIT/GW will not print the model's year-1 trough as harshly, and will not print the model's year-4 peak as cleanly. [G-11] is the lens: the 49.7% AI-cloud adj. EBITDA margin on a cheap live book will not survive as incremental ROIC on $183k Rubin GPUs, and the year-4 EBIT/GW print will look like a margin expansion that is only depreciation rolling off.

### Hyperscaler EBIT/GW is not GPU-hour economics

AWS ~$9.2bn, Azure ~$8.3bn, GCP ~$6.8bn of operating income per *operating IT* gigawatt is a real number, and it is the number that makes a mature $10 Rubin GW at $12.3bn EBIT look 'better than a hyperscaler'. It is not a GPU-rental number. The denominator is operating IT load (Lambda Finance / Synergy / Dell'Oro Q1 2026: AWS ~6.2, Azure ~5.4–5.5, GCP ~4.7 GW), not controlled footprint (Jefferies/Aterio AWS ~10.6 GW), and the numerator is cloud operating income that includes Workspace-class SaaS at Google, a blended Intelligent Cloud at Microsoft from which Azure OI has to be estimated, and a reserved-instance / enterprise-agreement IaaS mix at AWS whose GPU halls are a slice. Using controlled footprint as the denominator would cut AWS OI/GW by ~40% (~$6.2bn rather than $9.2bn) and would still not make the number a GPU-hour. Capital age is the other half: those GW were built over a decade at a mix of CPU and early-GPU stickers, much of the shell already depreciated, which is why a new Foxconn-sticker AI GW inside AWS would print something closer to Nebius year 1 ($5.3bn at $10) than to AWS's $9bn blend. Live Nebius at ~$2bn EBIT/GW is below the hyperscalers because it is a small, cheap, wholesale book; new $10 GW year 1 is below; years 4–5 are above. The rhyme is peak contracted-GPU EBIT/GW ≈ depreciated hyperscaler OI/GW; the mechanism is depreciation and software mix, not 'their chips are worse'. It is therefore not fair to say Nebius's new book 'earns what hyperscalers earn' until one has said which year of whose D&A schedule, and which mix. The VLM test on this comparison is the disqualifier, not the score: a neocloud is a layer-renter on Nvidia allocation and a coordination premium on time-to-energisation, and a hyperscaler is a software-mix owner sitting on a depreciated estate; rhyming their EBIT/GW does not make them the same layer.

### Why Nscale does not reprice Nebius 2027

The $7.3 hour is a six-year forward sale of late-2027/2028 MW at a site whose Microsoft LOI walked, whose remaining Phase 1 buildings are 2028, and whose 2 GW of Caterpillar BTM gas is 1H 2028, priced as the anchor line in a $51bn backlog ahead of a September IPO. First tokens if the date holds are 4Q 2027; slip is the [G-10] base rate for a 864-genset behind-the-meter gas campus after a hyperscaler exit. Anthropic is not doing charity: the same megawatts were competed by Microsoft and Google, Nscale's cap table carries Nvidia, Dell, Nokia and Aker, and a six-year lock against Rubin Ultra (H2 2027) and Feynman (2028) is a price Anthropic pays for delivery/timing risk and for IPO-prospectus insurance as much as for tokens. Cash-rate gap versus Nebius's 1–3 year 2026–27 ACV/MW is ~20–30%; TCV/MW gap is smaller (~$98m versus ~$60–75m for three-year $20–25m), which is the duration adjustment. Primary discount is time-to-power (late-2027/2028 versus 2026 prompt MW that Q2 already sold at $20–25m); secondary is generation lock across two Nvidia cadences; it is not that the first billed Rubin hour is obsolete. Working assumption, held as hypothesis: do not reprice [[Theses/NBIS - Nebius Group]] 2027 to $7.3. The falsifier is a named Nebius Rubin cash rate at or below ~$8.22 on five-year-equivalent paper with 2026–27 COD, or a Q3 active-MW miss that makes the 1GW starting point in the $10 case fail.
