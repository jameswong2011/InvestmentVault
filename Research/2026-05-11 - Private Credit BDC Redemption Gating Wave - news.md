# Private Credit BDC Redemption Gating Wave

## Thesis Delta

Universal BDC redemption gating now confirmed — Golub's GCRED joins Blue Owl, BlackRock peers. Confirmatory evidence for the "private credit clockwork" framework in [[Theses/STNG - Scorpio Tankers]] Risks and [[Research/2026-03-30 - Macro - Gemini Iran War Canvas]]; for [[Theses/EDEL - Edelweiss Financial Group]], raises whether LP rotation away from US BDCs accelerates Indian PC inflows or compresses overall PC allocation as risk appetite sours.

## Summary

BlackRock TCP Capital Corp. (TCPC), a $1.5B publicly-traded middle-market BDC, marked down assets ~5% in Q1 2026 with $35M of total markdowns. The largest contributor was Job and Talent (staffing/recruitment); software-related investments accounted for roughly one-third of all markdowns. Non-accrual loans declined to 7.6% on cost basis from 9.7% the prior quarter, but the improvement is mechanical — one loan was sold and two were restructured rather than recovering organically. CEO Phil Tseng signaled "more work to do" on disciplined execution. The Q1 mark-down follows a 19% NAV cut in January 2026 and a Q4 2025 dividend cut to $0.17/share, which was held flat in Q1.

In parallel, Golub Capital Private Credit Fund (GCRED, ~$9.9B) capped quarterly withdrawals at the 5% limit after receiving redemption requests for 8.5% of shares — joining every major non-traded BDC peer in gating. Reported context: requests were "concentrated in approximately 5% of more than 12,000 shareholders," and Golub took ~$14M of new subscriptions YTD through April. GCRED retains $4.1B liquidity against ~$10B portfolio fair value with <0.1% non-accruals — meaning the gate is preemptive risk-management, not solvency-driven. Industry context: Blue Owl OTIC saw 41% redemption requests; OCIC saw 22%; Golub's 8.5% is comparatively benign.

The article's structural framing: AI productivity disruption is the long-cycle threat to BDC software-loan portfolios — software borrowers are over-represented in private credit, and AI-driven margin compression undermines the cash flows underwriting their debt. BLK's $12B HPS Investment Partners acquisition (closed 2025) was meant to scale legacy private-credit franchises like TCPC at the same time the cycle was peaking — the capital-deployment timing is now an open question for the parent.
