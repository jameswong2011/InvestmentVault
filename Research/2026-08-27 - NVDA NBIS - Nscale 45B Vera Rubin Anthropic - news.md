## Summary
Crypto Briefing, 26 August 2026: Nscale agreed a $45 billion, six-year computing-services contract with Anthropic, powered by NVIDIA Vera Rubin, at Monarch in West Virginia. Power envelope ~460 MW (~345k US homes). Rubin deployment starts late 2027. The platform is described as seven chips functioning as a unified AI supercomputer, pitched by NVIDIA on throughput-per-watt and cost-per-token versus Blackwell/Grace.

Nscale already had a March 2026 Microsoft commitment for up to 1.35 GW of Vera Rubin NVL72 at the same campus; combined Anthropic + Microsoft language implies the site could eventually consume well over a gigawatt. European operations are planned at 100k–130k Vera Rubin GPUs. Nscale originated as a crypto-mining infrastructure spin-out turned modular AI data-center builder, now a counterparty to Anthropic and Microsoft, with a US listing in the rumour mill (not confirmed in this article). Anthropic is described as preparing to go public; the $45B lock is framed as prospectus insurance as much as training capacity. Agentic workloads (multi-step autonomous tasks) are cited as the reason the power envelope is this large. The article does not disclose $/GPU-hour, take-or-pay vs best-efforts, or whether Microsoft exited a slice of Monarch in Anthropic's favour (that detail lives in other write-ups, not this body).
