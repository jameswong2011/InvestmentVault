# Samsung HBM4 Yield Nears 80% Golden Yield; HBM4E Reliability >70%

## Thesis Delta
Consensus prices [[Theses/000660 - SK Hynix]] HBM pricing power on sole/lead Rubin qualification; Sedaily reports Samsung HBM4 yield near 80% (“golden yield”) six months after <60% Feb start, Q3 HBM4 revenue >3x QoQ, HBM4 >60% of HBM mix in 2H, and HBM4E reliability yield >70% with samples to major customers — second-source leverage that can compress HBM ASP escalation into Rubin/Rubin Ultra even if bit demand stays strong.
