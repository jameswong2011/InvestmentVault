# SK Hynix (000660) — Stress Test

*Adversarial short-seller case · 2026-05-22 · against thesis dated 2026-04-23, last sync 2026-05-11*

## Thesis Delta

Prosecutor frame against a medium-conviction long. Five of ten bull assumptions rate 🔴 (fragile), five 🟡 (contested), zero 🟢. The thesis is a **"trough multiple on peak earnings"** setup — 8.1x EV/EBITDA on a 72% Q1 2026 operating margin that booked 60% of LTM op profit in a single quarter — backstopped by three planks (HBM4 lock-in, MR-MUF materials moat, Solidigm IPO crystallization) each with documented vault failure modes. The sector note's own probability weighting puts the bull case at 15% and the base case (cycle persists, 8-12x multiples) at 60% — implying current pricing already embeds the expected-value upside. Conviction should drop to **low pending Q3 2026 Rubin allocation data**; the kill trigger is observable in a single quarter.

## Summary

The thesis fundamentally rests on the Q1 2026 record (72% op margin, $27.1B op profit) being the foundation of a 2-3 year HBM supercycle. Six structural problems undermine this case:

**(1) The HBM share trajectory is already in the bear path.** 62% (2024) → 57% (Q1 2026). Gemini's "Incumbent Erosion" model (62% → 45% by 2030) is what is observed in the data, not the consensus 57% extrapolation the sell-side prices. The bull's "share erodes but margin compounds" narrative requires HBM TAM growth to outpace share erosion AND blended HBM gross margin to hold above 50% as Samsung/Micron capacity comes online — both are contested in the vault's own research notes.

**(2) The MR-MUF materials moat (Risk #10) is in contract renegotiation NOW.** The May 2026 deep-dive contains an ex-SK Hynix VP saying "without stable access to optimized underfill and EMC, 3D stacking becomes a yield nightmare. That is not something you fix with capex alone." This is the actual MR-MUF moat. Namics liquid EMC exclusivity is up for renewal; Samsung is in active talks with Namics; the secondary supplier (Resonac) is Samsung-aligned. The HIGH-conviction trigger requires Namics renewal but no LOW trigger exists for materials moat loss — asymmetric trigger inclusion is itself a tell that the bear scenario was not stress-tested.

**(3) The "foundry base die lock-in" advantage is symmetric across all three IDMs.** SK Hynix uses TSMC ~12nm for HBM4 base die; Samsung uses Samsung Foundry 4nm internal; Micron uses TSMC custom logic. The investment brief's lead-in claim that base die fabrication "converts per-cycle GPU memory share into multi-cycle architectural lock-in" applies to all three vendors equally, not SK Hynix differentially.

**(4) Customer concentration ~70% Nvidia HBM revenue is binary anchor exposure per Mental Model #10**, which classifies single-anchor concentration as "existential survival test, not concentration risk to monitor." Any Nvidia shift toward genuine 50/50 dual-sourcing at Rubin Ultra (2027) or Feynman (2028) takes 14pp+ of HBM revenue overnight.

**(5) Iran War photo-materials supply chain risk (Risk #9) has NO conviction trigger.** PR/BARC/HBM-adhesive disruption could delay the 2026 HBM4 ramp 6-12 months per the PCN requalification cycle. The thesis acknowledges this but provides no probability weight, no trigger, no hedge framework. The thesis conviction stays "medium" even as the kill mechanism activates.

**(6) Conviction triggers are AND-gated, not OR-gated.** LOW trigger requires Samsung >35% Rubin AND >10% YoY HBM price decline H1 2026. CLOSE trigger requires CXMT HBM AND Samsung HBM5 70% yield. The thesis can degrade significantly through either condition without ever firing a formal trigger — vulnerability classification (Outstanding Question that is open under the current trigger framework).

The kill trigger has a single forward observable: Samsung publicly disclosing >35% of Nvidia Rubin HBM4 allocation in either Q3 2026 OR Q4 2026 earnings. Samsung has already passed Nvidia qualification with "best scores"; the only remaining question is whether 1c DRAM yield (50% pilot, 70% target by mid-2026) clears the production-allocation threshold by H2 2026 ship.

## Contradiction Check

**Strongly contradicts thesis Bull Case Pillar #2 ("HBM supercycle extension through 2028 — materials moat reinforced"):** the materials moat is up for renewal NOW per Risk #10, and the HIGH-conviction trigger requires Namics renewal — but there is no LOW trigger for materials moat loss. Asymmetric trigger inclusion. The pillar that anchors the "earnings compound even while share erodes" narrative depends on a single Japanese supplier's contract renewal, which Samsung is actively trying to break.

**Strongly contradicts Investment Brief lead-in ("Foundry base die lock-in is the non-consensus edge"):** the lock-in is symmetric across all three HBM4 vendors. SK Hynix uses TSMC ~12nm; Samsung uses Samsung Foundry 4nm internal; Micron uses TSMC custom logic. The investment brief asserts a SK Hynix-specific advantage that the underlying vault data does not support.

**Strongly contradicts Bull Case Pillar #1 ("Rate-cut-driven multiple expansion on proven earnings → 12-15x P/E on 2026 earnings"):** the Sector Note's "Evergreen Reset Hypothesis" explicitly probability-weights this at 15% (full reset) or 25% (partial reset 12-15x). Weighted-expected multiple = 60% × 8x + 25% × 12x + 15% × 25x = 11.9x — current trading multiple. The bull case's upside is already in the price.

**Contradicts Outstanding Q1 ("Samsung Rubin allocation 20% vs 40%?"):** the thesis treats this as an open question but maintains medium conviction. The kill trigger (>35%) is within the Q-asked range (20-40%). If 35-40% range is observed, the thesis maintains medium conviction by trigger framework but invalidates by economic substance. The trigger threshold is too high.

**Aligns with vault sector note Cycle Dynamics:** the sector note's case studies (2017-2019 and 2023-2024) both establish that memory cycles round-trip with 65-85% peak-to-trough swings, and the trough comes 18-24 months after peak. Q1 2026 is the peak by current indicators (72% op margin, 60% of LTM op profit in one quarter). The historical template projects an 18-24 month window to trough — Q3 2027 to Q1 2028 by simple extrapolation. The thesis's bull-case "supercycle extends through 2028" requires this cycle to break the historical template; the only argument for that is HBM-as-cycle-extender, which Samsung's HBM4 entry partially neutralizes.

**Aligns with Mental Model #11 (Memory triopoly discipline is rented):** "Triopoly discipline holds for 4-7 years then breaks." The current cycle started 2024; the next pricing break is positioned for 2027-2028 per the mental model's historical pattern — squarely inside the bull case's "supercycle extension" window.

**Supports a "low conviction with optionality" reframe:** the thesis has two real call options (HBF $5-10B TAM, Solidigm IPO $6-15B crystallization) plus a base business that the sector note prices at 15% upside / 60% basecycle / 25% downcycle. Reframe is "low conviction in base case + two convex call options" — which is materially different from "medium conviction long with asymmetric upside."

**Samsung Electronics publicly disclosing >35% of Nvidia Rubin HBM4 allocation in either Q3 2026 OR Q4 2026 earnings.** Single quarter is sufficient — does not require the cumulative 70/30 split to invert; just requires Samsung's earnings to confirm allocation share above the dual-source-parity threshold that compresses SK Hynix sole-source premium.

Why this is the kill trigger and not "Samsung HBM5 yield > 70%":
