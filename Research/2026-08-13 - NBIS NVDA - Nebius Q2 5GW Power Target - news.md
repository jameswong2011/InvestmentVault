# Nebius Q2: ARR $3.0B; YE26 Contracted Power Raised to 5 GW

## Thesis Delta
Consensus (and the CRWV-twin multiple) prices [[Theses/NBIS - Nebius Group]] as a demand/GPU-rental print and reads a 5 GW headline as the 2026 build landing → this 12 August 2026 shareholder letter implies YE26 5 GW is *contracted* land-plus-power, not energized MW, with >1 GW/year *deploy* only starting 2027, while commercial terms de-risk demand further ($20–25M/MW landmark ACV, four >$1B TCV deals, $40B commitments, >$9B 2026 prepayments) and the first $775M SOFR+2.50% facility is GPU-infrastructure-plus-IG-cash-flow backed rather than a CRWV-style DDTL. The load-bearing variable stays physical energization — Conviction Trigger → HIGH still needs active-MW and ARR tracking, not contracted GW; [[Theses/NVDA - Nvidia]] gets a neocloud sell-through plus first Vera Rubin NVL72 bring-up ([G-7] ROIIC of power/capex mines; [G-8] funding mix; [G-10] energization base-rate; [G-13] mispriced variable is MW delivered; Industry #1 power bottleneck; #10 Microsoft delivered / Meta early-2027; VLM layer-renter vs software/asset-light claim).

## Summary
Arkady Volozh's Q2 2026 letter (dated 12 August 2026; webcast 8:00 a.m. ET the same day) frames the quarter as strategy validation: "demand for AI capacity continues to grow exponentially" and Nebius is converting that demand into contracted, profitable growth on its strongest commercial terms to date. Group revenue printed $582.3M (+454% YoY) versus $105.1M in Q2 2025; H1 revenue $981.3M (+529%). Nebius AI cloud was $574.9M (+514% YoY) and ~98% of group revenue. ARR reached $3.0B (last month of quarter × 12), +598% YoY and +56% from the $1.9B March exit. Group adjusted EBITDA flipped to $236.2M (41% of revenue) from a $(21.0)M loss a year earlier and rose $106.7M sequentially from Q1's $129.5M; AI-cloud adjusted EBITDA was $285.7M at a 49.7% margin — above the 45% Q1 print the thesis treats as the small-base peak. Cash is cited at ~$8B including $2.3B of Q2 operating cash flow. Full-year 2026 guidance is reaffirmed across all metrics; the letter defers the detailed guide to the earnings call.

The commercial mechanism is a step-change in ACV per MW and prepaid mix, not a new demand narrative. Core AI-cloud deals averaged above $20M revenue per MW, driven by new-generation GPU prices plus >30% higher pricing on older-generation GPUs versus Q1. Four landmark deals closed at >$1B TCV each and $20–25M/MW. TCV of Q2 wins grew nearly 4× quarter-over-quarter; TCV from new customers grew more than 9×. Roughly 70% of closed deals included prepayments — an all-time high — covering 50–60% of associated capex (Reflection and Cohere cited as examples of 50–60% self-financed via prepay). Expected payback on associated capex and related opex for Q2 deals is 1 year 10 months, down from a prior two-to-three-year range. Volozh states they could sell the entire 2027 capacity book on these terms today and are deliberately not doing so, holding inventory for immediate/higher-value needs. Majority of the Q2 book was signed against capacity arriving late 2026 and will contribute primarily to 2027 revenue — so the $20–25M/MW print is a 2027 P&L event, not a 2026 ARR catch-up. Named logos: Reflection (multi-year, open-source models); Cohere (enterprise agentic, NVIDIA architectures); an unnamed US AI neolab for frontier training; a large US quantitative trading firm (first large-scale financial-services win); AMI (Yann LeCun co-founded, world models); Basecamp Research (EDEN 28B biological foundation model); Higgsfield usage expanded >20× since first contract.

Capacity language is the letter's sharpest distinction and the one a headline-reader will miss. Contracted-power guidance for YE26 has been raised four times this year along the path >2.5 GW → >3 GW → >4 GW → 5 GW. "Contracted power" is defined as power secured by contracted land and power commitments — not commissioned, not active, not revenue-producing MW. The deploy cadence the company will stand behind is >1 GW per year *starting in 2027*. Q2 added sites in the UK, Estonia and Finland; the 2026 footprint list also names Israel, Iceland, France, Spain, New Jersey, Missouri, Oklahoma, Alabama, Minnesota, Kansas City and Pennsylvania. Construction is underway at owned US AI factories. All Microsoft contract capacity tranches have been delivered, including two in the past month. The second [[Theses/META - Meta]] agreement is on track for early-2027 online. May's Bloom Energy partnership is to deploy behind-the-meter fuel cells this year. First NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 systems have arrived and are being used to validate compute, networking and orchestration as a single stack; customers are already being engaged for Rubin access. Agentic tool-calling and memory workloads are being complemented with general-purpose CPU, including early NVIDIA Vera CPUs. Token Factory inference more than tripled in Q2, increasingly on open models. A new asset-light partnership model has partners finance land, power and hardware while Nebius contributes software, systems architecture/reference designs and GTM — an explicit attempt to stretch the two industry constraints the letter names: capital and capacity.

Funding and accounting close the print. Q2 capex was ~$5.7B, primarily GPUs, GPU-related hardware and data-center expansion. Management expects over $9B of customer prepayments in 2026. July brought the first secured debt: $775M at SOFR + 2.50%, backed by deployed GPU infrastructure and contracted cash flows from an investment-grade customer, raised against one of the long-term IG agreements. With $40B of customer commitments, Volozh says they will do more of this. The ATM sold 12.7 million Class A shares through 30 June 2026 at a $223.6 weighted-average price for ~$2.8B gross proceeds; 12.3 million shares remain available. Server and network useful life was lengthened from four years to five in 2026, which mechanically lowers D&A intensity (D&A 45% of revenue vs 72% a year ago) even as the absolute charge rose to $259.7M. Product development of $191.0M includes $115.9M of non-recurring SBC/post-combination expense from the Eigen AI acquisition. Cost of revenues fell to 23% of sales from 29%; SG&A to 30% from 65%. The letter's implied 2026 self-funding stack is therefore prepayments + ATM equity already issued + a first contract/GPU-backed facility — not a clean "no leverage" path and not yet a CRWV DDTL replica.

## Framework / Mental Model
The letter names a **repeatable ACV-per-MW framework** and a **three-duration contract book**, then a new **asset-light partnership model**. Components:

| Instrument | Duration / role | Price / funding signature | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short-term / auction | 3–6 months; acute, time-bound need | $40–50M per MW; first Q3 deal signed "this week" | Scarcity inventory; not the core book |
| Mid-term (core) | 1–3 years; "world's most ambitious AI companies" | Q2 average >$20M/MW; landmark $20–25M/MW vs 2026 base $12M/MW | Primary commercial engine |
| Long-term IG | Multi-year; investment-grade counterparties | Lower spot yield; enables advance-rate financing | Collateral for facilities ($775M first print) |
| Asset-light partner DC | Partner-owned land/power/hardware | Nebius: software + reference design + GTM; partner: capex | Stretch capital and physical capacity without owning the MW |

Methodology: reprice ACV/MW each quarter (new-gen GPU mix + >30% older-gen lift in Q2); push prepaid share toward 50–60% of capex to collapse payback (now 1y 10m); hold some 2027 MW off-market rather than clear the book at today's mid-term yield; use IG long-dated paper as the borrowing base; add partner-financed sites as a third capacity sleeve beside owned and colocated. This is a pricing-and-funding typology, not a demand forecast.

## Contradiction Check
**Supports** [[Theses/NBIS - Nebius Group]] §Summary ("demand is de-risked (capacity sold out)") and Insight #1 (execution bet, not demand bet): four >$1B TCVs, $20–25M/MW, $40B commitments, and "could sell 2027 today" are demand/pricing confirmation, not an energization proof. **Does not close** §Outstanding Question #1 or Conviction Trigger → HIGH: the letter never discloses *active/commissioned* MW toward the 800MW–1GW YE26 / ≥600MW-by-Q3 bars, and the deploy plan of >1 GW/year *from 2027* is slower than a naive 5 GW-in-2026 headline; 5 GW is contracted land+power versus the thesis Bull Case's older 3.5 GW contracted figure. **Partial on** Insight #2 / #5 and Conviction Trigger → CLOSE ("funds the capex gap primarily via GPU-collateralized debt"): the $775M facility is the first secured print, priced SOFR+2.50% (tighter than the thesis's SOFR+450 CRWV-style reference), collateralized by *both* deployed GPU infrastructure *and* IG contracted cash flows — the gray zone the thesis Mental Models already flagged (contract-backed ≠ GPU-resale DDTL). Volozh's "we will do more of this" against $40B commitments is an early-warning on the CLOSE wording, not a trip. **Partial on** Insight #2 dilution path: $2.8B ATM already issued through 30 June at $223.6; share-count not in the letter, so the >15%-in-6-months LOW trigger is not computable here. **Supports** Outstanding Q#4 / LOW-trigger margin bar this quarter: AI-cloud adj. EBITDA 49.7% vs the 35% tripwire and vs the 45% Q1 print the Bear Case treats as a small-base artifact — one more quarter of sold-out pricing, not yet a scaled-base test (Q2 wins land in 2027). **Does not** deliver HIGH-leg ClickHouse/Avride monetization. Versus [[Theses/CRWV - CoreWeave]] §Summary, the print widens the orthogonal-binary: CRWV's concurrent story is backlog/credit/debt on a built base; this letter is contracted-GW and prepaid ACV on a still-thin energized base. **Supports** [[Theses/NVDA - Nvidia]] §Summary data-center sell-through (Cohere on NVIDIA architectures; first Vera Rubin NVL72 units in-house; Vera CPU attach; $5.7B quarterly GPU-heavy capex). [G-10] outside view still applies: quadrupling energized MW in one year has near-zero reference-class support; a contracted-GW raise does not beat that base rate. [G-11] useful-life 4→5 years lifts reported earnings relative to cash economics — the same EBITDA-hides-dep issue Insight #6 already flags on Rubin. VLM: software/asset-light is an attempted layer; the letter still rents NVIDIA silicon and grid interconnect.
