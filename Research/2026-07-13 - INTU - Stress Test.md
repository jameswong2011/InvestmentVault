# 2026-07-13 - INTU - Stress Test

## Thesis Delta

Adversarial stress test of the HIGH-conviction, active INTU thesis. Net: the thesis **survives as a business call** (QBO genuinely accelerating, the sell-off is over-extrapolated) but **HIGH conviction is not earned by current evidence**. The vault raised INTU medium→high on 2026-07-11 — one day before its own 2026-07-12 deep-dive concluded the decisive risk is unfalsifiable by any current print and the base rate is 12–24 months of compression. That is a self-documented disconfirm-on-agreement setup. 5 of 6 bull assumptions rate 🔴 fragility; three of the four growth legs (IES, Lightbox, AI-ARPU) carry zero-to-negative empirical support; the LOW/CLOSE trigger framework is structurally blind to the failure mode that already fired (multiple compression on improving fundamentals). New external evidence the vault lacked: sell-side is now actively cutting (Goldman Neutral→Sell PT $276; Stifel Buy→Hold PT $275), securities-fraud investigations opened over the drop, and TurboTax Live's mix shift is margin-dilutive. Recommendation surfaced (not actioned): reassess HIGH→medium via `/status`; timing/realization is the primary risk.

## Summary — Thesis Vulnerability (the single biggest reason it fails)

**INTU is a value trap in slow motion, and the vault upgraded into it.** HIGH conviction was set on precisely the cross-model agreement — OpenAI/Anthropic paying to embed TurboTax, ARPU +11%, QBO Accounting +22%, a raised guide — that the READING PROTOCOL says to distrust. The decisive risk the thesis itself names (FY2028+ agentic interface-disintermediation collapsing QuickBooks from an application to a low-switching-cost data store) is unrefutable by any 2026 print, and the operative base rate (CHGG −48%, Stack Overflow questions −78%; AI-narrative incumbents stay compressed 12–24 months past the fundamentals) says the ~10x multiple is not self-correcting on a schedule. Meanwhile the growth options that justify the platform multiple — IES, Lightbox, AI-tier ARPU — remain empirically unconfirmed 2+ years in. The failure mode is not a crash; it is dead-money compounding at ~10x for 1–2 years while the bull legs stay unproven and the one "confirmed" leg (TurboTax pricing) masks unit loss and margin dilution. The position can bleed the entire time without a single LOW or CLOSE trigger firing.

**4. The consumer-tax franchise is the *ungated* part of the AI curve — and it is already cracking.** The user's linked [[Research/2026-07-11 - Gating Thresholds for AI Adoption]] and its vault critique ([[Research/2026-07-12 - Enterprise AI Adoption - Gating Factors Critique, 2030 Trajectory, Winners-Losers - synthesis]]) argue enterprise agentic adoption is gated by decade-scale organizational redesign — which protects *SMB/mid-market* switching costs (QBO, IES). **6. Short interest ~4.5% (12.3M shares, ~3.7 days to cover) — the de-rating is long liquidation, not a crowded short.** There is no short-squeeze fuel and no over-positioned bear cohort to mean-revert the price up. The ~$130B of erased market value is real money leaving, which historically re-rates only on undeniable reacceleration — consistent with the 12–24-month base-rate compression, not a technical bounce.

## Contradiction Check — internal inconsistencies in the thesis

- **Conviction vs evidence:** HIGH was set 2026-07-11; the 2026-07-12 deep-dive it was immediately followed by says "the primary risk migrates to realization/timing" and the FY2028+ disintermediation risk "is where the HIGH conviction is genuinely at stake." The thesis's own `## Mental Models` "Disconfirming check" concedes "the trigger ledger reads net-HIGH on fundamentals while the price sits ~25% below where medium conviction was set." HIGH conviction is documented as contestable inside the thesis itself.
- **Trigger framework blind spot:** the realized failure mode was Risk #5 — "multiple compression continues even with operationally good results." No LOW or CLOSE trigger keys on multiple compression; every trigger requires *fundamental* deterioration (seat growth <5%, ARPC decline, Mailchimp impairment, CK <10%). The thesis can lose another 20–30% on narrative with zero triggers firing. HIGH leg 1 (>20% AI attach by Q4 FY26) is now unmeasurable (superseded by consumption pricing) — the confirmation path is broken, not just the failure path.
- **Bull Case is stale, not repriced:** it still frames upside as "$615–720 target, ~70–90% upside from current $361–383" — the pre-crash price. The stock is ~$275. The entire Bull valuation bridge (16x→22–24x on FY27 EPS ~$28–30) was never repriced when the Bear Case was rewritten on 07-12; the two sections describe different worlds.
- **Key Metrics internal drift:** Summary still says "compressed the multiple from ~30x to ~11.5x"; Bear Case says ~10x; Key Metrics Forward P/E ~11.5x. Revenue Growth cell "+12–13% guide FY2026" is stale vs the raised +13–14%. Minor but unreconciled.
- **"Pricing power confirmed" conflates price with mix:** ARPU +11% is celebrated as pricing power while units fall −2% and the ARPU lift is substantially a shift into lower-margin Live (53% of franchise). Rising revenue-per-return via mix-shift into an expert-assisted, lower-margin tier is not the same as raising price on a defended unit base.

## Research Gaps — what the thesis does not know that it needs to

1. **QBO Accounting seat/customer growth vs price.** The single most important unknown: QBO Accounting +22% is explicitly "higher effective prices + customer growth + mix." If the volume component is decelerating beneath the price uplift, the "core is accelerating" rebuttal to the bear is a monetization pull-forward masking volume softening. Not cleanly disclosed. A short seller runs expert-network channel checks on net seat adds; the vault has price-blended segment revenue only.
2. **IES customer count and ACV.** Undisclosed. The entire mid-market leg is unfalsifiable until management breaks it out — and management keeping it buried is itself weak evidence.
3. **Lightbox revenue.** Undisclosed; zero commentary. The embedded-finance-infrastructure thesis (Insight #5) has no data behind it.
4. **Consumption-pricing net-ARPU effect.** The Aug-2026 model is the empirical test of Insight #2 and there is no disclosed net-ARPU figure. Could confirm expansion or erosion; the thesis assumes the former.
5. **TurboTax cohort retention by income band.** The sub-$50K defection could be structural (share loss to free) or cyclical ("industry headwinds," management framing). A serious short has cohort retention curves; the vault has aggregate units.
6. **Margin trajectory under Live-mix + consumption.** OQ#7 (gross-margin path) is now partly answered *bearishly* by the Live mix (net income +8.6%), and the thesis has not integrated this.

## Kill Trigger — the single observable that vindicates the short

**Primary (fundamental roll-over, testable in ~5 weeks):** the FY2026 full-year print + FY2027 guide (August 2026) showing **QBO Accounting decelerating below ~15% OR Online Ecosystem below ~12%, AND a FY2027 revenue guide below ~10%, AND the consumption-pricing model disclosing net blended ARPU flat-to-down.** That combination confirms the QuickBooks core is entering the exact reverse-DCF path a ~10x multiple embeds (terminal growth collapsing toward low single digits within 2–3 years), converting "over-extrapolated sell-off" into vindication.

**Structural (disintermediation, the thesis-killer):** a major LLM vendor (OpenAI / Anthropic / Google) shipping a native consumer tax-filing agent that **e-files >1M U.S. returns in the 2027 season**, OR Microsoft bundling QuickBooks-equivalent AI bookkeeping into M365 Copilot with a **disclosed QBO churn or attach-rate hit**. Either collapses Insight #1.

## Verdict

The thesis **has 4 critical vulnerabilities that must be resolved before HIGH conviction is defensible**: (1) HIGH set into a self-documented disconfirm-on-agreement with an unfalsifiable decisive risk; (2) three of four growth legs empirically unconfirmed; (3) a trigger framework blind to the failure mode that already fired; (4) the "confirmed" pricing-power leg masks unit loss and margin dilution. None of these breaks the *business* — QBO is genuinely accelerating and the sell-off is over-extrapolated — but all four attack the *HIGH conviction rating and the active-pursuit sizing*. The honest resolution is medium conviction with realization/timing as the primary risk, pending the August print. External evidence (Goldman Sell, Stifel Hold, securities investigations, low short interest) tilts the near-term risk further down. Reassess HIGH→medium via `/status`.
