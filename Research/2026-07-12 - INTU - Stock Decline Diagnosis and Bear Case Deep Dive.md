## Summary

Intuit delivered a beat-and-raise Q3 FY2026 (revenue +10% to $8.6B, EPS $12.80 beat, full-year guide raised to +13-14% revenue / +18% EPS) and the stock was hammered — down ~13% on the print, ~62% from its July-2025 high of $813.70, ~-51% YTD, the worst S&P 500 performer of 2026. At ~$275 / ~$75B / ~10x forward P/E, the price broke clean through the thesis's own prior bear band ($265-335). The de-rating is a narrative repricing ("SaaSpocalypse" — AI models collapsing tax/bookkeeping switching costs), not an earnings miss.

The diagnosis: the market has correctly identified a real first crack in **consumer tax** (TurboTax Online total units -2%, the first modern-era decline; pay-nothing filers 7M down from 8M; price-sensitive sub-$50K DIY filers defecting to FreeTaxUSA / H&R Block free tiers) and layered on it a legitimate but multi-year **agentic interface-disintermediation** risk. But it has wrongly applied a whole-franchise decline multiple to a company whose largest, highest-quality segment is doing the opposite: **QuickBooks Online Accounting revenue +22%, Online Ecosystem +19%**, net retention ~115%+, on price + customer growth + mix. TurboTax defended revenue (+7% to $4.4B) via ARPU +11% and Live +36% (Live +38% customers, ~53% of TurboTax revenue) even as units slipped — pricing power intact. OpenAI (~$100M, Nov 2025) and Anthropic (Feb 2026) *paid* to embed TurboTax in their chatbots — moat validation, the inverse of the disruption the multiple prices.

The honest counterweight (disconfirm-on-agreement, per the READING PROTOCOL, given the vault's fresh HIGH-conviction upgrade): a ~10x multiple on +13-14% revenue growth is not self-correcting on a predictable schedule. The comparable set (Chegg -48%, Stack Overflow questions -78%) stayed compressed 12-24 months past the fundamentals. The August-2026 consumption / pay-per-task pricing reset is unproven and could confirm ARPU erosion rather than expansion. The FY2028+ risk — agents reading bank feeds/receipts/invoices to produce the P&L without opening QuickBooks, reducing QBO to a low-switching-cost data store — is exactly the risk a strong FY2026 print cannot refute, and it is where the HIGH conviction is genuinely at stake.

## Contradiction Check

- **The bull data and the price disagree, and the price is not obligated to converge on schedule.** Every fundamental datapoint (QBO +22%, ARPU +11%, guide raised, OpenAI/Anthropic paying to embed) points bullish — which, per the READING PROTOCOL, is the cue to hunt the bear hardest, not to commit. The base rate says AI-narrative-attacked incumbents stay compressed 12-24 months past the evidence.
- **QBO strength could be late-cycle price, not durable volume.** QBO Accounting +22% is partly "higher effective prices" — if underlying customer/seat growth is decelerating beneath the price uplift, the acceleration is a monetization pull-forward masking volume softening. Seat/customer growth is not cleanly disclosed; this is the single most important number to extract next print.
- **The consumption-pricing reset cuts both ways.** It could prove Insight #2 (AI as incremental ARPU) or invert it (consumption caps blended ARPU below the old per-seat tiers). Unproven until FY2027 prints.
- **TurboTax unit decline may be structural, not cyclical.** "Industry-wide headwinds" is management's framing; the sub-$50K DIY defection to free competitors is a share-loss vector that predates AI and accelerates with it. A second consecutive season of unit decline with ARPU no longer offsetting would confirm the consumer-tax bear.

## Framework / Mental Model

Applied per the READING PROTOCOL in [[Mental Models/Generalist - Overview]] — hypotheses to test, not verdicts:

- **[[Mental Models/Lens - Value Layer Monopoly]]** (application-layer overlay): QuickBooks is an application-layer position cheap intelligence makes *more* contestable — the FY2028+ agentic interface-disintermediation risk is the lens's core warning, and it is real and unrefutable by any single strong print. Counter: switching costs (chart of accounts, bank feeds, accountant channel, ~115% NRR) are higher than a pure app, and the 2026-07-12 Enterprise AI Adoption synthesis argues the binding gate is organizational redesign (decade-scale), so vertical switching costs survive longer than the sell-off priced.
- **[[Mental Models/Lens - Automation & AI Readiness]]** (Lens B): INTU owns the SMB-finance system-of-record + execution path; tax/accounting decision traces are proprietary and current. OpenAI/Anthropic paying to embed is execution-path validation, not wrapper-risk realization — for now.
- **[G-1] endgame / reverse-DCF + [G-13] expectations**: ~10x forward embeds near-zero-to-negative terminal growth; the price-implied expectation is a QuickBooks-core roll-over within 2-3 years. Isolate the single mispriced variable: **QBO Accounting / Online Ecosystem growth trajectory** — if it holds >15% while priced for collapse, that is the variant perception.
- **[G-10] base rates**: sustained high growth rarer than extrapolation assumes as the revenue base grows — but the inverse error (pricing terminal decline into a segment compounding +19-22%) is the mirror-image mistake the market is making here.
