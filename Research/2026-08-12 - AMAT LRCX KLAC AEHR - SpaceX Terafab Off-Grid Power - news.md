# SpaceX Plans to Power $16.8B Terafab Without the Grid

## Thesis Delta

## Summary
Data Center Knowledge: SpaceX’s Grimes County, Texas tax-abatement agreement states Terafab “is expected to be powered by electricity generated by on-site power plant(s) and [is] not expected to use electricity from the grid,” with a referenced battery storage project. Lead developer Riley Trettel told commissioners Aug 5 the plan is natural-gas plants plus large battery arrays. Scale: $16.8B initial investment announced Aug 6, 100M sq ft; Texas JETI filings describe four phases and up to $119B. Analyst Neil Osnato calls private-utility architecture “much more unusual” for a semiconductor/AI complex of this size. Document does not establish Terafab will lack a physical grid connection.
