# 000660 / SNDK / 285A — Nintendo Flags Memory Cost Inflation

## Thesis Delta
Consensus hears memory tightness mostly from suppliers; a **price-sensitive, high-volume OEM** disclosing a **¥100B (~$630M)** component hit and further console price hikes is cleaner demand-side confirmation that AI allocation is lifting DRAM/NAND costs — supportive near-term ASP power for [[Theses/000660 - SK Hynix]], [[Theses/SNDK - SanDisk]], [[Theses/285A - Kioxia]].

## Summary
Nintendo secured **$300M** IEEPA tariff refunds (absorbed most duties last year), boosting GPM +22pp YoY and operating margin +150.5%. Despite that relief, fiscal Q1 2027 took a ¥100B hit from rising component prices — “namely related to memory inputs” — and further raised console prices. Consumer class action seeks pass-through of tariff refunds; Nintendo moved to dismiss.
