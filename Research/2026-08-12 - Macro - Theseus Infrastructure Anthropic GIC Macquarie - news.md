# Macro — Theseus Infrastructure (Anthropic × Macquarie × GIC)

## Thesis Delta
Consensus frets AI CapEx concentration in hyperscaler balance sheets; Theseus shows **sovereign/infra GP capital** (GIC + Macquarie) owning purpose-built US DCs with Anthropic as anchor tenant under long-term leases — extending the off-balance-sheet financing wave that also shows up in Nvidia’s $500B platforms. Demand signal for compute/networking/memory complex ([[Theses/NVDA - Nvidia]], [[Theses/TSM - TSMC]] if present, [[Theses/AVGO - Broadcom]]) without a disclosed MW number.

## Summary
Aug 10 PR: Anthropic, Macquarie Asset Management, and GIC form **Theseus Infrastructure** to develop, operate, and lease DCs to Anthropic. Macquarie funds + GIC own the platform and fund majority equity; Anthropic anchors under long-term agreements; initial focus United States. Anthropic covers consumer electricity price increases from the sites. No MW/capex disclosed. Context: prior ~$50B custom-DC ambition; ~$35B chip-lease loan with Google backstop (press).
