# Musk Tells SpaceX Staff xAI Will Scale 1.4GW to 10GW by End-2027 on Vera Rubin

## Thesis Delta
Consensus (MS $300, SemiAnalysis 10 GW / ~$500B ARR, Microsoft-as-largest-offtaker) already prices [[Theses/SPCX - SpaceX]] as an AI compounder — this 13 August 2026 Tom's Hardware recap of Musk addressing SpaceX employees restates the same 10 GW / $30–50 per watt / $300–500 billion revenue claim, now pinned to “the end of next year” (end-2027) on Vera Rubin, from a printed 1.4 GW Memphis/Southaven nameplate. The thesis’s non-consensus is that GW headlines are not load-bearing until CSA duration exceeds D&A lives; this piece adds FLOPS arithmetic, not contract duration. Trigger touch (flag only): SPCX → HIGH needs YE26 nameplate ≥2 GW on the 1.4 GW definition — a 2027 10 GW aim does not print YE26 2 GW. → LOW if YE26 <2 GW remains the live watch. For [[Theses/NVDA - Nvidia]] this is a Rubin NVL72 demand-shape print (author’s 2.1–2.4 million Max-P GPUs), not a CUDA/Omniverse print.

## Summary
Anton Shilov reports Musk told SpaceX employees that xAI data-center power will rise about 7× to 10 GW by late 2027. Quote: “We have already built the most powerful AI training clusters in the world… What we expect to do by the end of next year is about 10 times more than what we have done thus far… aiming to get to 10 GW [of compute] by the end of next year… If the value per watt is probably going to be $30 to $50… $300 to $500 billion a year in revenue.” Present nameplate: Memphis and Southaven at 1.4 GW. SpaceX market cap is cited as $2.44T (20 June) → $1.43T (1 August) → $1.87T (12 August), a ~$570B drawdown then a rebound.

The author (not Musk) converts watts to IT and FLOPS. At PUE ~1.2, 1.4 GW nameplate is ~1.17 GW IT and ~0.8–0.95 GW accelerators if 70–80% of IT is GPUs. Incremental 8.6 GW nameplate on NVL72 VR200 at PUE 1.2 → 6.88 GW IT → 4.816–5.504 GW to Rubin GPUs at 2.3 kW Max-P → 2.094–2.393 million Rubin GPUs, or 29,083–33,236 NVL72 racks. Cluster NVFP4 inference 105–120 EFLOPS; NVFP4 training 73–84; FP6/FP8 training 37–42; FP64 70–80 EFLOPS vs Top500 combined FP64 of 18.73 EFLOPS (3.7–4.3×). He flags that nameplate ≠ compute, Max-P may not be universal, and $300–500B revenue is unproven against competing sellers of compute.
