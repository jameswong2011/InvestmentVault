# SK Group Governance & Minority-Cashflow Risk — SK Hynix (000660)

## Thesis Delta

Adds the governance / capital-allocation dimension that was entirely absent from [[Theses/000660 - SK Hynix]]'s prior 11 risks (all operational, competitive, supply-chain, or macro). It reframes — does not break — the Korea-discount leg of the bull case: the thesis Summary and sector Investor Heuristic #9 price the persistent 30-40% discount to Micron as a spring-loaded re-rate on Korean governance reform. This note argues a material slice of that discount is a *rational price* for a control-ownership wedge — the Chey family controls SK Hynix's board via a Chey → SK Inc. → SK Square → SK Hynix pyramid while holding only ~1% of Hynix economics — and will not fully close until the structure simplifies on terms that do not disadvantage minorities. Conviction unchanged (medium): the risk is valuation/tail, not thesis-breaking, because three counterweights currently bind it. Feeds new thesis Risks #12-13 + a control-pyramid map.

## Summary

SK Hynix is the crown-jewel cash engine at the *bottom* of a chaebol control pyramid. Chairman Chey Tae-won holds **17.9% of SK Inc.** (the group holding company); SK Inc. controls **SK Square** (the 2021 SK Telecom spin-off vehicle); and SK Square is SK Hynix's largest shareholder at **~20.1%**. The family's effective *economic* look-through to SK Hynix is therefore ~1% (17.9% × ~30% × ~20.1%), while its *control* rests on the ~20% SK Square voting block that anchors the board. That gap — ~1% economics vs. board control — is the textbook control-ownership wedge that makes diverting minority cash a structural incentive rather than a hypothetical. Chey made his *first-ever* personal purchase of SK Hynix shares in 2026; before that, his direct alignment with Hynix minorities (who are ~54% foreign institutions and ~7.5-8% National Pension Service) was essentially zero.

The diversion does not require classic below-market asset transfers. The live channels are subtler: (1) the **"golden dividend pipeline"** — SK Hynix's fixed ₩1,500/share dividend (~₩0.22T/yr to SK Square) upstreams to SK Square → SK Inc., where a ~1%-economic controller redeploys it at group discretion; (2) **inter-group co-investment** — the $500B SK Group–Nvidia deal (Jul 2026) entwines Hynix's arm's-length HBM supply with SK Telecom's 2GW→15GW data-center buildout and group AI vehicles, and the plumbing already ran the other way when SKT committed ₩738.4B to SK Hynix's US AI hub (2026-06-25); (3) a future **group cash call** — SK On's battery losses (borrowings ₩4.5T → ₩19T) are the standing pressure source, so far ring-fenced inside the SK Innovation / SK E&S / SK Enmove energy silo. The canonical minority-adverse precedent is the **2015 SK C&C / SK Holdings merger**, which the NPS took the rare step of opposing.

The honest, balanced read is that this is primarily a *valuation* risk plus a *tail* capital-misallocation risk — not an imminent tunneling event. Four mitigants bound it: Korea's **July-2025 Commercial Act §382-3** now extends directors' fiduciary duty to shareholders (not just "the company"), making minority-adverse related-party deals legally challengeable; the SK On rescue was funded inside the energy silo without tapping Hynix; ~54% foreign ownership plus the new SEC-reporting US ADR raise the disclosure cost of tunneling; and SK Square's own 38-46% NAV discount penalizes it for diverting Hynix cash to group support rather than passing it through. The strongest disconfirming datapoint: SK Hynix's ~₩100T / 50%-of-FCF return program (up to ~₩40T buyback) is the largest, most pro-rata return in its history, run in the same window as peak group cash needs — evidence the controller is choosing broad distribution over diversion.

## Framework / Mental Model

Applies three Generalist / Lens triggers as **hypotheses to test**, per the READING PROTOCOL — not verdicts:

- **[G-8] capital allocation is management's primary job, and operators have perverse incentives** — fires on the wedge: a controller optimizing group-level control (SK On survival, AI-infra scale, family succession) is not the same principal as a Hynix minority optimizing Hynix ROIIC. The $720B buildout and group co-investments must be underwritten against *Hynix* incremental returns, not group ambition.
- **[G-5] governance/culture quality** — the pyramid, the ~1% economic alignment, and the 2015 SK C&C precedent are negative markers; §382-3, the ADR disclosure regime, and the pro-rata return program are offsetting positive markers. Net: mixed, improving at the margin.
- **VLM (Value Layer Monopoly) disqualifier — conglomeration / layer-renter** — SK Hynix *owns* the HBM layer (strong fit), but sits *inside* a diversified conglomerate whose controller can tax the layer's cash for unrelated group functions. The layer monopoly is real; the wedge is the mechanism by which a minority may not fully capture it.

## Contradiction Check

**Supports** the sector note's open Key Industry Question ("Does the Korean memory equity discount represent persistent governance risk or a clean re-rating opportunity?") — this note answers *both*: real governance risk AND a re-rating option, with the mix determining how much of the discount is spring-loaded. **Challenges** the thesis Summary + sector Investor Heuristic #9 framing that treats the discount as a near-pure multiple-expansion catalyst; part of it is a rational wedge price. **Connects** to [[Theses/SKM - SK Telecom]], whose own Mental Models section already flags the ₩738.4B intragroup transfer as breaking the "clean chaebol unwind" premise and the 15GW plan as a [G-8] capital-allocation falsifier — the two SK-Group theses now share a governance vector. **Does not fire** any existing Conviction Trigger (all three are HBM-share/pricing/CXMT gated); governance is a valuation-multiple and tail-diversion risk that sits alongside, not inside, the trigger set. **Disconfirming datapoint to monitor**: the pro-rata ₩100T return program argues the controller is currently distributing broadly, not diverting — a Hynix capital commitment to a group AI vehicle without matched Hynix economics would be the datapoint that flips the read.
