# Macro — AWS Withdraws Calvert County 500MW Campus

## Thesis Delta
Consensus may read AWS pullback as AI CapEx cooling; primary source attributes it to **development timelines / deliverability**, coinciding with local political flip and an Aug 18 moratorium hearing — i.e. **siting friction, not demand destruction**. Raises conversion risk on announced-MW pipelines for the AI-infra complex ([[Theses/NVDA - Nvidia]], [[Theses/AVGO - Broadcom]], [[Theses/MRVL - Marvell Technology]], WFE names) while dollars rotate to permissive / behind-the-meter sites.

## Summary
AWS withdrew a 2.5M sq ft / 8-building / up-to-**500MW** application next to Calvert Cliffs nuclear (Constellation). Filed May 18; withdrawal confirmed Aug 4. Same day county set Aug 18 hearing on 6-month site-plan moratorium after three pro-development incumbents lost June 23 primaries. Broader: QTS $30B Digital Gateway abandoned in July; Data Center Watch cites 75 projects / >$130B delayed/canceled in 1Q26 partly from opposition. Omdia: operators cutting losses; nuclear adjacency no longer politically safe.
