# IREN Locked In $4 Billion ARR, Wall Street Still Sees Losses

## Thesis Delta
For Full-weight [[Theses/NBIS - Nebius Group]], IREN is the cleanest public contracted-ARR versus trailing-P&L peer: management cites $4 billion of contracted 2026 ARR (only about $1 billion live after Microsoft accepted Horizon 1 at Childress) from less than 10% of a 5GW+ secured-grid book, with recent three-year deals above $20 million per MW of IT load and talks around $25 million, while the fiscal Q4 print missed and took a $450.4 million mining impairment. Consensus still reads neoclouds on reported quarterly revenue and GAAP losses; this source prices contracted run-rate plus customer prepay and GPU financing, the same framing NBIS bulls use on Outstanding Q#1 (energize contracted GW) and insight #5 (prepay as anti-DDTL).

## Summary
On the 27 August 2026 fiscal fourth-quarter call, IREN said it has locked in $4 billion of contracted annualized recurring revenue tied to 2026 capacity, a forward-looking target management expects to be operational by the end of the December quarter, against only about $1 billion actually operating after Microsoft accepted Horizon 1 at Childress. The $4 billion is guidance for run-rate revenue on already-signed contracts, not a recognized-revenue print, and it comes from less than 10% of the company's five-gigawatt-plus portfolio of secured grid connections; recent three-year contracts are pricing in excess of $20 million per megawatt of IT load, with active discussions around $25 million per megawatt, and three-year pricing is up about 125% since November.

Reported fiscal Q4 revenue was $137.2 million, missing the roughly $157 million Wall Street consensus in this piece (the daily brief had about $140 million elsewhere), with a GAAP net loss of $684 million that included a $450.4 million non-cash impairment on decommissioned Bitcoin mining hardware. AI Cloud revenue reached $70.5 million in the quarter and overtook Bitcoin mining revenue of $66.7 million, the crossover the piece calls the whole trade. Customer prepayments are funding 45% to 55% of GPU capex, and combined with 90% GPU financing, total funding can exceed the cost of the underlying hardware; the company secured $6.5 billion of GPU financing over the past three months, including $3.6 billion of investment-grade paper at roughly 6% tied to Microsoft and $2.4 billion at a 9% fixed rate led by Blue Owl and PIMCO-managed funds.

Investors focused on the reported miss rather than the ARR target. Shares closed at $36.535 on the day of the release, a 9.86% drop from the prior close of $40.53, and selling extended into 28 August with IREN quoted near $35.50 by late morning, a 12.41% single-session decline. Reddit chatter around the earnings report stayed neutral, with sentiment scores of 54 and 57 and only two total mentions. CEO Daniel Roberts said signing deals is not the bottleneck in this market, bringing GPUs online is. Wall Street's $80.19 average target price and 11 Buy ratings still reflect the ARR trajectory; management guided to more than $4 billion of ARR by the end of the December quarter, with revenue recognition weighted to the March quarter as Horizons 2 through 4 come online, and if commissioning slips the loss profile widens before the ARR shows up on the income statement.
