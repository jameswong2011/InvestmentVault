# NVIDIA Announces Financial Results for Second Quarter Fiscal 2027: $96.2B; Vera Rubin Full Production

## Thesis Delta
Consensus going into the 26 August print was still anchored on the thesis's own Q2 guide of ~$91B and a ~25x-forward tape debating whether software/Physical AI is under-priced as share moderates — this official newsroom release prints **$96.2B revenue (+18% QoQ / +106% YoY)** vs Q1 $81.6B, **Data Center $89.0B (+117% YoY)**, **GAAP and non-GAAP GM both 75.0%**, GAAP diluted EPS $2.46 / non-GAAP $2.22, and **Q3 FY27 revenue $108.0B ±2% with zero China Data Center compute in the outlook**. The mechanism that matters is not another beat: Jensen's "compute is revenue" + "Vera Rubin, now in full production" with named racks at CoreWeave, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, OCI **and Nebius** is a production-status and partner-list print against [[Theses/NVDA - Nvidia]] §Summary (guide stale at $91B) and Outstanding Q on CUDA vs ASIC. ## Summary
NVIDIA reported Q2 FY27 (ended 26 July 2026) revenue of $96.221 billion, up 18% sequentially from $81.615 billion and 106% from $46.743 billion a year ago. Gross margin printed 75.0% on both GAAP and non-GAAP; GAAP operating income $63.734 billion (+124% YoY); GAAP net income $59.688 billion (+126% YoY) against non-GAAP net income $53.954 billion — the gap is almost entirely equity-mark gains ($7.771 billion of GAAP other income from equity securities, stripped in non-GAAP). Beginning Q1 FY27, non-GAAP no longer excludes stock-based compensation; historical non-GAAP was restated to include SBC.

Jensen Huang: "AI has reached its inflection point. It's doing useful work. Its tokens are productive and profitable. Now, compute is revenue." He frames demand as broadening from "one lab" a year ago to multiple frontier labs, an open-model ecosystem, and physical AI, with Vera Rubin "built to power exactly this moment" and now in full production. Capital return was $26.0 billion in the quarter (buybacks + dividends) with $99.0 billion remaining on the repurchase authorization; next dividend $0.25 on 1 October 2026.

Q3 outlook is $108.0 billion ±2%, GAAP/non-GAAP GM 74.0% ±50 bps, opex ~$9.2 billion GAAP / $9.0 billion non-GAAP, and **no Data Center compute revenue from China assumed**. FY27 tax rate 16–18%. Data Center was $89.0 billion (93% of revenue); Edge Computing $7.2 billion. Named Rubin rack partners: CoreWeave, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, and **Nebius**. Spectrum-6 switches (pluggable and CPO) are arriving with the Rubin platform. Groq 3 LPX is in full production. Compute-financing platforms with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR are still "subject to definitive agreements" for >$500 billion of third-party capital. A multi-year memory partnership with SK hynix is restated. Six-month revenue is $177.837 billion; operating cash flow in the quarter $24.077 billion; free cash flow $21.341 billion; inventories $31.575 billion; receivables $63.059 billion; long-term debt jumped to $32.366 billion (from $7.469 billion at FY26 year-end) after $24.896 billion of debt proceeds; Groq cash outflow $2.944 billion.
