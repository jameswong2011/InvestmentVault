# TSMC N2 Said to Hit 100k wpm by YE2026; N3 180k Early; N2 3% of Q3 Revenue

## Thesis Delta
Consensus already treats [[Theses/TSM - Taiwan Semiconductor]] N2 as sold-out at a 10–20% premium (Q2 mix 3%) — this 13 August 2026 Wccftech recap of Economic Daily News claims N2 monthly wafers go 20k → 100k by end-2026 (four months), N3 hits 180k wpm ahead of a prior 175k / Q4 2026 schedule, and N2 is already 3% of Q3 2026 revenue with 4× as many tape-outs as N3 had, named demand from NVIDIA, AMD, Broadcom, Apple A20 Pro (iPhone 18 Pro, September), Qualcomm/MediaTek 2027, AMD MI455X vs Rubin Ultra. Wccftech rates the rumor 50% / Plausible (source 3/5, corroboration 1/5). That is a capacity-shape print, not a GM/capex print. Trigger touch (flag only): TSM → HIGH-reaffirm needs FY26 >40% USD growth + Q3 GM ≥66% + Jan-2027 capex ≥$70B — none of those are in this piece. N2 3% of Q3 matches the Q2 3% already in the thesis.

## Summary
Omar Sohail, citing EDN, says AI customers will migrate to 2nm for inference performance per watt and that NVIDIA, AMD, and others force TSMC toward 100k N2 wafers/month by YE2026. N3 demand is not fading: target now 180k wpm vs a prior 175k, ahead of Q4 2026. N2 has “no silicon in sight that leverages this lithography” in the author’s phrasing, yet already 3% of Q3 revenue and 4× N3 tape-outs — treat as EDN/Wccftech, not a TSMC IR line. Fab 20 prior N2 output 20k/month. Apple A20 Pro on iPhone 18 Pro/Max in September is the mobile volume kicker; Qualcomm and MediaTek follow in 2027. AMD MI455X is named as a Rubin Ultra challenger that could choke N2 supply. Apple is said not to stay on 2nm more than two generations, wanting 1.4nm to avoid the squeeze.

Alternate eTeknix recap (thinner, same cluster): N2 3% of revenue vs N3 30% / N5 33%; GAAFET +15% perf or −30% power; Apple A20 Pro and AMD EPYC Venice named.
