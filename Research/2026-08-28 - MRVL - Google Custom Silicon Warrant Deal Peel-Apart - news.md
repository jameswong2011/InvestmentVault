# Peeling Apart Google–Marvell “$120B” Custom Silicon / Warrant Deal

## Thesis Delta
Consensus headlines read Google investing $12.5B / Marvell getting $120B through 2033 — Next Platform’s 8-K peel shows a **slipperier round-trip**: expanded custom-silicon commercial agreement spanning **TPU-ecosystem attach** (inference accelerators, storage/NIC/memory-interface controllers, near-memory compute) plus warrants on **58.97M shares (~6.3%)** vesting mostly in **$500M revenue buckets**. Google need not exercise; rights can wait until **18 Aug 2033**. Rumored “Merope” LPU is **not named**; addendum code **“Kestrel”** targets qual **≤10 Nov 2027**. Consensus priced a single mega-ASIC; this source prices a **multi-year shepherding franchise + optionality**. **Supports** [[Theses/MRVL - Marvell Technology]] custom-silicon/TPU attach — with less headline inflation. No trigger fire.

## Summary
Marvell’s 8-K expands Google custom products around the TPU ecosystem. Warrants: 1.36M vest over first four quarters; rest in 240 buckets tied to $500M Google-related revenue each. Floor context ~$206.58/share. Announcement-day market-cap rise (~$18.6B) more than covered the $12.5B warrant face value before a component shipped. Author links “Kestrel” product to Google’s Kansas City Kestrel datacenter cluster (~430 acres, up to five hyperscale DCs, ~$100B / ~2.5M sq ft) as a late-2027-qual / 2028-deploy inference accelerator basket shepherded through TSMC. Neither party confirmed Merope/CXL memory-processor rumors from The Information.
