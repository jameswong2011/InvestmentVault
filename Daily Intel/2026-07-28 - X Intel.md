The crowd banks the TAM without inspecting the product-positioning gap the thesis flags.

**$AVGO** — The crowd's single datapoint — AVGO committing next-gen AI silicon to Samsung's sub-2nm foundry — introduces a supply-chain diversification away from TSMC that the thesis does not engage; the thesis's XPU and $100B-2027 framing assumes TSMC-centric leading-edge and advanced-packaging supply, and a material Samsung foundry allocation would alter both the capacity and yield-risk picture the thesis rests on.

**$AAOI** — The crowd claims AAOI's internal InP fab 'shields it from component shortages while safeguarding gross margins' — directly contradicting the thesis's core insight that AAOI's fab makes only 1310nm DFB lasers (CATV/lower-speed), NOT the 200G EMLs that gate 1.6T, so its high-speed supply is merchant-sourced identically to the Chinese assemblers' and provides no margin protection at the bleeding edge, evidenced by flat ~29-30% gross margin despite 51% revenue growth.
