## ⚠ Thesis divergence

**$NVDA** — The crowd's binding constraint is upstream optical components — CW laser and CPO-grade supply locked into LTAs to 2030 across LITE, COHR, Landmark, MTSI and SMTC — meaning near-term Rubin rack shipments are gated by a handful of merchant laser suppliers rather than by CUDA lock-in, ASIC share or hyperscaler demand. The thesis carries TSMC concentration as the supply risk but does not engage the optics/CW-laser chokepoint that the crowd now treats as the actual ship-rate governor on the Vera Rubin ramp.

**$000660** — The crowd's position is that all three HBM suppliers have sold through 2027 capacity and that per-GPU memory content is stepping up generationally — which implies pricing is set by aggregate scarcity, not by the marginal supplier. That directly contests the thesis's central mechanism, in which Nvidia dual-sourcing at parity resets pricing and compresses SK Hynix blended HBM gross margin from 60%+ toward 40-45%: in a sold-out market, Samsung and Micron taking unit share need not reset price at all.

**$TSM** — The most-shared negative crowd datapoint is that TSMC is holding roughly $1B of finished Apple processors it cannot ship because DRAM is unavailable for packaging — memory scarcity, not foundry capacity, gating revenue conversion. The thesis models advanced packaging as a separable, TSMC-owned annuity and a TAM-expansion lever; it does not engage the possibility that the packaging line's output is throttled by third-party memory supply the company neither makes nor allocates.

**$GAW** — The crowd's argument is that GW is actively destroying funnel conversion at the exact moment the media flywheel should be catching new entrants: nuking the Space Marine range and letting Deathwatch lapse right as Total War, Dawn of War and Astartes 2 generate mainstream attention, with Space Marine 2's own breakout characterised as unplanned and unexploited rather than the product of a deliberate quality-gated licensing strategy. The thesis models media hits as a one-way conversion engine into a behavioural lock-in system; it does not engage the possibility that range invalidation and rules churn actively repel the converts the licensing slate delivers.

**$MRVL** — NVHBM landing inside the AWS custom-XPU package with Annapurna Labs as first partner attacks exactly the memory-interface and near-memory content Marvell bought Celestial, XConn and Polariton to own, and whose anchor economics — the Amazon warrant vesting on Photonic Fabric purchases through 2030 — the thesis attributes to Trainium 4. The thesis's stated Celestial falsifier is Nvidia CMX/BlueField-4 plus HBF capturing the KV-cache socket; the crowd is showing a different and more direct mechanism, NVIDIA memory technology co-designed into the AWS accelerator itself with NVLink Fusion absorbing the semi-custom integration role, which the thesis does not model.
