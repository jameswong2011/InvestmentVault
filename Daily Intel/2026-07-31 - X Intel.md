## ⚠ Thesis divergence

**$BE** — The crowd banks Bloom's 166% revenue growth and raised guide as validation but does not engage the thesis's central concern — the >40x gap between the cited $20B 'contracted backlog' and the ~$492.6M of audited remaining performance obligations, plus related-party (Brookfield JV) revenue concentration. Revenue acceleration is not backlog-conversion evidence.

**$AAOI** — The recurring crowd claim that AAOI's internal InP fab 'shields gross margins' during a 200G laser shortage directly contradicts the thesis: AAOI's fab makes only 1310nm DFB lasers (CATV/lower-speed), not the 200G EMLs that gate 1.6T, so its high-speed laser supply is merchant-sourced identically to the Chinese assemblers' and provides no margin protection at the bleeding edge — evidenced by flat ~29-30% gross margin despite 51% revenue growth.

**$SIVE** — The crowd treats the partnership inventory (GF SCALE reference laser, Jabil/Ayar/POET chipset mappings, $799M pipeline) as concrete, imminent design-win de-risking, while the thesis holds every named partnership remains pre-revenue and binary — and, most pointedly, the thesis's load-bearing catalyst/risk, the Nasdaq NY dual-listing under the Swedish Economic Crime Authority preliminary investigation, is entirely absent from the crowd's bull case.

**$NVDA** — RihardJarc's claim that NVDA is 'buying its way' into more and bigger orders on a faster timeline via neocloud backstops and equity investments — explicitly 'normally not a healthy sign' — reframes NVDA's own capital deployment as evidence that demand may be partly manufactured, a demand-quality/governance critique the thesis's ASIC-resistance/CUDA-moat and TAM-expansion framing does not engage.

**$INTC** — The crowd credits Intel's DCAI +59% YoY as an agentic-AI CPU tailwind accruing to Xeon, whereas the thesis argues Intel is structurally the weakest x86 vendor at exactly this inflection — Diamond Rapids removed SMT (192c=192t) versus AMD Venice Dense's 256c/512t, a 2.7x thread-density deficit on the parallel sub-agent/tool-call workload the inflection rewards.
