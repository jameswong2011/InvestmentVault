**$SIVE** — The crowd treats SIVE's ecosystem integration (GF SCALE reference laser, MI500 CPO read-through, CW-WDM MSA) as concrete, imminent design-win de-risking, whereas the thesis holds every named partnership is still pre-revenue and binary — and the thesis's load-bearing catalyst/risk, the Nasdaq NY dual-listing under the Swedish Economic Crime Authority preliminary investigation, is entirely absent from the crowd's bull case.

**$NBIS** — ripster47's argument — that Jensen's open-weights push moves the market toward smaller, local inference and away from the heavy training-cluster demand that funds high-debt neoclouds — challenges the thesis's premise that NBIS's demand is de-risked (sold-out, contracted through 2031); the thesis carries hyperscaler in-housing risk but does not engage the open-weights/local-inference demand-erosion vector as a threat to the contracted backlog.

**$HIMS** — JonahLupton (an actual peptide user) flags that if legal peptide prices run 50-100% above grey-market prices, customers simply keep buying from the grey market — a demand-ceiling risk to peptide monetization that the thesis's $300-500M incremental peptide-revenue catalyst treats as clean upside without engaging grey-market price competition.

**$MRVL** — The crowd frames Marvell's >$10B custom-silicon target as a compounding, near-monopoly franchise; the thesis argues the opposite — that the custom-ASIC 'second seat' is a structural procurement feature won by elimination, carrying a ~5pp lower margin ceiling than Broadcom and churning seat-by-seat (Trainium 3 lost to Alchip, Maia contested) — so the category compounds even as individual sockets are lower-quality than the crowd assumes.

**$SOI** — The crowd frames Soitec's optical strength as a clean pure-play beat ('powers 100% of next-gen data centers'), foregrounding only the Photonics-SOI / Edge & Cloud AI growth — it does not engage the thesis's central caveat that RF-SOI/Mobile (52% of revenue, -41% YoY) still dominates the P&L and drove the FY26 revenue collapse, nor the rich ~11.5x EV/revenue multiple and CEO-transition overhang that gate the equity.

**$AAOI** — The crowd claims AAOI's internal InP fab 'insulates it from 200G laser-diode shortages while safeguarding gross margins' — directly contradicting the thesis's core insight that AAOI's fab makes only 1310nm DFB lasers (CATV/lower-speed), NOT the 200G EMLs that gate 1.6T, so its 200G supply is merchant-sourced like the Chinese assemblers' and provides no margin protection at the bleeding edge (evidenced by flat ~29-30% gross margin despite 51% revenue growth).

**$CRWV** — The crowd's central claim is that CRWV's backlog is 'safe' and its financials 'de-risk as the backlog converts' — the direct inverse of the thesis, which argues the load-bearing risk is credit/counterparty (67% Microsoft, OpenAI's $22.4B commitment with no operating cash flow, and the DDTL amortization-cliff on second-cycle GPU re-rent rates already down 50-70%); the crowd treats contracted backlog as forward protection while the thesis treats the re-rent economics behind it as the unpriced credit risk.
