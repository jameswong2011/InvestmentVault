  Batson's argument is that China's 14% REER depreciation from the 2022 peak is entirely a relative-price story, not a currency one — the BIS nominal effective rate is back at its March 2022 peak and the CFETS basket is similar, so the gap comes from Chinese CPI staying flat while trading partners inflated in 2022-23.   Nvidia's developer blog quantifies why long-context agentic inference is a memory problem: on DeepSeek-R1, attention's share of prefill time rises from 18% at 4K context to 85% at 128K, and decode arithmetic intensity scales with group size (QH/KV heads) while tensor parallelism is capped at KV head count before KV cache duplication starts wasting capacity.
