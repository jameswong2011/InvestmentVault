## Summary
Five days after Innolight’s $6.8B raise, Reuters’ FCC import-ban draft sparked double-digit US optical moves. PhotonCap reads Innolight disclosures: US customer revenue 61.7% in Q1’26, yet goods made in mainland China sold to US ~0–1% historically—volume already offshore (TeraHop/Thailand). Draft lacks clear “Chinese” definition (company nationality vs place of manufacture). Article asks whether US-made supply (and InP substrate chain) can absorb redirected demand; concludes regulation exposes that without Chinese module capacity, US AI DCs still depend on that supply chain.
