## Thesis Delta
Consensus treated QCOM Q3'26 as a mixed handset quarter with diversification hope → IA reads the call as Apple accelerating Qualcomm modem design-out ahead of the Apr 1 2027 licensing expiration, with Verizon likely green-lighting next-gen iPhones without mmWave (last gate to a global Apple modem). Handset down-spec as memory eats BOM structurally dilutes QCOM value; datacenter "Dragonfly" still RTL/simulation with no test vehicle. Challenges any thesis that QTL/Apple share stays at the previously expected ~20% around Mar/Apr 2027.

## Summary
IA frames Qualcomm's Jul 29 2026 earnings as the tell that Apple is preparing a "holy war" against QTL while Android is crushed by the memory crisis. Key call/slide tells: (1) China smartphone has not bottomed once seasonality is stripped; (2) selling modems to Apple is highest-margin QCT—so "capacity constraints" as the excuse for mix is weak and folded under sell-side challenge; (3) down-spec of premium smartphone silicon is structural as DRAM/NAND take BOM share and OEMs keep cheaper SKUs if consumers do not notice; (4) Apple silently started FCC (and foreign) qualification of an iPhone lineup that would leave QCOM at low-single-digit share (foldable-only?) vs prior ~20% expectation; (5) Apple waited until late to notify Qualcomm to maximize pain and banked modems for inventory into the war; (6) new datacenter revenue is small and margin-dilutive; investor-day racks lack silicon to test—IA brands Dragonfly as vapor. Author holds puts (tiny). Monitoring ask: Verizon C-band deployment completeness.
