# Amazon Lifts 2026 Capex to ~$220B; AWS Still Capacity-Constrained into 2028

## Thesis Delta
After Alphabet’s $195–205B 2026 capex raise (already ingested 2026-08-12) the market prices (and has been selling) hyperscaler capex raises. Amazon’s Q2 call (recapped 31 Jul, still circulating) lifts 2026 capex to ~$220B from ~$200B, with Jassy citing *higher memory costs* as the primary delta and saying capacity stays short through 2027 with contracted demand into 2028. AWS $42.2B (+36.7%, fastest in 18 quarters), $169B ARR, AI + custom silicon each >$25B ARR, AWS backlog $496B. ## Summary
Data Center Knowledge recap of Amazon Q2 call: 2026 capex ~$220B vs ~$200B prior, “primarily reflects higher memory costs rather than a” broader demand miss. Jassy: still not enough capacity in 2026 *or* 2027; 2028 demand “striking.” AWS revenue $42.2B (+36.7% YoY), operating income $16.6B, $169B annualized run-rate; AI and custom-silicon businesses each >$25B ARR. AWS backlog $496B, triple-digit YoY. Framing: bottleneck is delivering capacity (power, servers, memory), not finding customers. Complements the Alphabet $195–205B note rather than duplicating it.
