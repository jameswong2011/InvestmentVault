# Nebius Q2: ARR $3.0B; YE26 Contracted Power Raised to 5 GW

## Thesis Delta
Consensus (and the correlated-twin multiple vs CRWV) prices NBIS as a demand/GPU-rental print. The vault’s NBIS thesis is an *execution* bet (energize hundreds of MW off a thin base), not a demand bet. Shareholder letter raises YE26 *contracted* power (land+power commitments, not energized MW) to 5 GW from >4 GW last quarter, with AI-cloud ARR $3.0B, four >$1B TCV deals at $20–25M/MW, $40B customer commitments and >$9B 2026 prepayments. Demand/pricing is even more de-risked; the binding constraint remains physical energization — they plan >1 GW/year *deployed starting 2027*. Read-through to [[Theses/NVDA - Nvidia]] GPU sell-through. Mental-model triggers: [G-7] ROIIC of power/capex mines; [G-10] base-rate of datacenter energization slips; Industry #1 bottleneck has moved to power.

## Summary
Nebius Q2 2026 letter (released August 11, 2026): group revenue $582.3M (+454% YoY); Nebius AI cloud $575M (+514% YoY); ARR $3.0B. YE26 contracted-power target 5 GW (path this year: >2.5 → >3 → >4 → 5 GW). Average new-deal yield >$20M/MW; four landmark deals >$1B TCV each at $20–25M/MW. $40B customer commitments; expect >$9B customer prepayments in 2026; FY26 guidance reaffirmed (letter). $775M secured facility in July against one of those agreements. Cash cited ~$8B including $2.3B Q2 operating cash flow. Deploy >1 GW/year starting 2027. Contracted ≠ energized — thesis still turns on bringing MW to market.
