## Summary
SK hynix board (August 7, 2026) approved ~KRW 54T: KRW 35.2T Yongin “Y2” (cleanroom Jun 2029) and KRW 19.1T Cheongju “M17” (cleanroom Dec 2028), executing the June master plan of KRW 600T Yongin cluster + KRW 100T Cheongju expansion. Y1 is already under construction; Y2/M17 are the subsequent fabs. Omdia cited: DRAM+NAND demand CAGR 19% through 2030, framed as structural (memory as AI compute input) not a temporary supercycle. Stated purpose: expand DRAM *and* NAND production bases aligned with customer demand and “stability of the global AI semiconductor supply chain.” This is *not* the EE Times Asia “2027 mass-prod / 300k wpm” teaser — those dates/capacities are not in the company PR; use PR cleanroom dates.
