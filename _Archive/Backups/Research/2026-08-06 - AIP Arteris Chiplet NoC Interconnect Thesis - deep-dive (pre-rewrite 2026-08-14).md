## Summary
Arteris sells network-on-chip IP (FlexNoC/Ncore) plus Magillem integration software and Cycuity security tools. FY25 mix ~90% license/support vs ~9% royalties; 83 design starts in 2025; >4B SoCs/chiplets cumulative. Chiplet/UCIe adoption multiplies interconnect instances. Moat: switching costs + ASIL-D auto certification (~70–80% share in ADAS NoC per Temple 8); AMD licenses despite Infinity Fabric. Risks: Synopsys/Cadence bundling; hyperscaler/IDM internalization; small scale vs platform EDA vendors. Q1'26 rev +39% YoY to $22.94M; FY26 guide $91–95M.
