# Nvidia Spectrum-6 CPO Switches Shipping; Multi-Vendor Test Ecosystem Still Missing

## Thesis Delta
Consensus CPO narrative is binary: either pluggables die (bear LITE/AAOI) or CPO is vapor (bull pluggables). OCP APAC 2026 (Taipei, wrapped 12 Aug): NVDA SVP Gilad Shainer confirmed Spectrum-6 CPO switches are *in production and shipping* (early adopters include CoreWeave, Lambda, Meta, Microsoft, OCI) on TSMC COUPE — while ASE’s packaging house warned the *multi-vendor* test/assembly ecosystem does not exist. Productizes CPO for [[Theses/NVDA - Nvidia]] lock-in and [[Theses/TSM - Taiwan Semiconductor]] COUPE/CoWoS; preserves a near-term pluggable window for [[Theses/LITE - Lumentum]] / [[Theses/AAOI - Applied Optoelectronics]] (Bechtolsheim: one more switch generation of pluggables). Mental-model triggers: Industry #8 (CPO remaps optical bottleneck) + #2 (qualification-gate); Value Layer Monopoly §2 falling-switching-cost disqualifier is *not* firing yet — NVDA’s CPO is proprietary.

## Summary
TechTimes recap (August 12, 2026) of OCP APAC Summit (TaiNEX2, Taipei). Shainer: Spectrum-6 CPO in production/shipping; capacity expected to expand through the year. Manufacturing foundation: TSMC COUPE (Compact Universal Photonic Engine), co-developed over years; COUPE bandwidth-density roadmap from 0.5 Tb/s/mm (cited). TSMC, AMAT, ASE shared a keynote stage with MSFT/NVDA/Google. ASE: ecosystem required to generalize the milestone to a multi-vendor market is not there (test/assembly procedural gap). Bechtolsheim: buy another pluggable generation while CPO standards catch up; CPO becomes structurally necessary beyond copper’s ~25 cm 448G reach. June 2026 analyst line (yields, etc.) still: NVDA shipping ≠ industry-wide CPO readiness. Open specs work ongoing for interoperable CPO.
