## Thesis Delta
Consensus cheers Lumentum’s first $1B quarter as a pure transceiver boom print → PhotonCap argues the real news is **five in-rack optics signals** (1.6T production, OCS triple-digit quarter path, UHP CPO lasers, first ELS PO, broadening NPO) that expand LITE’s TAM beyond pluggables into scale-up, while the **$7.2B GAAP loss is non-cash convert equitization**. Supports [[Theses/LITE - Lumentum]] as a multi-S-curve optics compounder, not a one-cycle EML story.

## Summary
FQ4’26 revenue $1,006.3M (+24.5% QoQ / +109.3% YoY); non-GAAP OM 36.6% (+2,160bps YoY); FQ1’27 guide $1.225–1.275B rev and 39.5–40.5% OM—OFC target model early. GAAP net loss $7.2B from ~$7.8B debt-extinguishment accounting on convert-to-equity (~$1.1B debt retired). Components $649.4M and Systems $356.9M both >2× YoY. Five signals: 1.6T production start; OCS doubling with first $100M+ quarter guided; UHP CPO demand accelerating (HVM 2H27, scale-up 2028); first ELS module PO (deliver 2H27); NPO engagements broadening. EML supply gap persists; high-power CW shortage intensified, motivating AXT substrate LTA. China InP competition “no impact yet” per mgmt. Stock reaction muted (~+2% AH) with shares still >20% below 52w high.
