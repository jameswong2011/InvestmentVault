## Summary
White House Aug 6 Section 232 action covers polysilicon and derivatives with (1) minimum import prices ($21/kg poly, $100/kg ingots/wafers, $0.22/W cells, $0.38/W modules), (2) additional 15% Section 232 on specified derivatives from Dec 4, 2026 (not a blanket extra 15% on all raw poly), and (3) Commerce onshoring incentives allowing tariff relief while US capacity is built (construction start by Jan 20, 2029). Policy logic: US poly capacity share fell ~50% (2005) to <2% (2024); wafer-fab share 37% (1990) to ~10% (2024); semiconductor-grade poly is only ~2.4% of global poly output, so solar-scale economics underwrite electronic-grade security. Friend-shoring: Japan/Korea/Taiwan/EU/Switzerland/Liechtenstein generally face a combined ordinary+232 rate structured to 15% (UK 10%). TSPA frames this as precedent for a broader materials perimeter (wafers, chemicals, gases, photoresists, substrates, copper foil, glass fabrics). Timing coincides with SIA Q2'26 worldwide sales $403.3B (+35.1% QoQ) and 2026 revenue expected >$1.5T — restrictions arrive into a demand boom.
