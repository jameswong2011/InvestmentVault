## Thesis Delta
Consensus frames Apple–CXMT talks as a China localization / cheap-DRAM story → SemiVision/TSPA argues the real move is **pricing-power dilution**: a credible fourth supplier in the OEM qualification pool changes negotiation behavior for Samsung/SK hynix/Micron even at ~7% global DRAM revenue share. Bifurcation: HBM/AI DRAM stays concentrated and expensive; PC/mobile/commodity DRAM becomes more dual-sourced and regionalized. Relevant to [[Theses/000660 - SK Hynix]] and [[Theses/MU]] via mix-shift incentives, not near-term HBM displacement.

## Summary
WSJ (Aug 9) reported Apple testing CXMT DRAM for iPhone/MacBook lines and preliminary China-device discussions; Reuters could not independently verify. CXMT now ships DDR5 and LPDDR5/5X, with customers including Alibaba Cloud, ByteDance, Tencent, Lenovo, Xiaomi and others per IPO prospectus. HP/Acer (and reportedly Asus) already qualify CXMT outside the US. Under tight AI-driven memory allocation, Apple’s interest skews to **supply optionality and procurement leverage**, not just unit cost—some CXMT product reportedly priced near or above Korean/US peers. Geopolitics adds a second qualification layer: CXMT is on DoD 1260H but (as of Aug 11, 2026) not on the BIS Entity List. Downstream spillover: packaging/test/modules and China equipment/materials HVM qualification.
