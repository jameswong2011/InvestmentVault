## Thesis Delta
Consensus still slots Google as an AI “Big 3” frontier lab whose cloud is secondary → SemiAnalysis’s read is that **DeepMind/Gemini has exited frontier contention** (talent exodus, canceled 3.5 Pro, weak Flash) while **Thomas Kurian’s GCP won the internal compute war**, financializing TPUs into third-party IaaS/TaaS and system sales (including to Anthropic/Meta). Market should price Google less as AGI upside and more as a high-growth infra vendor—bullish near-term GCP EPS, bearish long-duration Gemini optionality.

## Summary
High-profile departures (Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, Oriol Vinyals forming Discovery Loop; Koray replacing Demis) signal leadership collapse after Gemini 3 Pro’s late-2025 peak. Institutional Tokenomics note (Jul 9) already called widening gap vs OpenAI/Anthropic, flopped 3.5 Flash, canceled 3.5 Pro, and Gemini now ~8th–9th. Root cause: lack of AGI conviction—selling >20% of 3Q26–4Q27 TPU shipments to Anthropic and renting hundreds of thousands of TPUs to rivals. Gemini ARR ~$12B in 2Q26 vs GCP path to >$73B third-party AI ARR IaaS/TaaS + ~$120B TPU sales by end-2027; 2Q26 GCP growth 82% (core low-70s after ~$1.2B TPU system sales). >$150B TPU systems backlog can push 2027 GCP growth to mid-100s vs sell-side ~64%, adding ~$3 EPS. Irony: GDM invented the transformer but is choosing IBM/Intel-style financialization over frontier RSI.
