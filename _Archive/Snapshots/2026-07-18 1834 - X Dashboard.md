- @StormDirac quoting Ayar's Vishal Chandrasekar: optical scale-up is now practical because the manufacturing ecosystem has matured around co-packaged optics, with hybrid bonding letting electronic and photonic chiplets be built separately then integrated into one optical engine converting electrical signals to light millimeters from compute silicon.
- Distinct approaches within NVLink Fusion: Ayar Labs pursues optical chiplets beside processors while Lightmatter pursues photonic interposers beneath compute chips, with Marvell also named; integrating large numbers of lasers efficiently and reliably remains the key unsolved challenge.
