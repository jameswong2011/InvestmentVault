# SPCX - SpaceX

## Summary
SPCX at ~$140 is two capital pools consolidated into one Class A residual: a **proven, owned Connectivity annuity** (Starlink: $4.291B Q2 revenue, 60.5% adj. EBITDA margin, 12.0M subs, riding Falcon's launch-cost monopoly — 73% of group adj. EBITDA) and an **unproven AI capital-recovery bet** ($15.828B of $18.369B Q2 capex against $2.561B AI revenue, a $(1.257)B operating loss, and Cloud Service Agreements terminable on 90 days' notice post-ramp). Sell-side prices the second pool as if proven — avg PT ~$232 (range $62–$800), MS $300 on the SemiAnalysis 10 GW / MSFT-largest-offtaker path — while the printed record supports only 1.4 GW nameplate, a ~2 GW YE26 company target, and $14.1B of contracted sales counted under a non-cancellable-window definition that covers 89% of a single quarter's AI build. The mispriced variable is not GW count; it is **contract duration versus GPU/turbine depreciation lives**. Conviction medium: Connectivity cash, net cash ~$60.6B, and printed anchor tenants (Anthropic bought out all of Colossus 1; Google, Reflection followed) keep it off low; unproven AI ROIC, the widest large-cap valuation dispersion on the board ($63 Morningstar FV to $800 high), and cancellable paper keep it off high.

**AI (renter pool).** Colossus campuses + CSAs. Tenants: Anthropic (all of Colossus 1), Google, Reflection AI, unnamed others. Cursor adds an application-layer coding franchise folding into Grok branding.

**Space (shipping, not the residual).** Internal mass-to-orbit is the Starlink factory; customer launch ($648M) is the third-party line. Starship is R&D toward a 99% cost-to-orbit cut and V3 mass deployment — not in 2026 constellation or revenue math.

## Bull Case

Connectivity compounds as an owned annuity: the June hike lifts Q3 ARPU without a churn break, enterprise/Starshield mix (42.1% and rising, >$6B awards) extends duration, and Leo — commercial start slipped to late-2026 with a limited enterprise/gov/mobility initial scope — proves too late and too thin (~400 of 3,000+ authorized sats in orbit vs Starlink's operating constellation + V3 pipeline) to break pricing. The AI campus converts: YE26 prints ≥2 GW on the 1.4 GW definition, CSAs convert to matched-cohort payback or long-duration non-cancellable paper, and a named GW-scale hyperscaler offtake (the unverified MSFT 3 GW, or equivalent) lands on 2026–27 MW — at which point the SemiAnalysis 10 GW / ~$100B-per-GW path acquires a denominator and SPCX becomes the only neocloud whose build is funded by internal cash ($100B + $47.5B backlog) rather than the sector's strained credit stack. Cursor (closed 14 Aug) converts colo into application-layer attach (enterprise coding accounts + Grok distribution), showing up in Q4 AI solutions mix. Starship V3 makes the constellation lead unassailable post-2026 (1 Tbps sats at Starship mass economics). Valuation path: Connectivity annuity (~$10.4B adj. EBITDA run-rate, growing with price extraction) + funded AI option re-rates toward the MS $300 framing as duration disclosure de-risks the second pool.

What has to go right: Connectivity margin band holds, CSA duration prints, YE26 nameplate lands on-definition, no termination cascade, power buildout keeps pace (Memphis turbine replacement, Terafab generation).

## Bear Case

The High-multiple pool is the unproven one. AI capex at $15.8B/quarter builds a depreciating Rubin book ($1.885B quarterly AI D&A and compounding) whose customers can walk on 90 days' notice after ramp — and those customers are competitors (Anthropic, Google) with structural exit incentive the moment Meta's excess-compute resale or neocloud capacity offers an alternative. First termination cascade, or YE26 <2 GW, or a Connectivity margin break, and the ~$1.9T full valuation compresses toward the satellite-broadband + launch stub — Morningstar's $63 FV marks where sum-of-parts-ex-AI pessimism already sits, and the market briefly priced $104.83 (−11% on the capex print) before the unlock-week recovery. Starlink is not immune: ARPU already fell $99→$66, the June extraction tests price-anger-vs-churn with Leo's commercial start slipped to late-2026 but its enterprise beta already carrying carrier distribution (Verizon/AT&T/Vodafone) and Jassy signaling sub-Starlink pricing, and the Jul-2025 global outage + congestion complaints are the product-quality tell in an underserved-rural base with rising alternatives. The 10 GW / $500B-ARR / Terafab / MSFT-largest narrative stack is unverified aspiration priced as pipeline by the bullish half of a $62–$800 dispersion. Supply overhang persists into December (next ~319M unlock 20 Aug; ~40% tradeable by year-end); Cursor's $60B all-stock close issued 389.3M Class A shares (~3% dilution) for an application-layer asset that does not move the capital-recovery clock; related-party interest ($327M Q2) and key-person concentration compound governance discount. World where this loses money: stranded Rubin fleet + Connectivity churn inflection + multiple compression from 34×/59× run-rate toward the stub — no fundamental miss required, just duration disclosure that never arrives.

## Risks

**Thesis risks (the case is wrong)**
- **Capital recovery:** 90-day post-ramp exits < GPU/turbine D&A lives; termination cascade among tenant-competitors; $15.8B/qtr capex uncovered by the non-cancellable book.

- **Jevons non-transfer:** AI colo stays a layer-renter (NVDA/HBM/optics/turbines); the Starlink ownership exception does not repeat; hardware chain captures the value (PhotonCap).
- **Power architecture:** Memphis turbine removal by 2027 + unproven Terafab islanding + 24–48mo interconnect queues cap nameplate growth below target.

**Position risks (thesis right, stock down anyway)**

- **Supply:** lock-up tranches into December (next ~319M unlock 20 Aug; ~40% tradeable by year-end); Cursor close added 389.3M Class A shares (~3% count).
- **Key-person / governance:** Musk concentration, $327M Q2 related-party interest, dual-class structure; unverified ~2–2.5y median tenure as execution-fragility signal.
- **Narrative unwind:** 10 GW / Terafab / MSFT-largest headlines deflating hits the multiple even while Connectivity prints.
