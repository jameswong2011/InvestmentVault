**Educational frame:** Company-specific educational analysis, not personalised financial advice. Disclaimer delivery is description-only in all 15 episodes. #### English Script

One of the biggest financial winners from artificial intelligence doesn't design the AI model or the graphics processor. It makes the memory that keeps the processor fed with data.

That company is SK hynix, a South Korean chipmaker. Its core products are DRAM, the short-term working memory used by computers, and NAND, the longer-term storage used in solid-state drives. The product transforming the business is high-bandwidth memory, or HBM. HBM sits close to an AI processor and moves far more data at once than ordinary memory.

The change in SK hynix's numbers has been extreme. In the first quarter of 2026, the company reported 52.6 trillion won of revenue and 37.6 trillion won of operating profit. That is a 72 percent operating margin for a business that investors historically treated like a commodity manufacturer.

The company also listed depositary receipts on Nasdaq in July, making it easier for American investors to own. The shares jumped on debut, and the offering raised $26.5 billion for a company preparing another wave of memory investment.

Here is the tension. The bull sees SK hynix as a toll collector on every new AI system: faster processors need more specialised memory, and only a few suppliers can make it reliably. The bear sees a familiar memory cycle wearing an AI label: shortages create record prices, record profits fund new factories, and new supply eventually destroys the returns.

So this series is not asking whether SK hynix is a real AI beneficiary. It clearly is. The question is whether HBM has changed the economics for long enough to change the investment.

To answer that, we first need to see why a powerful AI chip can still be useless without the right memory beside it.

争议也由此出现。多头认为，SK海力士是每一套新AI系统都要经过的收费站：处理器越快，就越需要专用内存，而真正能稳定量产的供应商只有少数几家。空头看到的却是一个披着AI外衣的传统内存周期：短缺推高价格，创纪录的利润带来新工厂，而新增供应最终摧毁回报。

### Episode 02 — Why a Faster GPU Still Needs HBM

**Series role:** Explain HBM's function and why memory bandwidth can bind AI performance.

**Standalone promise:** A new viewer will understand the difference between computing and feeding the computation.

**Freshness hook:** SK hynix shipped HBM4E samples in June 2026 at up to 16 gigabits per second per pin.

**Suggested video title:** Why Faster AI Chips Still Wait for Memory

**建议视频标题（简体中文）:** 为什么更快的AI芯片仍在等待内存

#### Video Description

A faster AI processor is wasted if data cannot reach it quickly enough. This episode explains high-bandwidth memory through a simple factory analogy, then connects HBM4 and HBM4E bandwidth, power efficiency, and software compression to the real question: is memory demand a durable bottleneck or a solvable engineering constraint? Educational content only, not financial advice. I hold a position in SK hynix.

#### 视频简介（简体中文）

如果数据送不到处理器，再快的AI芯片也会浪费算力。本集用工厂与装卸平台的比喻解释高带宽内存，并把HBM4、HBM4E的带宽和能效，与软件压缩带来的反作用放在一起。核心问题是：内存会长期成为AI瓶颈，还是一个最终可以被工程优化解决的问题？内容仅供学习，不构成投资建议。本人持有SK海力士的仓位。

#### English Script

A faster AI processor does not help if it spends its time waiting for data. That waiting problem is why high-bandwidth memory has become one of the most valuable parts of an AI system.

Think of the processor as a factory with thousands of machines. Normal computer memory is a warehouse across the road. High-bandwidth memory, or HBM, is a giant loading dock built against the factory wall, with many more doors operating at the same time.

The design is physical. Instead of placing memory chips separately on a board, HBM stacks multiple layers of DRAM vertically and connects them with tiny pathways through the silicon. The stack sits beside the graphics processor inside the same advanced package. Shorter distance, more connections, and wider data lanes let the processor receive far more information every second.

SK hynix says its HBM4 generation doubles the number of input-output connections to 2,048 and improves power efficiency by more than 40 percent versus the previous generation. Its newer HBM4E sample reaches up to 16 gigabits per second per pin, or roughly four terabytes per second for a stack.

Those numbers matter because modern AI models repeatedly move huge sets of weights and temporary data. When that movement becomes the bottleneck, buying more processor power without enough bandwidth is like adding machines to a factory while leaving the loading dock unchanged.

There is a counterpoint. Better software, compression, and smaller models can reduce the memory needed for each task. More efficient AI does not automatically mean less total memory, because lower cost can also create more usage, but demand is not guaranteed.

HBM is valuable because it removes a real system constraint. The next question is how SK hynix turns that engineering role into revenue and profit.

#### 中文脚本（普通话，简体）

如果AI处理器大部分时间都在等数据，那么处理器再快也没有用。正因为这个等待问题，高带宽内存才成为AI系统中最有价值的部件之一。

可以把处理器想成一座拥有几千台机器的工厂。普通电脑内存就像马路对面的仓库。高带宽内存，也就是HBM，则像直接贴着工厂建造的巨型装卸平台，而且同时开放更多入口。

这种差异来自物理设计。HBM不是把内存芯片分散放在电路板上，而是把多层DRAM垂直堆叠，再用穿过硅片的微小通道连接起来。整组内存与图形处理器一起放在先进封装内。距离更短、连接更多、数据通道更宽，因此每秒能送入处理器的数据大幅增加。

SK海力士表示，HBM4把输入输出连接数量翻倍至2,048个，相比上一代，能效提高超过40%。更新的HBM4E样品，每个引脚速度最高达到16Gbps，单个堆栈的总带宽约为4TB/s。

这些数字之所以重要，是因为现代AI模型会反复搬运海量权重和临时数据。如果数据传输成为瓶颈，只增加处理器数量，就像不断给工厂增加机器，却完全不扩建装卸平台。

但这里也有反面因素。更好的软件、压缩技术和更小的模型，都可以降低每项任务所需的内存。效率提高不一定会减少总内存需求，因为成本下降也可能创造更多使用量，但需求增长绝不是自动发生的。

HBM的价值，在于它解决了一个真实的系统限制。接下来的问题是，SK海力士如何把这个工程位置转化为收入和利润。

#### Video Description

HBM requires good memory dies, precise stacking, vertical connections, thermal control, and customer qualification—all at once. This episode explains compounded yield, warpage, heat, and SK hynix’s MR-MUF process, while showing why a genuine manufacturing lead still has to be rebuilt every generation. The distinction determines how durable current pricing power may be. Educational content only, not financial advice. I hold a position in SK hynix.

#### 视频简介（简体中文）

HBM要求多颗合格芯片、精确堆叠、垂直互连、热管理与客户认证同时成功。本集解释良率叠加、翘曲、散热和SK海力士的MR-MUF工艺，并说明为什么真实的制造领先，仍然必须在每一代产品中重新建立。内容仅供学习，不构成投资建议。本人持有SK海力士的仓位。

#### English Script

High-bandwidth memory is difficult because the manufacturer has to make several good chips, stack them perfectly, connect them vertically, manage the heat, and then prove the finished package works beside a very expensive processor.

Start with the stack. HBM places multiple layers of DRAM on top of one another. Tiny metal pathways carry data through those layers to a base chip underneath. Every extra layer creates another chance for a defect, alignment error, or weak connection. One bad die can reduce the value of the whole stack.

Then comes heat and physical stress. Different materials expand at different rates. A tall stack can warp during assembly, and trapped heat can reduce performance or reliability. SK hynix's main packaging process is called mass reflow moulded underfill, shortened to MR-MUF. It bonds the stack and fills the gaps in one moulding process, helping control warpage and move heat away.

The company's June 2026 HBM4E sample still used an advanced version of this process. SK hynix says it lowered heat resistance by 17 percent while supporting speeds up to 16 gigabits per second per pin.

Manufacturing success is measured by yield: the share of output good enough to sell. A rival can have more nominal factory capacity and still ship fewer qualified stacks if its yield is worse. The customer also has to validate the memory with a specific processor and package, so a late redesign can miss an entire product generation.

That creates pricing power, but not permanent ownership. Samsung and Micron can improve. A new assembly method can reset the race. Customer qualification can move from one preferred supplier to several.

HBM is therefore not a pure commodity, and it is not software-like lock-in. It is a difficult physical product whose moat must be rebuilt every generation. That is the right frame for comparing SK hynix with Samsung and Micron.

#### 中文脚本（普通话，简体）

高带宽内存之所以困难，是因为制造商必须先做出多颗合格芯片，再把它们精准堆叠、垂直连接、控制热量，最后还要证明整个封装能在一颗极其昂贵的处理器旁边稳定工作。

先看堆栈。HBM把多层DRAM叠在一起，微小的金属通道穿过这些层，把数据传到底部的基础芯片。每增加一层，就多一次出现缺陷、对位错误或连接不良的机会。只要有一颗裸片出问题，整个堆栈的价值就可能大幅下降。

接下来是热量和物理应力。不同材料受热后的膨胀程度不同，高堆栈在组装时可能翘曲，积聚的热量也会降低性能和可靠性。SK海力士的主要封装工艺叫做大规模回流模塑底填，简称MR-MUF。它在一次模塑过程中连接堆栈并填充缝隙，帮助控制翘曲和散热。

公司在2026年6月推出的HBM4E样品，仍然使用这项工艺的升级版。SK海力士表示，新工艺把热阻降低17%，同时支持每个引脚最高16Gbps的速度。

制造成功与否，要看良率，也就是最终产出中有多少可以出售。竞争对手即使拥有更多名义产能，如果良率较低，能交付的合格堆栈仍可能更少。客户还必须让内存与特定处理器和封装完成验证，因此一次晚到的重新设计，就可能错过整整一代产品。

这会创造定价权，但不会带来永久所有权。三星和美光可以改善工艺，新的组装方式也可能重置竞争，客户认证也可能从单一首选供应商变成多家供应。

因此，HBM既不是纯粹的大宗商品，也不是软件式锁定。它是一种困难的实体产品，护城河必须每一代重新建立。用这个框架，才能正确比较SK海力士、三星和美光。

#### English Script

SK hynix has a real packaging advantage, but calling it a permanent moat goes too far.

Its current edge is mass reflow moulded underfill, or MR-MUF. During assembly, multiple memory layers are connected and the gaps are filled with a moulding material. Done well, the process reduces warpage, improves heat flow, and lifts the share of finished stacks that work.

That is not marketing trivia. Better yield means more sellable HBM from the same expensive wafer capacity. 第三，大客户开始签订更长期的供货协议，因为内存供应可能限制整个数据中心建设。合同不会消灭周期，但可以让销量和投资规划不那么脆弱。

目前的证据异常强劲。SK海力士在2026年第一季度实现72%的营业利润率。HBM4已经进入生产，HBM4E样品每个引脚速度最高达到16Gbps，而公司仍在需求紧张时投资新产能。

真正的差异化观点是：即使业务已经变成半周期型，投资者仍可能把这些利润全部当成暂时的大宗商品价格暴涨。这里的“半周期”意味着公司仍然波动，但谷底利润率更高，份额受到客户认证保护，而且需求具有多年可见度。

不过，多头仍然必须战胜内存行业的历史基准。我的验证标准是：即使SK海力士份额逐步正常化，HBM价格仍至少连续2个季度保持稳定；而新产能到来以后，利润率仍高于过去周期峰值。只有这样，才能证明客户支付的是认证性能，而不只是稀缺溢价。

### Episode 13 — Solidigm and the Memory Hierarchy Option

**Series role:** Explain the upside and execution risk beyond today's core HBM franchise.

**Standalone promise:** A new viewer will understand why AI needs several memory tiers and where Solidigm or HBF could fit.

**Freshness hook:** SK hynix now presents HBM, low-power server memory, and enterprise solid-state drives as one connected AI-memory portfolio.

#### Video Description

AI systems cannot keep every piece of data in expensive HBM. This episode maps the memory hierarchy from HBM to DRAM and NAND, explains Solidigm’s enterprise-storage role, and treats high-bandwidth flash as a speculative middle tier that still needs standards, software, and customer adoption. Educational content only, not financial advice. I hold a position in SK hynix.

#### 视频简介（简体中文）

AI系统不可能把所有数据都放在昂贵的HBM中。本集梳理从HBM、DRAM到NAND的内存层级，解释Solidigm在企业存储中的角色，并把高带宽闪存视为一个仍需标准、软件与客户采用验证的潜在中间层。内容仅供学习，不构成投资建议。本人持有SK海力士的仓位。

#### English Script

The most expensive memory should hold the data an AI processor needs immediately. Everything else needs a cheaper place to wait.

That simple rule creates a memory hierarchy. High-bandwidth memory sits closest to the processor and delivers extreme speed, but it is costly and limited in capacity. Conventional DRAM holds a larger working set. NAND flash stores much more data at a lower cost, but responds more slowly.

SK hynix has exposure to all three layers. It agreed to pay $9 billion for Intel's NAND and storage business, and the first closing created Solidigm in December 2021. Solidigm sells high-capacity solid-state drives to data centres, including products built with QLC NAND, a design that stores more bits in each memory cell. That lowers cost per unit of storage, which matters when AI systems keep vast model files, training data, and inference records.

The vault's more speculative option is high-bandwidth flash, or HBF. The idea is to stack NAND in a form that offers much more bandwidth than a normal storage drive. It would not replace HBM for the hottest data. It could create a middle tier: slower than HBM, far larger and cheaper, and close enough to the processor for workloads that do not need maximum speed every moment.

The upside is larger system value. SK hynix would sell not just the fastest memory, but a combination of fast, medium, and persistent tiers designed around the same AI workload.

The counterpoint is adoption. A simulated architecture is not a product ecosystem. HBF needs standards, controller technology, software support, and a processor customer willing to design around it. Solidigm also remains exposed to the volatile NAND cycle.

So treat this as optionality, not the base case. The current HBM business must justify the investment on its own. Solidigm and HBF add value only when customers name deployments, volumes, and economics.

With the pieces now in place, the next step is a five-signal dashboard that tells us which story is becoming true.

SK海力士同时覆盖这3层。公司同意以90亿美元收购英特尔的NAND与存储业务，并在2021年12月完成第一阶段交割，成立Solidigm。Solidigm向数据中心销售高容量固态硬盘，其中包括使用QLC NAND的产品。QLC让每个内存单元保存更多位元，从而降低单位存储成本。当AI系统需要保留巨大的模型文件、训练数据和推理记录时，这一点非常重要。

资料库中更具投机性质的选择权，是高带宽闪存HBF。这个概念把NAND堆叠成一种带宽远高于普通存储硬盘的形式。它不会取代HBM来处理最热的数据，而是可能创造一个中间层：速度低于HBM，但容量更大、成本更低，而且离处理器足够近，适合那些并非每一刻都需要最高速度的工作负载。

它的上行逻辑，是提高SK海力士在整个系统中的价值。公司销售的不再只是最快内存，而是围绕同一种AI工作负载，组合快速、中速和持久存储层。

#### Video Description

#### English Script

The SK hynix debate can be reduced to one question: has high-bandwidth memory permanently improved the economics, or merely extended the most profitable memory cycle in history?

The bull starts with a real bottleneck. AI processors need enormous memory bandwidth, and SK hynix has proved it can manufacture qualified stacks at scale. The company reported a 72 percent operating margin in the first quarter of 2026. Its HBM4E sample reached up to 16 gigabits per second per pin, and the same shift toward HBM is restricting ordinary DRAM supply. Longer customer agreements may turn some volatile spot demand into visible multi-year volume.

In that world, SK hynix is reclassified. It remains cyclical, but qualification, customer co-design, and higher trough margins deserve a better valuation than a commodity producer.

The bear responds that every cycle looks structural at peak earnings. Samsung is shipping commercial HBM4 and has sampled HBM4E. Micron is shipping HBM4 for Rubin. Conventional DRAM prices are still rising, but the quarterly increase has slowed. SK hynix is committing huge capital and just raised $26.5 billion on Nasdaq. The supply response arrives late, then meets efficiency gains, weaker consumer demand, or slower hyperscaler spending.

In that world, today's low-looking earnings multiple is a warning. The market is correctly discounting profits that new capacity and competition will compress.

My evidence-weighted classification as of July 19, 2026 is a qualification-gated semi-cyclical: better than plain commodity DRAM, but not a permanent monopoly. The business has won the current generation. The investment case has not yet proved that today's economics survive the next capacity wave.

Two observations can settle it. The bull wins if HBM pricing stays firm while supplier share normalises and margins remain above old peaks after capacity arrives. The bear wins if Rubin becomes near-parity multi-sourcing while price increases fall to single digits and capex keeps climbing.

The decision rule is simple: follow qualified volume, pricing, and the supply response — not the loudest AI headline.
