# SOI — Soitec SA

## Summary

Soitec is two businesses traded as one engineered-substrate company, with sell-side anchored to the wrong half. RF-SOI / Mobile Communications (52% of FY26 revenue, -41% YoY constant currency, Q4 -52% YoY) dominates the framing and drove the FY26 revenue collapse to €592M (-34%) — but the underlying story is that Photonics-SOI (>$100M FY26, mgmt guides 20-30% annual growth) is the **sole volume-qualified substrate supplier at all three CPO silicon-photonics foundries** (TSMC COUPE, GlobalFoundries Fotonix, Tower PH18) at the exact moment hyperscale CPO commercialisation begins (TSMC COUPE in risk production with AMD Feb 2026; Broadcom Bailly has shipped 50K+ switches; NVIDIA Quantum-X engines are TSMC COUPE-based). The non-consensus claim is that the market is valuing this near-monopoly substrate position at zero while pricing in a worst-case RF-SOI extension — the inverse of the actual structural setup. Conviction is medium: the Photonics-SOI thesis is real and the moat (qualification across three foundries + 4,300+ Smart Cut patents + 30-year process refinement) is durable; offset by a ~€6.7B market cap at ~11.5x EV/Revenue / ~45x EV/EBITDA on FY26 leaves no margin for the cyclical earnings miss that low FY27 fab loading + FX + lower French CIR funding has already been pre-announced for, and the CEO transition (Barnabé out 31 March 2026, Rémont in 1 April 2026) is a 6-12 month strategy-clarity gap with the formal AGM director vote not until July 2026.

## Key Non-consensus Insights

**1. Soitec is two businesses traded as one — and the market is anchoring to the wrong one.** Sell-side coverage of Soitec is dominated by the RF-SOI / Mobile Communications narrative (52% of FY26 revenue, -41% YoY constant currency, Q4 -52% YoY) and treats Photonics-SOI as upside optionality. The actual structural setup is inverted: Photonics-SOI is the only commercially qualified volume substrate supplier at TSMC COUPE, GlobalFoundries Fotonix, and Tower PH18 — the three CPO/silicon-photonics foundries that will ship all hyperscale optical-interconnect volume through 2028. Tower's PH18, GFS's Fotonix, IMEC's iSiPP, and Intel's SiPh platform are all SOI-based with SiN waveguide layers added on top, and the SOI starting material has to be individually qualified at each foundry for specific layer thickness (220nm or 340nm), uniformity (<1nm across the wafer), and defect density (<0.1/cm²). Shin-Etsu holds a Smart Cut license but sells no meaningful volume in photonics-grade SOI; the qualification gap is the moat, not the patent. Management guides 20-30% annual growth on a $100M+ FY26 base — entirely consistent with TSMC COUPE volume ramp 2026-2028, AVGO Bailly's 50K+ shipped switches, and NVIDIA Quantum-X engines (all TSMC COUPE-based). The market valuing this near-monopoly position at zero implies Photonics-SOI will not scale — the opposite of every observable foundry datapoint.

**2. POI is a structurally durable second leg the market is conflating with the RF-SOI cycle.** POI (Piezoelectric-on-Insulator) is to SAW filters what RF-SOI was to PA front-end switches a decade ago: an engineered substrate that fundamentally enables a new generation of high-frequency components. Skyworks signed a multi-year agreement on March 4, 2026 to use Soitec POI wafers for its Sky5 5G platform; Qualcomm has a separate POI HVM agreement; 13 customers are in production and 12 more in testing. Content per phone is structurally rising 4mm² → 11mm² as 5G spectrum crowding intensifies and phones need more sophisticated filtering. Because POI revenue is bundled inside Mobile Communications, it gets pulled down with the RF-SOI inventory correction in sell-side modelling — but the underlying multi-year SAW-filter design-win cycle is independent of smartphone unit volumes and even directionally opposite (more bands per phone, not more phones). The TC-SAW/ML-SAW market is split across Skyworks / Qorvo / Murata / Qualcomm / Broadcom, all of whom either are POI customers or have qualified Soitec material — Soitec captures the substrate layer below the filter-vendor competition.

**3. The Smart Cut royalty stream is a hidden annuity on ~80% of global SOI volume that sell-side does not separately model.** Soitec licenses Smart Cut to Shin-Etsu (1997, renewed 2012) and Simgui / NSIG (2014, 200mm-only) — neither license includes new technology transfer or post-license process improvements; both are version-locked at the license date. Licensees produce competing wafers but pay Soitec a royalty per wafer, and ~80% of all SOI wafers shipped globally accrue some royalty stream. The Simgui arrangement was extended for ten more years on March 13, 2026 with **no new tech transfer and a joint commitment to strengthen Soitec's patents in China** — strategically defusing the Chinese substitution risk that bears habitually use to discount the equity. Soitec filed 42 new patents in H1 FY26 alone on a stock of 4,300+ active patents; the IP barrier is being reinforced as a deliberate strategy. The royalty stream is invisible in segment disclosure but represents a durable annuity on the architecture, not the wafer.

**4. The market is mispricing the CEO transition as continuity risk when Rémont's RF/sensor/auto/AI background signals the opposite.** Pierre Barnabé (ex-Atos, ex-Bull, ex-Crédit Lyonnais) was parachuted in 2022 as a financial/operational fixer to manage the RF-SOI cycle. Laurent Rémont — appointed January 8, 2026, advisor from March 16, CEO effective April 1, board-director vote at the July 2026 AGM — is Infineon SVP, with prior roles at Kontron and STMicroelectronics, all in RF / sensors / automotive / AI. His background maps **precisely** onto Soitec's diversification end-markets (RF-SOI / POI for mobile, FD-SOI for auto-AI, SmartSiC for ST power, Photonics-SOI for AI datacom). The advisor-onboarding before formal handoff implies strategy continuity and likely an explicit board mandate to accelerate the diversification away from RF-SOI single-cycle exposure — exactly the analyst bear thesis. Market is treating the transition as ambiguity discount; the better read is that the board picked a technical / end-market specialist rather than another financial fixer because the cycle work is done and the strategic ramp execution is the next phase.

**5. The scrapped $2B revenue / 40% EBITDA medium-term targets are a tactical narrative reset, not a strategic capitulation.** Bears read the FY26 scrapping of guidance — both formal FY26 outlook and the medium-term $2B / 40% EBITDA target — as management losing confidence. The opposite read: management chose to remove a public anchor that the RF-SOI cycle had already disproved (RF-SOI alone could never have hit $2B), and quarterly-only guidance gives the new CEO room to reset on the back of Photonics-SOI ramp without the optical baggage of the prior frame. The pattern is closely analogous to Lisa Su scrapping Read Rory's PC-volume targets at AMD in 2014-2015 and resetting on data center; the credibility came from the segment-mix shift, not the absolute numbers. If Rémont uses the July 2026 AGM and the Q1'27 print (likely late July / early August) to anchor a new revenue mix framework foregrounding Photonics-SOI and POI, the narrative reset becomes a re-rating catalyst rather than a discount.

## Business Model & Product Description

Soitec is the dominant global manufacturer of **engineered substrates** — semiconductor wafers that are not standard bulk silicon, indium-phosphide, or silicon-carbide but custom-built structures (typically a thin top device layer bonded to a different bulk handle wafer with an intervening insulating layer) that enable specific device performance characteristics no monolithic substrate can deliver. The core enabling technology is **Smart Cut™**, a proprietary process invented at France's CEA-Leti in the 1990s and exclusively licensed to Soitec: hydrogen ion implantation into a donor wafer at a precise depth, bonding the donor to a handle wafer, and thermally splitting off a thin (typically 60-340 nm) device layer that remains attached to the handle wafer with the implanted oxide between them. The result is a wafer with the surface electrical properties of the donor and the mechanical / thermal / cost properties of the handle. Soitec holds 4,300+ active patents on Smart Cut and its derivative process variants, filed 42 new patents in H1 FY26 alone, and licenses the technology to Shin-Etsu (1997, renewed 2012) and Simgui / NSIG (2014, extended 10 years March 2026) — together ~80% of global SOI wafer shipments use Smart Cut and pay Soitec a royalty.

**Analogy**: Soitec is to engineered substrates what ASML is to EUV lithography — a single-vendor process-architecture monopoly with a long IP / qualification / customer-relationship moat. The difference is that ASML sells equipment at €200M+ per system to ten foundries; Soitec sells wafers at $50-1000+ per wafer to dozens of customers depending on product, with annual unit volumes in the millions and revenue concentration much lower than ASML's customer mix.

## Industry Context

**Where Soitec sits in the semiconductor value chain**: Engineered substrates occupy a niche between the standard wafer suppliers (Shin-Etsu, SUMCO, GlobalWafers, Siltronic, SK Siltron — combined >90% of bulk 300mm silicon) and the foundries (TSMC, Samsung, GlobalFoundries, Intel, Tower, UMC, SMIC). For specific applications — RF where insulator-isolation reduces loss; FD-SOI where the buried oxide enables sub-1V operation; silicon photonics where the buried oxide is the optical cladding; SiC where engineered substrates dodge the bulk-SiC defect-density wall — engineered substrates are mandatory and the standard wafer cannot substitute. Soitec is the only company with a broad portfolio across all engineered substrate types (RF-SOI, FD-SOI, Photonics-SOI, POI, Imager-SOI, SmartSiC). Competition is product-specific rather than company-specific.

### Pricing power dynamics

Pricing power is **asymmetric across the product portfolio**:
- **Highest in Photonics-SOI**: monopoly + custom foundry qualification + small unit-volume / high-ASP profile. Photonics-SOI wafers reportedly run $500-1000+ per wafer vs. standard 300mm SOI ~$200-300. Margin profile is closest to ASML-like in the semicap ecosystem.
- **High in POI**: sole qualified vendor at scale; multi-year design-in cycles create switching cost; Skyworks and Qualcomm multi-year agreements lock pricing visibility.
- **Moderate in RF-SOI**: dominant share but Shin-Etsu and Simgui licensees compete; smartphone OEM oligopoly (Apple, Samsung, Huawei, Xiaomi, OPPO) has annual ASP negotiation leverage; Chinese internal substitution at SMIC compresses long-term pricing.
- **Moderate in FD-SOI**: dependent on GlobalFoundries 22FDX volume cycle; GFS pricing leverage caps Soitec margin.
- **Pre-volume in SmartSiC**: pricing TBD at ST qualification; SiC bulk substrate ASPs are under pressure from Wolfspeed bankruptcy-era inventory dumping.
- **Royalty annuity stream**: durable, low-touch, ~80% of global SOI volume; structural margin >90%.

### Structural forces reshaping the engineered-substrate market

**1. Silicon photonics commercialisation at hyperscale (2026-2030)**. TSMC COUPE in risk production with AMD (Feb 2026); AVGO Bailly with 50K+ shipped switches; NVIDIA Quantum-X / Spectrum-X with TSMC COUPE engines; Marvell-Celestial AI Photonic Fabric. Each photonic die requires Photonics-SOI starting material; foundries cannot substitute with bulk silicon (no buried oxide → no optical confinement). Cross-reference: [[Sectors/Photonic Metrology]] §Macro shifts — 1.6T module volumes 2.5M → 20M units 2025→2026 (Nomura); SiPh share of high-end transceivers 50-70% 2026 (TrendForce).

**2. 5G mid-band density driving POI substrate growth**. POI content per smartphone rising 4mm² → 11mm² as 5G spectrum allocation expands and filter selectivity requirements tighten. POI adoption ramps even if smartphone unit volumes are flat — the architecture is volume-multiplicative, not unit-cycle-correlated. Skyworks Mar 2026 Sky5 deal is the bellwether; Qualcomm in HVM; Murata, Qorvo, Broadcom all qualified or qualifying.

**3. SiC 150mm → 200mm cost-leverage transition**. SiC bulk substrate cost has fallen from $1500 (2020) to $700 (2025) for 150mm; the 200mm transition compresses unit cost another 50%. SmartSiC's donor-reuse architecture should be cost-advantaged vs. bulk SiC at 200mm but requires production-scale validation at STMicro. Cross-reference: [[Sectors/MLCC & Power Semiconductors.md]] for SiC value-chain context.

**4. Chinese substitution pressure on commodity SOI**. SMIC has internal RF-SOI capability; Chinese foundries are under government mandate to source domestically. The Simgui ten-year extension (March 2026) is the defensive structure — Soitec licenses the manufacturing rather than ceding the IP. Effectiveness vs. Beijing's broader substitution drive is the open question (See [[Theses/SOI - Soitec.md#Outstanding Questions]] #6).

**5. Edge AI driving FD-SOI revival**. GlobalFoundries 22FDX+ launched late 2023 with embedded AI accelerators for IoT edge; Soitec FD-SOI wafer capacity expanded 40% early 2024 specifically for industrial / automotive Edge AI ramp. 22FDX has 50+ designs across automotive, 5G connectivity, IoT. The FD-SOI revival is real but small (~€110-130M FY26 vs. €592M total); thesis upside requires FD-SOI to step-function up if hyperscaler captive accelerators move some volume to FDX-class processes.

## Bull Case

The bull case is a Photonics-SOI-led re-rating coupled with a cyclical RF-SOI / Auto recovery.

**Mechanism**: Photonics-SOI ramps to $200-300M revenue by FY28 on hyperscale CPO commercialisation (TSMC COUPE volume + AVGO Bailly expansion + NVIDIA Quantum-X engines), pulling group revenue from €592M FY26 → €750-900M FY28. POI scales with Skyworks Sky5 production ramp and Qualcomm HVM growth — Mobile Communications recovers to €400-450M by FY28 (mid-cycle, not peak). FD-SOI + Photonics-SOI Edge & Cloud AI segment compounds at 20-25% to €350-400M by FY28. SmartSiC begins meaningful ST volume contribution in H2 FY27 / FY28, adding €40-60M to Auto & Industrial. Smart Cut royalty stream grows ~10% per year as global SOI volume expands with photonics ramp. Total group revenue €900M-€1.1B FY28 at 30-32% EBITDA margin = €270-350M EBITDA. At a 25-30x EV/EBITDA multiple (consistent with a structurally-growing pure-play picks-and-shovels at a recovering cycle), equity value €7-10B = €155-220/share at current ~46.5M share count.

**Specific drivers**:
