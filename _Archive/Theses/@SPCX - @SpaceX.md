# SPCX - SpaceX

## Summary
The listed residual is Starlink/Connectivity cash ($4.291B Q2 revenue, 60.5% adj. EBITDA, 12.0M subs, ARPU $66 flat) plus Falcon shipping (38 launches, 28 internal). The market High sleeve (Live Portfolio 10–25% at $138–146) prices an AI-infra compounder: 1.4 GW **nameplate**, YE26 ~2 GW as the only company YE26 compute target, Rubin-exclusive 2026–27 campus buy. 10 GW / MSFT-as-largest-offtaker / $100B/GW / Terafab IDM are not load-bearing. AI is a convex unproven option — $15.828B of $18.369B Q2 capex, $2.561B AI revenue against a $1.257B operating loss, Note 2 contracted sales $14.1B defined as the non-cancellable window under 90-day post-ramp exits. Demand is printed; capital recovery is not. Conviction is **medium**: Connectivity/Falcon keep it off low; AI ROIC and CSA duration keep it off high.

## Key Non-consensus Insights
- **Honest residual is Connectivity cash, not the AI campus**
  - **Consensus:** Q2 is the first print of an AI-infrastructure compounder (PhotonCap reframe; High sleeve crowded on that reframe). $7.814B (+92%) and $3.538B adj. EBITDA are read as AI operating leverage.
  - **Variant:** Adj. EBITDA is Connectivity ($2.597B, 60.5% margin) plus AI D&A/SBC addbacks ($1.146B AI adj. EBITDA against a $1.257B operating loss). Space is a $962M, 38-launch shipping engine that still loses $(542)M at the operating line. The cash residual that survives a failed AI ROIC is Starlink + Falcon, not 10 GW.
  - **First confirming observable [2026-11]:** Q3 Connectivity adj. EBITDA margin holds near 60% with ARPU not collapsing after the June hike, while AI operating loss stays large versus $15.8B-run-rate capex.
  - **Falsifier:** Connectivity adj. EBITDA breaks — same condition as → LOW.

- **Demand proven ≠ capital recovery — two value-chain pools**
  - **Consensus:** $14.1B Note 2 contracted Cloud Services sales (plus +$6.7B early Q3) plus SemiAnalysis ~$30–50M/MW/yr and ~$100B/GW ARR make the AI build a covered offtake story.
  - **Variant:** Note 2 counts only the non-cancellable, enforceable period. CSAs are 90-day terminate-after-ramp. Connectivity is SPCX-owned. AI gross ($30–50M/MW/yr, SemiAnalysis) is cancellable against non-cancellable NVDA / turbine / HBM / optics POs. Shanaka: $15.828B AI capex in 90 days vs $14.1B contracted sales = 89% of one quarter's AI build. Rubin exclusive = this year's campus BOM; Terafab is a later custom line, not 2026 hardware.
  - **First confirming observable [2026-11]:** matched-cohort payback or long-duration non-cancellable CSA prints (duration > D&A life).
  - **Falsifier:** first CSA termination cascade, or D&A lives < 90-day exits with no Connectivity residual — → LOW / → CLOSE.

- **Starlink internalized Jevons because SpaceX owned Falcon; AI colo inverts COTS**
  - **Consensus:** the firm that collapsed launch cost and ate Starlink demand will repeat the move in AI compute (Historian transfer; 10 GW / MSFT offtake as the next Jevons).
  - **Variant:** Starlink Jevons worked because SpaceX owned the scarce complementary asset (Falcon). AI colo for MSFT (or Anthropic/Google) rents NVDA/HBM/optics/turbines — COTS inverted. 90-day CSA vs Azure is a substitute only if a GW-scale MSFT CSA takes the same 2026–27 MW; else it is a small bridge. Starlink's 2026 lead does not need Starship (Falcon internal 28/38; IFT-13 deployed 20 production V3 sats on a test profile, V3 is not the constellation workhorse). Off-grid Terafab language is Grimes County tax-abatement text, not proven electrical islanding.
  - **First confirming observable [2027-03]:** a disclosed GW-scale MSFT (or equivalent) CSA covering the same 2026–27 MW as Azure-substitute talk, with duration longer than 90 days post-ramp.
  - **Falsifier:** YE26 nameplate <2 GW on the same definition as the 1.4 GW print — → LOW.

- **High sleeve is ahead of the file — 10 GW / $100B/GW / Terafab / MSFT-largest are not load-bearing**
  - **Consensus:** Live Portfolio High (10–25%) at $138–146 prices SpaceX as the next hyperscaler-class compute supplier (SemiAnalysis 10 GW 2027, Microsoft largest offtaker, ~$500B ARR framing).
  - **Variant:** Company YE26 compute target is ~2 GW; printed nameplate is 1.4 GW. Tech: Rubin exclusive is the 2026–27 campus buy. Sentiment: rural Starlink PMF is real; 2026 price anger exists without evidenced churn; tenure 2–2.5y is unverified Reddit/career-site color. Cursor at $60B announced (close targeted Q3) spends a material slice of the $100B cash pile on an application-layer asset, not campus MW.
  - **First confirming observable [2026-12]:** YE26 nameplate ≥2 GW, same definition as 1.4 GW, with Connectivity margins intact.
  - **Falsifier:** capital recovery clock fails (D&A lives < 90-day exits) with no Connectivity residual — → CLOSE.

**Connectivity (owned pool).** Vertical integration: design/build/launch/operate the constellation on Falcon. 2026 Starlink lead does not need Starship. ARPU mix shift (international + lower-priced plans) already printed; June hike is the first US consumer price extract in years.

**AI (renter pool).** Colossus II campus + Cloud Service Agreements. Rubin exclusive = 2026–27 campus silicon buy, not a custom IDM. Note 2: contracted sales = non-cancellable enforceable period only; excludes cancellable future periods. CFO: +$6.7B additional cloud services contracted over a six-month period ramping from October. TechCrunch-named counterparties: Anthropic, Google, Reflection AI — not a printed MSFT GW CSA.

**Space (shipping, not the residual).** Internal mass-to-orbit is the Starlink factory. Customer launch is the third-party revenue line. Starship is R&D ($1,076M Space R&D in Q2) aimed at 99% cost-to-orbit cut — not in 2026 constellation math.

## Bull Case
Connectivity remains a 60%+ adj. EBITDA cash engine; June hike lifts Q3 ARPU without a churn break; Starshield/enterprise mix (already 42.1%) compounds. Campus hits YE26 ≥2 GW on the 1.4 GW definition; CSAs convert from 90-day post-ramp into matched-cohort payback or long-duration non-cancellable paper; Rubin fleet fills 2026–27. If — and only if — a GW-scale MSFT CSA takes 2026–27 MW, Azure-substitute talk becomes real and the High-sleeve 10 GW path starts to have a denominator. Cash $100B + $47.5B backlog funds the build without a dilutive primary. Cursor closes and AI solutions mix (ex-infra) shows an application-layer attach on top of colo. Falcon keeps shipping Starlink; Starship V3 becomes constellation-relevant after 2026. Valuation path: the market stops paying 30–50× run-rate for unproven AI ROIC and starts paying a Connectivity compounder plus a call option on campus MW — or, in the full Jevons transfer the High sleeve already prices, 10 GW at SemiAnalysis ~$100B/GW.

What has to go right: Connectivity margins, CSA duration, YE26 nameplate definition, no termination cascade.

## Risks
**Thesis risks (the case is wrong)**
- Capital recovery: 90-day post-ramp CSA exits < GPU/turbine D&A lives; first termination cascade. AI $15.828B quarterly capex is not covered by Note 2's non-cancellable definition.
- Connectivity break: June hike produces churn or ARPU fails to lift; adj. EBITDA margin leaves the ~60% band; Kuiper/terrestrial share take.
- YE26 nameplate <2 GW on the 1.4 GW definition — the only company YE26 compute target misses.
- Jevons non-transfer: AI colo remains a NVDA layer-renter; Starlink exception does not repeat.
- Cursor $60B is application-layer spend that does not move campus payback.

**Position risks (case can be right and the stock still falls)**
- Live Portfolio High (10–25%) is ahead of a medium file — multiple compression without a fundamental miss.
- Lock-up supply (~912M first tranche; more through December).
- Key-person / related-party (Musk; $327M related-party interest in Q2) and unverified tenure 2–2.5y.
- Headline 10 GW / Terafab / MSFT-largest narrative unwind even if Connectivity prints.
