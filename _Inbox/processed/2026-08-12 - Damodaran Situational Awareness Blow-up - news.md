# The Situational Awareness Blow-up: The Collateral Damage from Investing Conviction!

*Aswath Damodaran, Musings on Markets — August 2026. Brief score `2` (extreme positioning macro). Holdings: flow overhang for NVDA/AVGO/MRVL; short-cover side includes NOW.*

## What happened

Leopold Aschenbrenner’s Situational Awareness fund — built entirely around a near-term AI payoff (long AI chips/infrastructure, short software AI would disrupt, plus an illiquid Anthropic stake) — rose roughly **367% from Aug 7 2025 to Jun 19 2026** (~**450%** through late June on another window), then lost more than two-thirds of its **public** equity value in four weeks and was forced to liquidate. **Citadel bought almost all of the public book.** From Jun 19–Jul 29: overall principal loss **>43%** with Anthropic private holding mostly intact; **public portfolio down ~67%**.

## Fund construction that mattered

- Blueprint: Aschenbrenner’s 167-page “Situational Awareness: The Decade Ahead” paper after leaving OpenAI.
- Early capital from tech luminaries; Jane Street among later names; 2-and-20 fee structure.
- Returns of that magnitude required substantial leverage (explicit borrowing and/or options).
- Private Anthropic stake against redeemable public capital created a liquidity mismatch — the transmission channel for forced selling.

## Damodaran’s analytical frame (conviction)

Conviction is a continuum that drives **position sizing** and **leverage**. Absolute conviction → concentrated, levered bets; mush → cash/index. Conviction requires belief in (1) fair-price assessment, (2) market correction, and (3) correction inside the holding horizon.

Life-cycle point: young/tech stories have noisier fair-value estimates and fewer catalysts — they tolerate less concentration and less debt than mature cash-flow businesses. Combining a risky macro AI story with maximal leverage is inconsistent with that — truncation risk forces liquidation before the thesis can be vindicated (LTCM parallel).

## Portfolio read

This is a **positioning event, not a fundamentals event**. Forced unwind mechanically pressured AI-infrastructure longs (NVDA, AVGO, MRVL and adjacent) while short-covering lifted disrupted-software shorts (NOW among them). Recent drawdowns in AI-infra names may carry less demand information than price action implies, until Citadel’s absorbed inventory clears and the next hyperscaler order/guide cycle confirms or refutes the fundamental bear case.

## Damodaran’s three lessons

1. Investment actions inconsistent with conviction risk ruin (AI macro + max leverage = time bomb).
2. Momentum is a wild card — rise and fall can be explained as levered momentum as much as AI narrative.
3. Humble money beats smart money — overconfidence from short track records and pedigree drives over-reach.
