# NVIDIA’s Kyber Racks To Sport 340.4TB Of DRAM, With HBM4E Priced At $19.76/GB And Each Rack Reportedly Priced At $41.6 Million

*Source: Wccftech / Rohail Saleem — Aug 11, 2026. Brief score `8`. Portfolio: NVDA (High), 000660 (Full).*

NVIDIA's upcoming Vera Rubin Ultra systems are in a state of flux, with persistent rumors pointing to a potential HBM de-spec where the GPU giant might lower HBM4E per GPU to preserve margins. Bank of America quantified the dynamics between HBM4E and LPDDR5X-based SOCAMM2 modules within NVIDIA's upcoming Vera Rubin Ultra-based Kyber racks.

## Key facts

- Each base Rubin GPU currently equipped with 288 GB of HBM4 across 8 stacks, up to 22 TB/s per GPU.
- Vera CPU abandons standard server RDIMMs for LPDDR5X-based SOCAMM2 modules (compression connector, flat against motherboard). Each Vera CPU: 8 SOCAMM2 slots → theoretical 1.5 TB LPDDR5X per CPU (192GB modules).
- **BofA: HBM4E within Rubin costs $19.76 per GB.**
- Each **144-GPU Kyber rack**: ~216TB LPDDR5X costing around **$2.8 million**, above the **$2.5 million** bill for **124.4TB of HBM4E**.
- **Total Kyber rack cost: $41.6 million.**
- Related UBS: each Rubin GPU in Vera Rubin superchip ~$9,247 (incl. HBM4, packaging, interposer, peripherals); each Vera CPU ~$20,059 incl. SOCAMM2 LPDDR5X (+$350 board). ~Half of GPU cost and nearly all of CPU cost (CPU only ~$704 after stripping memory) is DRAM.
- Blackwell servers: Grace CPUs 480 GB memory; Blackwell GPUs 192 GB HBM3E (GB200) / up to 288 GB HBM3E (GB300). Vera Rubin racks total memory pool ~74.7 TB (HBM4 + SOCAMM2). **Kyber racks balloon to 340.4TB.**
- BofA framing: even as HBM capacity/pricing rise, HBM stays a relatively small piece of Nvidia rack economics (Blackwell Ultra HBM ~7.5% of rack; HBM stays around just 5–6% of Kyber rack value) — memory cost pressure shifts toward LPDDR.
