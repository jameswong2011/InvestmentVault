# SpaceX $100B Second Starbase Spaceport in Louisiana

**Source:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/25/spacex-will-build-a-second-100b-starbase-spaceport-in-louisiana/
**Date:** 2026-08-25 · **Brief score:** 9 · **Tags:** daily-intel-triage

## Why it matters (portfolio)
Hard cadence/capex commitment for held **SPCX**: $100B site, 2027 construct / 2029–30 ops, Starship reuse still unproven — underwrite vs Falcon retirement comments.

## Article body

SpaceX will build a second, $100B 'Starbase' spaceport in Louisiana (TechCrunch, Aug 25, 2026).

SpaceX announced a second Starship spaceport at a former Exxon property in Vermilion Parish, Louisiana (Gulf Coast, west of New Orleans; ~10 hours from Starbase South Texas), ending what the company describes as a seven-year site search. Investment: $100 billion. SpaceX: construction starting 2027, first Starship launch 2029. Louisiana Economic Development: construction by end-2027, initial operations expected 2030.

Jobs: 3,000 direct over 10 years (avg ~$92,600); >30,000 construction jobs at peak. Site intended as "self-sustaining spaceport" with propellant production, power generation, deep-water shipping, vehicle processing, and an airport.

Context: Starship still proving LEO + reuse — 13 test flights since 2023; Flight 14 could send upper stage to orbit; company has not yet returned booster and upper stage to pad on a single flight. Musk said Falcon will retire once Starship flies multiple times per week. >$8B spent developing Starship (SEC filings). Starship is crucial to Starlink (only profitable segment) and planned SpaceXAI orbital compute satellite network.

Safety/env: Texas Starbase historically high injury rates; LED says SpaceX engaged Louisiana wildlife/coastal agencies; partnering on Coastal Master Plan / shoreline breakwaters.
