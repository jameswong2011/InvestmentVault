# TSMC Board Approves US$29.4B Capex, NT$7 Dividend, Sony Image-Sensor JV

## Thesis Delta
Consensus debates a sell-the-beat / capex-peak setup into hyperscaler guides; TSMC’s Aug 11 board 6-K appropriates ~US$29.44B for advanced tech, advanced packaging/specialty, and fab construction — a forward order-book signal for [[Theses/AMAT - Applied Materials]], [[Theses/LRCX - Lam Research]], [[Theses/KLA - KLA Corporation]], [[Theses/ASMI - ASM International]], [[Theses/BESI - BE Semiconductor Industries]] and confirms the up-capex side of [[Theses/TSM - Taiwan Semiconductor]] rather than a plateau.

## Summary
TSMC Form 6-K (board meeting Aug 11, 2026): Q2 consolidated revenue NT$1,270.38B, net income NT$706.56B, diluted EPS NT$27.25. Board approved NT$7.0/share Q2 cash dividend (ex-div Dec 10, record Dec 16, pay Jan 7 2027) and capital appropriations of approximately US$29,442.50 million for (1) installation/upgrade of advanced technology capacity, (2) advanced packaging, mature and/or specialty technology capacity, and (3) fab construction and facility systems. Also approved a Sony Semiconductor Solutions JV for next-generation image sensors with share subscription up to JPY 282B. Six-month net sales NT$2,404.5B; total assets NT$9,375.7B.
