# Marvell Q2 Revenue Hits Record $2.74B as Data Center Reaches 79% of Sales

Source: https://convergedigest.com/marvell-q2-fy2027-data-center-ai-revenue

Marvell Technology posted record revenue of $2.739 billion for its fiscal second quarter ended August 1, 2026, up 37% year over year and 13% sequentially as AI infrastructure continued to reshape the company’s revenue mix. Data center revenue climbed to $2.172 billion, rising 46% year over year and 18% sequentially, and accounted for 79% of total sales compared with 74% a year earlier. Communications and other revenue reached $567.8 million, up 10% year over year but down 3% from the preceding quarter. CEO Matt Murphy said AI-related bookings remain exceptionally strong, with demand broadening across Marvell’s connectivity portfolio and the company expecting a significant acceleration in its custom silicon business during the second half of fiscal 2027.

Profitability also expanded as revenue scaled. GAAP gross margin reached 53.1%, compared with 50.4% a year earlier, while non-GAAP gross margin was 58.9%. GAAP operating income increased to $459.7 million, producing a 16.8% operating margin, while non-GAAP operating income crossed the $1 billion mark at $1.003 billion and non-GAAP operating margin rose to 36.6%. GAAP net income was $308.0 million, or $0.33 per diluted share, while non-GAAP net income reached $865.9 million, or $0.94 per diluted share. Operating cash flow totaled $605.5 million. Marvell also increased R&D spending to $741.1 million from $519.0 million a year earlier as it expands its portfolio across custom compute, networking, optical interconnect, memory connectivity and other data center silicon.

Marvell expects another sizable sequential increase in the third quarter, forecasting revenue of $3.150 billion, plus or minus 5%, which at the midpoint would represent approximately 15% growth from Q2. The company expects non-GAAP diluted EPS of $1.10, plus or minus $0.05, and non-GAAP gross margin of 57.5% to 58.5%. Murphy said Marvell is again raising its revenue outlook for both fiscal 2027 and fiscal 2028 compared with guidance issued last quarter, although the earnings release did not provide revised full-year revenue figures. Marvell plans to provide a broader view of its long-term AI infrastructure strategy at its Investor Day in New York on October 6, 2026. The accompanying financial materials reinforce the accelerating concentration of the business in data center infrastructure: data center revenue has risen from 73% of sales in Q3 FY2025 to 79% in the latest quarter.

🌐 Analysis

The most important structural metric in Marvell’s results is not simply the 37% overall growth rate but the continued shift of the company toward data center infrastructure. The quarterly trend in Marvell’s supplemental financial materials shows data center revenue moving from 73%–76% of total revenue through much of the preceding two years to 79% in Q2 FY2027. Data center revenue is now growing substantially faster than Marvell’s communications and other businesses, making AI and cloud infrastructure the principal driver of the company’s growth trajectory.

That shift coincides with a much broader data center portfolio. Marvell closed the Celestial AI acquisition on February 2, adding Photonic Fabric technology for optical Scale Up connectivity, and acquired XConn Technologies on February 10 to expand PCIe, CXL and UALink-related switching capabilities. Marvell also entered a strategic partnership with NVIDIA in March under which NVIDIA invested $2 billion and Marvell is developing custom XPUs and compatible Scale Up networking for the NVLink Fusion ecosystem. Together with Marvell’s existing Ethernet switching, SerDes, coherent DSP, optical interconnect and custom silicon businesses, these moves extend its addressable infrastructure from inside an accelerator system through Scale Up and Scale Out networking to Scale Across data center interconnect.

The Q3 outlook indicates that this shift is entering another growth phase. A $3.15 billion revenue midpoint would represent roughly 15% sequential growth following Q2’s 13% sequential increase, while management specifically points to a significant acceleration in custom silicon during the second half.
