# Peeling Apart Google–Marvell Custom Silicon / Warrant Deal

## Why it matters (portfolio)

## Article body

Peeling Apart That Supposed $120 Billion Chip Deal Google Inked With Marvell (The Next Platform, Aug 27, 2026).

Marvell 8-K: expanded commercial agreement with Google for custom semiconductor products attaching to the TPU ecosystem — AI inference accelerators, storage controllers, NICs, memory interface controllers, and near-memory compute. Google warrants: 58.97M Marvell shares (~6.3% fully vested). Vesting: 1.36M over first four quarters; remainder in 240 buckets tied to $500M revenue increments sold to Google. Floor/exercise context around $206.58/share; Google need not exercise; rights can wait until Aug 18, 2033 then expire if unused.

Press framed as Google investing $12.5B into Marvell stock / $120B chippery through 2033 — Next Platform argues the economics are slipperier (round-trip / funny-money rebate via warrants as Marvell market cap rose ~$18.6B on announcement day). Rumored "Merope" LPU (decode-oriented inference) and CXL memory processor are NOT named in the 8-K. Addendum names product code "Kestrel" with qualification target no later than Nov 10, 2027 — possibly the custom inference/memory basket for Google's Kansas City "Kestrel" datacenter cluster (~430 acres, up to five hyperscale DCs, ~$100B / ~2.5M sq ft aggregate). Author's hunch: Kestrel accelerators qualify late 2027, roll into DCs 2028 via TSMC shepherding.

Neither Google nor Marvell commented on Merope rumors. Deal expands Marvell's chip-shepherding franchise around TPU attach rather than a single named ASIC headline.
