# AWS Data Center Withdrawal Signals Growing Political, Community Challenges

*Source: Data Center Knowledge / James Walker — Aug 11, 2026. Brief score `5`. Portfolio: extreme AI-infra siting macro — delivery timing risk for NVDA/AVGO/MRVL/LRCX/AMAT complex; not a demand cut.*

AWS withdrew its application for a proposed **2.5 million sq ft** data center project in Calvert County, Maryland, as opposition grows and the county prepares to consider a six-month moratorium.

## Key facts

- Application filed May 18 for site adjacent to **Calvert Cliffs Nuclear Power Plant** (Constellation): three campuses / 200 acres / eight buildings / **up to 500 MW**.
- Withdrawal confirmed Aug 4. AWS: 'development timelines, operational requirements, and our ability to deliver quickly for customers.'
- June 23 primaries: three incumbent commissioners who opposed a development pause lost (Ireland, Cox, Hance).
- Same day as withdrawal confirmation: county set **Aug 18 hearing** on six-month moratorium on data center site-plan approvals.
- July: QTS abandoned **$30B** Prince William County Digital Gateway.
- Data Center Watch: **75 major projects / >$130B** delayed or canceled in first three months of 2026 partly due to organized local opposition.
- Change.org petition against Calvert project: >10,000 signatures.
- Omdia analyst Alan Howard: operators pivoting to 'cut losses and move on'; nuclear adjacency no longer a political safe bet; 'early innings' of a perception problem.
- Separate AWS setback: 12-month permit moratorium in Worcester County affects proposed Ocean City cable landing station.
- AWS says >$20B invested in Maryland since 2010; remains 'deeply committed' to the state.
