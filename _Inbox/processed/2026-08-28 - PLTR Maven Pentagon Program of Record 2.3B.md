# Palantir Maven Now Official Pentagon Program of Record ($2.3B Request)

**Source:** https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/08/25/palantirs-maven-is-now-an-official-pentagon-progra/
**Date:** 2026-08-25 · **Brief score:** 9 · **Tags:** daily-intel-triage

## Why it matters (portfolio)
Largest book name **PLTR**: PoR stickiness + FY27 $2.3B/5yr budget request vs prior ~$1.3B ceiling — durable funded path for Maven (distinct from commercial RDV test).

## Article body

Maven Smart System, an artificial intelligence-powered military platform, is now an official program of record for the Pentagon. The designation will be a key factor for Palantir as it seeks to get $2.3 billion over the next five years to expand Maven, which identifies and analyzes battlefield data and supports AI-enabled targeting. Image source: The Motley Fool. "It is imperative that we invest now and with focus to deepen the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) across the Joint Force and establish AI-enabled decision-making as the cornerstone of our strategy," Deputy Secretary of Defense Steve ​Feinberg wrote in a memo obtained by Reuters. The budget request is part of the Pentagon's $1.5 trillion 2027 budget proposal, which is still pending in Congress. But even if lawmakers choose not to fully fund the Pentagon's budget, Palantir is in an excellent position to generate revenue and profit from Maven for many years. What is Maven? Maven traces its roots to the Pentagon's 2017 plan, Project Maven, which was conceived as a drone-imagery labeling program. Palantir won a Pentagon contract in 2024 worth up to $480 million; the program contract ceiling increased to $1.3 billion in 2025. Maven analyzes data from satellites, drones, radar, intelligence reports, and sensors, and uses artificial intelligence to identify potential threats and target them. It is the primary AI operating system for the U.S. military. Its status as a program of record will help streamline the use of Maven across all branches of the military -- giving the project stickiness that will help assure Palantir's continued funding. CEO Alex Karp has said Maven gives U.S. troops a huge advantage in combat. "Once the war zones heat up anywhere in the allied world, the Palantir Maven platform takes very valuable LLMs and makes them actually lethal and useful in the battlefield," he said during an interview on CNBC.
