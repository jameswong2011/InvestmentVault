# TSMC CoWoS 5.5x Reticle Hits 99% Yield; Capacity Near Balance; SoIC to 4.5µm by 2029

## Thesis Delta
Consensus still prices CoWoS scarcity as the binding AI constraint; TSMC packaging VP He Jun says 5.5x-reticle CoWoS is in HVM with yields stably >98% (up to 99%) across multiple AI customers and capacity is “very close” to demand after three years of roughly doubling — so scarcity rent migrates to HBM ([[Theses/000660 - SK Hynix]]) and ABF ([[Theses/2802 - Ajinomoto]]), while CoWoS→14x by 2029 and SoIC 6µm→4.5µm (A14-on-A14) keep bonder/tool intensity rising for [[Theses/TSM - Taiwan Semiconductor]], [[Theses/BESI - BE Semiconductor Industries]], [[Theses/AMAT - Applied Materials]].

## Summary
Economic Daily (UDN) coverage of TSMC advanced-packaging VP He Jun (“I’m the CoWoS guy”) at an Aug 11 industry forum: 5.5x-reticle CoWoS is in high-volume production with yields stably above 98% and sometimes 99% across multiple AI customers. TSMC will keep an annual CoWoS technology cadence and targets 14x reticle by 2029. CoWoS capacity has roughly doubled each of the past three years; TSMC now runs 10 advanced-packaging fabs and capacity is “not fully satisfying but very close” to customer demand — the first explicit near-balance signal from the supplier with the best visibility.

SoIC hybrid bonding has been in HVM at 6µm pitch since last year and is slated for 4.5µm by 2029 with A14-on-A14 stacking, offering >50x interconnect density and 5x energy efficiency versus prior interconnect approaches. He Jun framed chiplet pairing as global “matchmaking” across TSMC’s manufacturing network to improve package-level performance consistency. Companion LTN reporting adds the same yield/roadmap facts and notes yesterday’s board approval of the Sony image-sensor JV and NT$7 Q2 dividend.
