# SK hynix to expand production capacity in China as it mulls Solidigm IPO — second phase could boost local production by 50%

*Source: Tom's Hardware / Anton Shilov — Aug 11, 2026 (citing Sedaily, Korea Herald). Brief score `7`. Portfolio: 000660 (Full), SNDK (High), 285A (Low); WFE read-through AMAT/LRCX.*

SK hynix has resumed investments in its fab in Dalian, China, operated by Solidigm, and plans to boost output by 50% already in 2027. Coincidentally, it is mulling listing some of Solidigm's shares on NASDAQ to raise capital while retaining control.

## Key facts

- After acquiring Intel's 3D NAND/SSD business (2021) and forming Solidigm, expansions in China were suspended partly due to the 2022–23 memory downturn and U.S. export controls.
- Demand for solid-state storage — especially Solidigm high-capacity SSDs — is exceptionally strong; capacity plans for Dalian are being reconsidered ahead of a possible Solidigm IPO.
- Following a four-year pause, Solidigm resumed Dalian investment this year. **Phase-two construction is complete**; **tool install could start as early as November**; **mass production of floating-gate 3D NAND targeted 1H27**.
- Phase two designed for ~**50,000 WSPM**. Combined with ~**100,000 WSPM** phase one → ~**150,000 WSPM** (+50%).
- U.S. annual licenses through 2026 allow SK hynix and Samsung to ship U.S.-origin tools to China fabs (replacing open-ended VEU), enabling upgrades including >200-layer floating-gate QLC.
- Solidigm intends to start making floating-gate 3D QLC with >200 active layers at Dalian in 2H26; additional capacity supports **245TB-class SSDs** into 2027.
- Solidigm ~quarter of data-center SSD market. Unofficial reports: NASDAQ listing could raise **up to $7B** while SK hynix retains control — capital roughly enough to expand Solidigm premium DC SSD output.
- SK hynix Aug 5 filing only said Solidigm was 'considering various ways' to improve competitiveness — **IPO not formally confirmed**.
