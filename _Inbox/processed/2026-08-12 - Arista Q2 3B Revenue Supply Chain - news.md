# Arista smashes revenue records with $3bn+; CEO bullish on supply chain overhaul

*Source: Data Center Dynamics / Ben Wodecki (SDxCentral) — Aug 11, 2026. Brief score `6`. Portfolio: AVGO/MRVL ecosystem (datacenter Ethernet); memory allocation signal for 000660/SNDK.*

Arista Networks Q2 2026 revenues surpassed $3 billion for the first time.

## Key facts

- **Q2 revenue $3.036B**: +12.1% QoQ, **+37.7% YoY** (vs $2.32B revenue for all of 2020).
- Ethernet switch line surpassed **100 cumulative customers** (from single digits in 2024).
- Multi-year purchase commitments nearly tripled to **$9.7B** from **$3.6B** a year ago.
- Hired former Nefeli Networks CEO Eugenia Corrales to spearhead global operations.
- COO Todd Nightingale: secured multi-year agreements with strategic component vendors; qualified new suppliers; **memory supply secured for 2026** with visibility into 2027 across DDR4, DDR5, and NAND.
- Raised Q3 revenue guide to ~**$3.3B**; full-year revenue guide to **$12.6B** (~40% growth).
- 1.6Tb switches debuted June; ship early 2027.
- Growth bet: **scale-across / distributed compute networking** amid power/space/compute scarcity; stronger in scale-out/across vs NVLink; eyes scale-up for **non-Nvidia accelerators** (Google TPUs, custom inference) including custom racks.
