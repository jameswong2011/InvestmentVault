# Microsoft plans to announce Maia 300 as early as September 2026; in talks with TSMC for 300,000+ units by 2027

Source: [GIGAZINE](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260813-microsoft-maia-300-ai-chip-fall/) — Aug 13, 2026. Relays The Information / Reuters.

According to The Information, Microsoft plans to announce its next-generation AI chip, Maia 300, as early as September 2026, and is in negotiations with Taiwan's TSMC to manufacture more than 300,000 chips by 2027.

NVIDIA holds an overwhelming market share as a manufacturer of AI chips, but major technology companies are trying to reduce their dependence on NVIDIA by developing their own AI chips. Google has developed its own processor called TPU which is specifically designed for AI processing, and Amazon is also manufacturing its own AI chip called Trainium.

Microsoft also announced its own AI chip, the Maia 100, in 2023, and the second generation, the Maia 200, appeared in 2026. However, it is lagging behind Google's parent company, Alphabet, which began recording TPU sales revenue in its second-quarter 2026 earnings report, and Amazon, which has started negotiations to sell its Trainium chip to other data centers.

Only tens of thousands of the previous generation Maia 200 were manufactured, and as of late July 2026, they were only operational in two data centers in the United States. However, Microsoft aims to eventually produce more than one million Maia 300s and reportedly plans to acquire major cloud customers, including AI development company Anthropic. The Information points out that constraints such as component shortages and ongoing negotiations with TSMC will be obstacles to achieving this goal.

Andrew Wall, General Manager of Azure Maia at Microsoft, commented: "Microsoft continues to invest in custom silicon as part of its long-term AI infrastructure strategy. We do not disclose production volumes, but the reported figures do not reflect the scale of our program."

## Additional context from same-story English coverage (TNW / Quartz)

Microsoft's custom chip programme started years after Amazon and Google. The 300,000-unit order for Maia 300 is Microsoft's attempt to close that gap. TSMC's advanced packaging capacity is tight through 2027. Microsoft recently told employees to stop "tokenmaxxing" and set division-level AI budgets, a sign that compute costs are becoming a constraint even at the company spending $35.8 billion per quarter on infrastructure.

Maia 200 relies on TSMC's 3-nanometer process and incorporates substantial SRAM. Microsoft was in early talks to supply Maia 200 chips to Anthropic, though no deal had been finalized. Anthropic agreed to use Amazon Trainium in a 10-year arrangement and announced plans to use Google TPUs.

TSMC did not respond to requests for comment.
