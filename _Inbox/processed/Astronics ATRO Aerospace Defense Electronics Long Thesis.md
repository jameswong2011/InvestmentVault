# Astronics ATRO Aerospace Defense Electronics Long Thesis

## Astronics Corporation (NASDAQ: ATRO) — Aerospace & Defense Electronics

The structural long thesis for Astronics rests on a classic mid-cap inflection: a high-moat aerospace component supplier transitioning from supply-chain-constrained recovery into a period of aggressive, high-margin operational leverage. As global commercial airlines aggressively upgrade cabins to meet passenger demand for high-wattage in-seat power and fast satellite connectivity, Astronics sits as the dominant player across critical interior architectures, with an estimated 80 percent plus share of commercial in-seat power. Rather than being a purely speculative play on commercial OEM aircraft delivery schedules, ATRO functions as an operating leverage engine capturing high-margin revenue across both commercial fleet retrofit cycles and expanding defense program budgets.

Operationally, the business is demonstrating unprecedented demand velocity. Backed by a record backlog surpassing $730 million and book-to-bill reached 1.26 in the most recent quarter with the company raising its annual revenue guidance toward the $1 billion mark. Crucially, top-line scale is unlocking significant margin expansion: as factory throughput normalizes and supply-chain friction eases, Aerospace segment operating margins are expanding toward 17% to 18%, pushing consolidated adjusted EBITDA margins past 16%. With near-monopolistic market share in passenger in-seat power systems (via its flagship EmPower architecture), alongside deep roots in exterior military lighting and automated defense test systems, Astronics possesses genuine pricing power and multi-year revenue visibility.

From a valuation perspective, Astronics is not a cheap stock and we are not going to pretend it is. At roughly 18x forward EV to EBITDA the stock trades in line with the aerospace supplier group. The case is narrower and more durable than a re-rating story: revenue guidance implies 14% growth, adjusted operating margin is expanding, and the September 2025 refinancing cut interest expense by a quarter. Investors are buying a market-leading platform with a record $734 million backlog and secular tailwinds across airline refreshes, defense electronics, and next-generation mobility, at a fair price rather than a cheap one.

## What Services Do They Offer?

Founded in 1968 and headquartered in East Aurora, New York, Astronics is a major supplier of power, lighting, connectivity, and test systems for aircraft and defense platforms.

- Cabin Power & Connectivity: They produce the widely used EmPower in-seat power supply systems found on major commercial airlines globally, as well as in-flight Wi-Fi hardware and cabin management systems.

- Military & Avionics: They supply tactical displays, exterior/interior lighting, and ruggedized power control systems for military aircraft, warships, and defense hardware.

- Edge AI & Rugged Computing: Through subsidiaries like Ballard Technology, they produce MOSA-aligned (Modular Open Systems Approach) edge AI accelerators and avionics interfaces for rugged military platforms.

- Key Customers: Major aerospace primes including Boeing, Airbus, Panasonic Avionics, and the U.S. Department of Defense.

Astronics Corporation organizes its operations into two main divisions, dominated by its Aerospace segment, which delivers a wide spectrum of specialized onboard technologies. At the core of its commercial product portfolio is its market-leading EmPower line of in-seat power systems. Astronics provides intelligent cabin power management architectures, universal 110V AC outlets, high-output USB-A and USB-C charging ports, and modern wireless charging modules. These systems manage electrical loads dynamically across commercial airliners, business jets, and rotorcraft, ensuring passengers and flight crews can power personal devices and flight electronics safely without overloading the main electrical grid.

Beyond power distribution, the company manufactures inflight entertainment and connectivity hardware, aircraft data distribution systems, and cabin management solutions. Its connectivity architecture encompasses satellite antenna systems, onboard server nodes, wireless access points, and media distribution units. Through specialized units like Ballard Technology, Astronics supplies interface cards and databus hardware supporting protocols such as ARINC 429, Ethernet, and MIL-STD-1553. These hardware tools allow commercial airlines and military operators to connect cockpit avionics with cabin networks, enabling real-time flight data monitoring, passenger Wi-Fi, and integrated inflight entertainment.

Astronics is also a major global developer of specialized aircraft lighting, safety systems, and interior hardware. Its lighting portfolio spans interior ambient cabin lighting, cockpit illuminated control panels, night-vision-compatible exterior lighting, and emergency escape path lighting. On the safety and surveillance side, the company produces Max-Viz enhanced vision systems, which combine thermal imaging and optical sensors to help pilots navigate through fog, haze, and darkness. Additionally, the company designs passenger service units, seating motion actuation controls, custom environmental control hardware, and specialized structural components such as fuel access doors for commercial and defense airframes.

The company’s second primary operating unit is its Test Systems segment, which builds automated test equipment and diagnostic tools for mission-critical applications. Astronics creates field-level and depot-level automated test platforms, synthetic instruments, and test management software used by defense agencies and aerospace contractors to verify radar, avionics, and electronic warfare systems. This segment also produces tactical radio frequency test equipment and maintenance training simulators, helping military personnel and public transit operators troubleshoot complex circuit cards and communications hardware efficiently.

Complementing its physical manufacturing, Astronics offers turnkey engineering, systems integration, and regulatory certification services. The company provides Supplemental Type Certificate engineering, custom design manufacturing, and regulatory compliance management to guide new electronic systems through Federal Aviation Administration and European Union Aviation Safety Agency approvals. Through these integrated services and global technical support, Astronics maintains long-term aftermarket relationships with commercial airlines, aircraft leasing firms, business jet operators, and defense primes.
