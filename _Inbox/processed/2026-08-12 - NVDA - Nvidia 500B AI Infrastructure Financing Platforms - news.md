# Nvidia Partners with Wall Street to Mobilize >$500B AI Compute Financing

## Thesis Delta
Consensus still frames NVDA demand as hyperscaler FCF-funded; Huang’s MOUs with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman, KKR to raise >$500B third-party capital — with Nvidia option to backstop up to 25% (~$125B) residual-value financing — expands the buyer set but also socializes refinancing/residual-value risk, sharpening the financing-cycle bear case around [[Theses/NVDA - Nvidia]] even as it supports near-term GPU pull.

## Summary
NY Post / Reuters: Nvidia said it partnered with six major financial institutions (Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, KKR) to launch compute financing platforms aimed at raising over $500B in third-party capital for AI infrastructure. CEO Jensen Huang said on X Nvidia has the option to backstop up to $125B, or 25% of potential deals. Intended to broaden access to Nvidia-based infrastructure among frontier labs, enterprises, governments and cloud providers via longer-duration, usage-linked structures. No individual firm commitments or deployment timetable disclosed. Big Tech combined AI outlays set to surpass ~$730B this year per the piece.
