# SpaceX Plans to Power $16.8B Terafab Without the Grid

*Source: Data Center Knowledge / Shane Snider — Aug 11, 2026. Brief score `4`. Portfolio: WFE/test demand optionality AMAT/LRCX/KLAC (Low); AEHR if test content converts; TSM competitive foundry narrative.*

SpaceX plans to power its Terafab semiconductor and advanced-computing campus in Grimes County, Texas, with onsite generation and battery storage rather than grid electricity, per a fully executed tax-abatement agreement with the county.

## Key facts

- Agreement: facilities 'are expected to be powered by electricity generated by on-site power plant(s) and are not expected to use electricity from the grid.' Also references 'the battery storage project.'
- SpaceX/Tesla announced initial **$16.8B** investment Aug 6; **100M sq ft** campus. Texas JETI filings: four phases, potential investment up to **$119B**.
- County June 3 reinvestment-zone order: semiconductor manufacturing plant, AI facility, and power plant.
- Does **not** establish that Terafab will have no physical grid connection — could remain connected while relying on onsite generation routinely.
- Neil Osnato (Persistence Analytics): primary self-supply with essentially no routine grid electricity is 'much more unusual' for a semiconductor/AI complex of this scale.
- Lead developer Riley Trettel told commissioners Aug 5: plan is **natural-gas plants + large battery arrays** (fuel/tech/capacity not in the abatement agreement itself).
- SpaceX IPO framing: key AI constraints are 'physical chip manufacturing, data center infrastructure, and power generation'; target chips to support **1 TW** of computing capacity annually (chip compute capacity, not Terafab electrical load).
- Chips for Tesla vehicles, Optimus robots, and SpaceX computing systems.
- Treat $119B / off-grid claims as abatement-application aspirations until tool orders, named node/partner, gas turbine/BESS procurement, and phase-1 milestones appear.
