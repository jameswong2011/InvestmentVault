# Marvell Q2 FY27 Record $2.74B — Data Center 79%; FY28 Outlook ~$18B

## Thesis Delta
Consensus still underweights how fast Marvell’s **connectivity + custom** mix is compounding after the Google warrant headline. This print shows **data center at 79% of sales**, Q3 guide **~$3.15B (~15% QoQ)**, and a second consecutive raise to **~$18B FY28 revenue (~50% growth)** with management saying **scale-up optics/switching** — not only custom — drive most of the $1.5B raise. Consensus priced a one-shot custom story; this source prices a **broad AI-infra silicon franchise accelerating into H2/FY28**. Supports [[Theses/MRVL - Marvell Technology]].

## Summary
Marvell reported record FQ2 FY27 (q/e 1 Aug 2026) revenue of $2.739B (+37% YoY / +13% QoQ). Data center hit $2.172B (+46% YoY / +18% QoQ), 79% of sales vs 74% a year earlier. Non-GAAP GM 58.9%, non-GAAP op income $1.003B (36.6% OM), non-GAAP EPS $0.94, OCF $605.5M; R&D +43% YoY to $741.1M. Q3 guided to $3.150B ±5% and non-GAAP EPS $1.10 ±$0.05 with non-GAAP GM 57.5–58.5% (custom mix pressure). Management raised FY27 revenue to ~$12B (~45% growth) and FY28 to ~$18B from $16.5B last quarter. Call color: custom accelerates H2 FY27 and more than doubles in FY28; 1.6T DSP and 51.2T switching ramping; NPO/CPO framed as “and, not or”; CXL memory expansion called a “home run” amid DRAM scarcity; ~$1B supplier capacity prepayments still on pace for FY27. Investor Day 6 Oct 2026.
