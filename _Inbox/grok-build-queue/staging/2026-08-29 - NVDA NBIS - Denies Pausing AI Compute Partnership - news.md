# Nvidia Denies Pausing AI Compute Partnership After WSJ Report

Consensus briefly priced a **pullback in Nvidia’s take-or-pay / revenue-share cloud financing** (neocloud support). ## Summary
WSJ reported Nvidia paused some AI Compute Partnership (take-or-pay) transactions less than two months after the July launch, citing partner irritation at Nvidia steering who could rent GPUs and internal antitrust concerns. Nvidia denied a pause to Tom’s Hardware: the July business model “is still in place and continues to evolve due to high demand.” Structure (per Nvidia prior explanations): take-or-pay on a portion of new facility capacity (minimum revenue guarantee for lenders) in exchange for a share of cloud/product upside — not direct circular lending. Commitments typically six years; $36B outstanding as of 26 Jul 2026 per SEC filing. Tom’s notes economic terms (% of capacity, revenue share) remain undisclosed.
