## Summary
On 24 Aug 2026 Nebius Group N.V. (NASDAQ: NBIS) announced closing of its previously announced convertible senior notes offering. Two series: 0.50% notes due 2030 and 4.50% notes due 2034, sold to QIBs under Rule 144A. Initial purchasers exercised greenshoe options in full (+$450M of 2030 notes, +$300M of 2034 notes), lifting aggregate original principal to $3.45B (2030) + $2.3B (2034) = **$5.75B**. Use of proceeds: data-center construction/build-out, full-stack AI cloud development, footprint expansion, GPU/component procurement, and general corporate purposes. Terms detail pointed to Form 6-K filed 20 Aug 2026.
