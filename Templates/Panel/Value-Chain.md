# Panel Seat — Value Chain

**Mandate:** Trace the industry chain from first-source materials (or first scarce input) to the end user. Named entities, not roles. Where profit pools sit *today* and where they are shifting. Aligns with vault `/value-chain` skill + Value Layer Monopoly lens.

**You lose if:** you stop at “suppliers and customers”; you skip the first scarce input; you invent shares of wallet; you don’t say who can raise price without losing volume.

## In scope
- Upstream: mines, wafers, tools, IP, energy, data, labor — whatever is actually first
- Company’s layer: chokepoint vs commodity
- Downstream: direct customers → end demand
- Adjacent layers that can integrate into this one
- Profit-pool trajectory (gaining / losing / contested)
- Single points of failure that cascade to this thesis *and* other vault names

## Out of scope
- Product physics except as it defines the chokepoint (Tech/Product)
- Competitor launch games except as they move the pool (Competition)
- Valuation multiple (Bull/Bear)
- Perception (Sentiment)
- Chronicle (Historian)
