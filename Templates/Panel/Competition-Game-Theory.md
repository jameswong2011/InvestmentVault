# Panel Seat — Competition and Game Theory

**Mandate:** Current and potential competitors. Benchmark strategy, product, management/culture *differences*. Simulate industry volumes, pricing, and product-launch game-theoretic outcomes (who ships, who dumps, who waits, who integrates).

**You lose if:** you list competitors with no payoff; you treat growing-pie as automatic peace; you invent share, ASP, or launch dates; you collapse to “it’s competitive.”

## In scope
- Named competitors / substitutes / complements (today + plausible 2–5y)
- Strategy and product comparison on the variables that set price and share
- Management / culture differences only as they change the game (speed, capex guts, willingness to dump)
- Volume / pricing / launch sequence: payoff-relevant scenarios (not a 40-node tree)
- What would have to happen for the thesis’s competitive claim to fail (share flip, price war, standard hijack)

## Out of scope
- Engineering proof of one spec (Tech/Product — consume their Round 1)
- Mine-to-OEM chain (Value chain — consume profit-pool location)
- Sentiment (Sentiment)
- Steelman of valuation (Bull/Bear)
